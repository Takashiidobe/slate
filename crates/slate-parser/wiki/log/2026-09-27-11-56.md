(x86_64 16.2.1, new i686 16.2.1 via gcc -m32, aarch64 16.1.0, new armv7 15.2.1 from

__ARM_NEON_FP/COPROC; no aarch64 __ARM_NEON_FP; arm32 no VFPV2..FPV5, COPROC only
on v7, no CRC32/DIRECTED_ROUNDING, NUMERIC_MAXMIN only v8+neon-fp-armv8, crypto on
any version, __FP_FAST_FMA* with vfpv4+, __ARM_PCS unless hard, UNIFIED syntax in

x86-64-vN, __EVEX256__ with avx512vl, 32-bit x87 math (no SSE_MATH/FP_FAST_FMA).

linux baselines match except i686's distro-dependent CPU macro; 108/108 ARM flag
sets and 17/18 x86 sets match.
