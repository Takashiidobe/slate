# msvc flavor defines __STDC_VERSION__ under explicit -std

_2026-09-27 10:24:19_

slate-parser-v74.2.1. The cl.exe predefine dumps have no __STDC_VERSION__, so seed_standard_predefines had nothing to rewrite and --flavor=msvc never defined it, which made vcruntime_c11_stdatomic.h hit its '#error C atomics require C11 or later'.

cl.exe (verified with /Zs probes): no /std leaves it undefined; /std:c11 = 201112L, /std:c17 = 201710L, /std:clatest = 202312L (not 202311L).

Since slate's default standard (gnu23) applies when -std is absent, CompilerOptions gains explicit_standard (set from the parsed -std). Preprocessor::configure defines __STDC_VERSION__ for the msvc flavor only when -std was given, mapping C23 to 202312L. Clang-cl (--flavor=clang) is unchanged; its predefines already carry the value.

Fixture: tests/fixtures/sema/x86_64-pc-windows-msvc/stdc_version_msvc.c (no -std / c11 / c17 / c23).
