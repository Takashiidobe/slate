# Sweep the full fixture corpus through IR and Clang

_2026-09-19 21:56:09_

Added tools/corpus_sweep.py to run every non-error FileCheck configuration through forced IR lowering and pinned Clang 22.1.8 syntax checking with the fixture's defines, standard, target, flavor, include paths, and warning flags.\n\nThe sweep covers 2,673 configurations and skips 116 expected-error configurations. Baseline: Slate IR accepts 1,861; Clang accepts 2,564; 751 are Clang-accepted IR failures; 48 are Slate-accepted Clang rejections; 215 accepted configurations differ in warning sets.\n\nThe dominant IR gaps are builtin bindings, GNU asm statements, tag visibility, declaration attributes/asm labels, file-scope declarations, void handling, layout constant expressions, and unsupported initializer/expression forms.\n\nFiled slate-parser-dyd.1 through dyd.17 by root cause and dyd.18 for permanent dual AST/IR FileCheck generation.
