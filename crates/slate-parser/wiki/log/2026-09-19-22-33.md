# make unhandled builtin lowering explicit

_2026-09-19 22:33:59_

Reopened slate-parser-dyd.1 after finding that generated builtin recognition exceeded semantic lowering coverage. Recognized builtins without a handwritten handler now produce a named unsupported Clang builtin diagnostic instead of falling into ordinary call lowering and reporting a missing expression binding. Existing atomic, varargs, and constant-p handlers retain precedence. Added generated FileCheck coverage with __builtin_clz. The bead remains in progress until generated prototypes drive function-like builtin lowering. cargo nextest run --release passes.
