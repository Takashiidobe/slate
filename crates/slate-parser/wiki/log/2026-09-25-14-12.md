# Gate constant arithmetic by compiler flavor

_2026-09-25 14:12:29_

GCC and MSVC disagree with Clang on required constant-expression values and acceptance. GCC folds oversized shifts to zero and rejects negative left shifts with nonzero operands; MSVC folds typed division by zero to zero and masks preprocessor shift counts. Threaded flavor into typed and preprocessor evaluation, validated static initializers, and folded MSVC division by zero only in static initializer IR. Added generated GCC and MSVC FileCheck fixtures. Direct compiler probes and clippy, fmt, release nextest passed. Diagnostic promotion remains tracked in dyd.55.
