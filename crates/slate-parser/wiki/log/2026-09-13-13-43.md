# Split src/parser.rs into src/parser/ modules

_2026-09-13 13:43:48_

Plain refactor of the ~4200-line src/parser.rs into a src/parser/ directory, mirroring src/pp/.

- mod.rs: Parser struct, public entry points (new/with*defines/parse_source/parse_file/parse_declaration), Loc, Cursor, Fragment, error_at/error_at_tokens, node*\* helpers, string-literal and span helpers.
- decl.rs: top-level item, declaration, tag, typedef, linkage-spec and field parsing, plus token/node scanning helpers (matching_brace, matching_paren, split_top_level, signature_node_span, ...).
- attributes.rs: AttrCursor, GNU/C23 attribute parsing, AttributeName, apply_vector_attributes.
- declarator.rs: DeclaratorError, DeclaratorParser, apply_abstract_declarator, is_target_builtin_name.
- stmt.rs: function bodies, K&R parameter declarations, statements, parse_expression.

Items used across files are now pub(super); DeclaratorParser fields are pub(super) for decl.rs.
mod.rs re-exports apply_vector_attributes, matching_brace, DeclaratorParser and is_target_builtin_name as pub(crate), so const_expr.rs paths are unchanged.

Verification: token multiset of the new files equals the original, except for the two new impl Parser wrappers and rustfmt trailing commas.
cargo nextest r --release fails the same 2 fixtures before and after (gcc/c-torture/compile/20031031-2.c, 200031109-1.c).
Wiki concept pages updated to the new paths.
