# Remove free lex() fn; move scan helpers onto Lexer

_2026-09-12 09:22:41_

Per feedback: dropped pub fn lex(file, src) -- callers now construct Lexer::new(file, src).tokenize() themselves rather than going through a wrapper. Made Lexer/new/tokenize pub.

Moved numeric_end, string_prefix_len, char_prefix_len, literal_end, and universal_character_name_end from free functions taking (chars: &[char], i: usize) into &self methods on Lexer reading self.chars directly -- they were always operating on the lexer's own buffer/position, so threading chars+i by hand was pointless indirection. integer_digits and the decode_char_literal/decode_char_escape/hex_char_escape family stay free functions since they operate on independently-passed strings, not the lexer's scanning state.

Updated the two  imports (parser.rs, pp.rs) to  -- their actual call sites (~25 across parser.rs/pp.rs/const_expr.rs) still need to change lex(file, text) call patterns to Lexer::new(file, text).tokenize(), which is the remaining work. Error count dropped from ~98 to 24 now that the errors are 'cannot find fn lex' rather than signature-mismatch pileups.
