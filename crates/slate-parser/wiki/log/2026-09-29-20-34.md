# Token pasting in object-like macros and ## runs

_2026-09-29 20:34:40_

slate-parser-cxg.7.1. Object-like macro replacements were stamped and rescanned without applying ##, so `#define X fo ## o` produced `fo ## o`. They now go through substitute_object_macro (src/pp/expand.rs), which shares a paste helper with function-like substitution.

Runs of `##` (`x ## ## y`, `x##########y`) are collapsed to one `##` when the #define is recorded (src/pp/define.rs). gcc pastes once across a run; clang errors ("pasting formed 'a##'"), so collapsing under clang is permissive, not wrong. Consequence: the C99 6.10.3.3 hash_hash example (`# ## #`) now yields "x ## y"; the cpp__strp2.c expectations had recorded the old wrong "x # ## # y" and were regenerated.

Fixtures: gcc/linux/x86_64/pp-token-paste-runs.c (new), gcc-dg cpp__paste16.c (migrated). cpp/paste2.c still fails on the lexer's missing <: :> digraphs; moved to cxg.7.15.
