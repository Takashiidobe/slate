# Resolve x86-64 R and AArch64 y to pinnable register subsets

_2026-09-26 22:57:09_

x86-64 R (legacy eight) and AArch64 y (v0-v7) resolved to Unresolved before; they now lower to AsmRegisterClass::RegLegacy / VRegLow8, printed reg_legacy / vreg_low8.
Neither has a Rust class, but no usable member is reserved, so emission pins the operand to a free explicit register in the set (in("v7"), in("rsi")). The push/pop trick is only for reserved registers like rbx and isn't needed here.
Register choice is noted on slate-parser-25m.15, where alternatives and registers are picked together.
Fixtures: sema/ir_asm_classes.c, sema/aarch64-unknown-linux-gnu/ir_asm_classes.c.
