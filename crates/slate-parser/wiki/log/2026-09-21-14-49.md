# decide attribute applicability per subject, not per attribute

_2026-09-21 14:49:52_

`sema/attributes.rs` classified an attribute on its own, so the same spelling
was applied, dropped or refused no matter what it was written on. Both halves
of slate-parser-dyd.15 fall out of that: attributes clang ignores reached
lowering, and attributes clang ignores *stopped* it.

Added `Subject` (`Function`, `Object { automatic }`, `Parameter`, `Typedef`,
`Record`) and made `declaration_use` take it. A pair outside clang's subject
list is now `Use::Inapplicable`, which drops the attribute and reports
`-Wignored-attributes`; `Use::Unsupported` is left to mean "applies here, but
lowering cannot express it". `typedef_unsupported` and `parameter_unsupported`
collapsed into `declaration_use` as subject arms. `Lowerer::check_attributes`
is the single warn-or-refuse point, and every call site filters the
inapplicable attributes out before `symbol_attributes` and
`requested_alignment` read them.

Measured the whole matrix against clang 22.1.8, table in
wiki/concepts/diagnostic-severity.md. Fixed too-strict refusals of
`transparent_union`, `ms_struct`, `gcc_struct`, `ifunc` and
`scalar_storage_order` on a plain object, and stopped applying `used`/`retain`
to an automatic local — the IR observable the bead asked for.

Fixtures `sema/ir_attribute_applicability.c` (applied vs dropped) and
`sema/attribute_applicability_warnings.c` (the diagnostics);
`ir_parameter_attribute_diagnostics.c` gained a WARN_IGNORED config and traded
`packed`/`cleanup` for `nocommon`/`mode` in its error configs, since those two
are now ignored on a parameter like clang. Gate green.

Filed dyd.46 (-Wunknown-attributes for unregistered spellings, incl. the
dllimport-on-ELF wrong-IR case), dyd.47 (record/field subjects), dyd.48
(symbol/layout on a parameter is still too strict), dyd.49 (the four remaining
named diagnostics).
