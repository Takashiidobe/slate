# Lower ifunc as a SymbolAttributes string

_2026-09-29 15:14:07_

slate-parser-cxg.49: ifunc("r") was Use::Unimplemented.

- SymbolAttributes.ifunc: Option<String> (first wins on merge), printed [ifunc="r"] after weakref. The resolver names an assembler symbol, so it stays a string; resolver semantics are left to Slate.
- ifunc is Use::Symbol, folded by symbol_attributes and included in function_symbol's filter. On a variable it stays inapplicable (-Wignored-attributes, dropped), as in clang and gcc.
- Not checked, like alias (permissive; both oracles reject): undefined resolver, a body on the same function, ifunc + weak (gcc only).

Fixtures: ifunc_resolver.c; target-type-attributes.c now lowers (was an expected 'not implemented: ifunc' error); gcc-dg attr-ifunc-1/3/4/5.c. Wattribute-alias.c, pr114115.c, pr81854.c also lower now.
