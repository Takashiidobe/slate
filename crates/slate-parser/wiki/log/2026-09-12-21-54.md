# Recognize GCC __typeof keyword alias

_2026-09-12 21:54:56_

The lexer now recognizes GCC's __typeof and __typeof_unqual spellings alongside the standard-facing and double-underscore aliases. This lets expression-based typeof casts such as (__typeof(array[0]))value enter the existing type-name cast parser instead of becoming a grouped call with trailing tokens. Added generated focused coverage and generated torture__vec-cvt-1.c checks. The complete nextest suite passes.
