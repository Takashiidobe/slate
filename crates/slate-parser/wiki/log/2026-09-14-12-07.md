# Allow **real**/**imag** in constant expressions

_2026-09-14 12:07:48_

Follow-up to the **real**/**imag** unary-op fix (slate-parser-ixa.4): GNU semantics treat **real**/**imag** of a non-complex scalar as identity/zero respectively, and both are valid constant expressions. const*expr.rs's integer evaluator (evaluate_expr) now folds UnaryOp::Real to the operand's value and UnaryOp::Imag to 0, instead of always returning NotConstant. Added a matching arm in evaluate_wide for \_BitInt operands to avoid recursing back into evaluate_expr's contains_wide dispatch (which would have infinite-looped, since Real/Imag previously fell through to the generic '*' wide-eval arm that re-invokes evaluate_expr on the same node). sema.rs's is_integer_constant_expression now also accepts Real/Imag operators, matching Plus/Minus/BitNot/Not. Verified 'int arr[__real__ 5];' now parses without a constant-expression diagnostic. Extended tests/fixtures/gnu_real_imag_unary.c with real_array_size/imag_array_size declarations.
