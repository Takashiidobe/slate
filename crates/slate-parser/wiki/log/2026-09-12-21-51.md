# Handle C23 bit-precise integer suffixes

_2026-09-12 21:51:56_

Integer literal classification now strips the C23 wb suffix before or after the unsigned suffix and treats its letters case-insensitively, covering wb, uwb, wbu, uppercase, and mixed-case spellings. Initializer expression failures now propagate as source-located ParseErrors rather than panicking. Added generated valid-suffix and malformed-suffix coverage, and generated torture__bitint-1.c checks. Full nextest advances to the tracked wf8.2.10 vector-conversion expression failure.
