# Keep file-scope pragmas through reachability pruning

_2026-09-29 14:54:09_

filter_translation_unit now roots every DeclKind::Pragma. Previously header pragmas were pruned, so a header's #pragma pack(push,1) struct got natural layout (size 8 vs clang 5) and a header's #pragma STDC FP_CONTRACT OFF left later functions at contract=on. Fixture clang/linux/x86_64/pruning-header-pragmas.c checks both against clang. A system-header sweep (stdio..immintrin, clang and gcc flavors) produced no new diagnostics; the gcc flavor's existing __builtin_ia32_slwpcb failure is cxg.43. Updated ir/pipeline.md reachability roots.
