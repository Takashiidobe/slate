# Parse **real**/**imag** as unary operators

_2026-09-14 12:05:09_

const_expr.rs parsed GNU **real**/**imag**/**real/**imag as Call{callee: Identifier("**real**"), ...}, which name resolution would treat as a call to an unknown function. Added UnaryOp::Real and UnaryOp::Imag and changed parse_unary to build Unary{op, operand} directly instead of a synthetic Call. Display renders them as "**real** "/"**imag** " prefixes (mirroring how sizeof is rendered). The const-expr integer evaluator treats them as NotConstant, same bucket as AddrOf/Deref, since complex real/imag isn't part of integer constant-expression evaluation.

Regenerated every FileCheck fixture that exercised **real**/**imag** (complex.c, complex_lvalue.c, complex_record_fields.c, gnu_integer_complex.c, long_double_complex.c, and the gcc-torture/gcc-dg complex fixtures) since they had the old buggy Call shape baked into their expected output. Added tests/fixtures/gnu_real_imag_unary.c covering all four spellings (**real**, **imag**, **real, **imag).
