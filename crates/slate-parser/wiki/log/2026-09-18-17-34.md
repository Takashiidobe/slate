# Lower va_start, va_end, va_copy

_2026-09-18 17:34:33_

slate-parser-lh7.2.19. The three builtins are recognized by callee name (sema/expression.rs va_builtin) and skipped by name resolution instead of being declared. New ValueKind::VaStart/VaEnd/VaCopy, all void and treated as side effects. va_start's second argument is resolved but not kept; also accepts __builtin_c23_va_start with one argument. Not validated: that the enclosing function is variadic or that the argument is the last named parameter (clang only warns). Fixture ir_va_start_end_copy.c.
