# Record and field attributes go through check_attributes

_2026-09-26 22:18:07_

slate-parser-dyd.47: attributes on struct members and tag definitions were never checked for applicability; only the declarator-less declaration path passed Subject::Record, and only to reject Unsupported ones.

- attributes.rs: new Subject::Field and Subject::Record { union }, filled from clang 22.1.8 -fsyntax-only over ~30 attributes on a field and on a struct definition (table in diagnostic-severity.md). packed applies to both; ms_struct/gcc_struct to records (now Use::Layout, since layout_record implements them); transparent_union to unions (now Use::Ignored: calls needing it already fail in argument conversion, so accepting the definition cannot lower a wrong program, and Unsupported would have rejected union definitions that lower today). section/alias/tls_model on either, and mode on a record, are Invalid, as in clang.
- The warning sink and check_attributes moved from Lowerer to TypeResolver (Lowerer::warn forwards), because record lowering happens in TypeResolver::define_tag. tag_ids is never rolled back, so each tag is diagnosed once, and one sink keeps diagnostic order.
- Leading attributes on a declarator-less tag declaration (__attribute__((x)) struct S {...};) are ignored by clang with a 'place it after struct' warning for every attribute; slate now warns instead of rejecting Unsupported ones there. reject_unsupported removed.
- Specifier attributes are checked once per declarator, matching clang's duplicate warnings for 'cold int a, b;'.

Left as is: const/pure outside functions warn (gcc behavior; clang is silent).
Fixtures: sema/record_attribute_applicability.c (warnings match clang one to one; sizes 32+5+8 = clang's 45), error/field-section-attribute.c.
