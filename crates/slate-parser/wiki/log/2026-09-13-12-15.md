# wf8.6: port batch 4 (files 751-1000) of gcc-torture fixtures, fix 4 bugs

_2026-09-13 12:15:39_

Ported all 250/250 gcc-torture fixtures (lto-tbaa-1.c through pr42614.c,
alphabetically) to tests/fixtures/gcc-torture/. Unlike prior batches, every
failure was fixed directly in-session rather than left triaged-only.

11 fixtures failed purely because the fixture lacked the standard
SLATE-FILECHECK-ISYSTEM directive (missing <stdint.h>/<stdlib.h>/<stdarg.h>/
<limits.h>/<string.h>) -- not a parser bug, just added the usual
"~/Projects/slate/libc-shim/include /usr/lib/clang/22/include" ISYSTEM line.

4 real parser bugs found and fixed, one shared fixture (pr38151.c) hit two of
them at once:

- wf8.6.1: **extension** wasn't handled as a leading expression-prefix inside
  const_expr.rs's parse_unary (only stripped at declaration-dispatch call
  sites in parser.rs). Fixed by consuming it and recursing into parse_cast().
- wf8.6.2: **attribute (single underscore) wasn't accepted as an alias for
  **attribute\_\_ in parser.rs's parse_attribute_groups.
- wf8.6.3: const_expr.rs's try_parse_type_name (casts/sizeof(type)/compound
  literals) never called attribute parsing after the base type, so
  **attribute**((vector_size(...))) inside a cast type-name broke. Exposed
  DeclaratorParser::parse_attributes() and parser.rs's apply_vector_attributes
  as pub(crate) and wired them into try_parse_type_name.
- wf8.6.4: const_expr::starts_type_name (used by the general statement
  dispatch to detect "does this begin a declaration") didn't know about
  target-builtin type names like **builtin_va_list/**m128/**m256, so
  '**builtin_va_list ap;' at block scope misparsed as an expression
  statement. Fixed by having it defer to parser.rs's is_target_builtin_name
  (a superset of the three names it special-cased before).

Ran update_filecheck.py --in-place across all 250 files in parallel
(xargs -P 12) since each invocation shells out to cargo run per fixture.
One regenerated already-ported fixture (tests/fixtures/c99.c) needed
--in-place too: fixing **extension** handling changed its parenthesized
**extension**(...) expression from a bogus Call node to the correct
transparent Binary node.
