# MS mixed-size pointers: __ptr32/__ptr64/__sptr/__uptr

_2026-09-27 10:38:19_

slate-parser-v74.1.6. Parse, type and IR for MS mixed-size pointers under MS extensions; replaces the error for __ptr64 on 32-bit targets.

- Lexer/AST: __ptr32, __sptr, __uptr keywords (gated like __ptr64). All four are Qualifiers flags (is_ptr32/is_ptr64/is_sptr/is_uptr), after * or in specifier position.
- ir::PointerSpace {Default, Ptr32Sptr, Ptr32Uptr, Ptr64} (clang addrspaces 270/271/272) on both CTypeKind::Pointer and ir::Type::Pointer; printed ptr<T, ptr32_sptr>.
- TypeResolver::qualify resolves the flags with clang's width rule (64-bit: ptr32 -> sptr/uptr; 32-bit: ptr64 -> ptr64, uptr -> ptr32_uptr), through typedefs; non-pointer and ptr32+ptr64 / sptr+uptr are errors.
- Storage via TargetInfo::pointer_storage(space) (4/4, 8/8); ABI chunks use the space width.
- Distinct type (_Generic, redeclaration); composite ?: is the default space. Cross-space conversion is implicit and lowers to the new address_space_cast.
- msvc flavor: cl.exe keeps __sptr/__uptr out of type identity (_Generic and redeclaration verified), so CTypes::ptr32_extension_is_qualifier makes the two ptr32 spaces compatible there.

Verified against clang -emit-llvm for x86_64-pc-windows-msvc and i686-pc-windows-msvc, and cl.exe /Zs. windows.h/winsock2.h now parse in both flavors without -D__ptr32=.
