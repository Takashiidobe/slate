# Classify AArch64 records and atomic aggregates by compiler flavor

_2026-09-24 19:50:48_

Closed slate-parser-o9q. Recursive HFA detection now handles one to four float members through arrays, aliases, nested structs, and unions. AArch64 records bypass the flat gate and use the HFA or size rule. Clang and MSVC atomic aggregates use their qualified layout as integer or indirect values; GCC retains ordinary aggregate passing, including HFA. Added generated FileCheck fixtures for WinARM64 MSVC and AArch64 Linux clang/GCC, checked against clang IR and GCC AArch64 assembly. Updated the IR specification.
