# pp: per-flavor __LINE__ in multi-line invocations (ryfo.6)

_2026-09-30 08:44:04_

slate-parser-ryfo.6. __LINE__ in a macro body now depends on the flavor when the invocation spans lines, matching each compiler:

- clang: the line of the outermost invocation's closing paren (the end of its expansion range). PPToken carries end, a Loc: its own for file tokens, and for replacement tokens Stamp.end, set to the rparen's end under clang. So nested invocations and names from other expansions resolve to the file-level rparen.
- gcc: the line of the outermost macro name (unchanged).
- msvc: how far the source has been read (Preprocessor::source_position, updated as physical lines and directives are read), even for __LINE__ written directly in an argument. Checked with tools/cl.exe /EP, with and without /Zc:preprocessor.
- Wiki: preprocessor.md Expansion.

Validation: 7479 tests pass; new fixtures multiline-invocation-line.c under clang/linux/x86_64, gcc/linux/x86_64 and msvc/windows/x86_64, each matching its compiler's -E output. pp_diff: the __LINE__ cluster is gone. cJSON, libexpat, libyaml, quickjs, tinycc and zstd are now clean; libuv 243/1, redis 215/1, mbedtls 198/8. The rest is ryfo.7 (8), ryfo.8 (1) and ryfo.9 (1).
