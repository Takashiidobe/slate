# Add AST grammar

_2026-09-18 19:57:00_

Added wiki/concepts/ast-grammar.md, an EBNF-style grammar of the AST dump printed by `slate-parser parse`, as the AST counterpart of ir-grammar.md. The dump is the Rust Debug form of the types in src/ast.rs and src/const_expr.rs laid out by src/render.rs, so the grammar uses the printed node, variant and field names, marks custom_debug skip_if fields as optional (`f?:`), and covers the dump lines, spans (Spanned/provenance, --show-ids), declarations, specifiers, type specifiers, declarators, tags, statements, expressions, literals, initializers, pragmas, inline asm and attributes. It is evergreen: CLAUDE.md, ast-spec.md and wiki/index.md now point to it and require updating it with any AST type or dump change.
