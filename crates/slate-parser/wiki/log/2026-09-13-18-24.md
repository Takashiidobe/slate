# IR design draft

_2026-09-13 18:24:58_

Added wiki/concepts/ir-design.md: draft design for the targeted IR (AST -> IR -> Rust). Covers prerequisites (typed sema, target data layout, neither exists today), structured tree IR over CFG/SSA, canonical type + typedef sugar, place/value split, pointer op catalog (Decay, PtrOffset, PtrDiff, NullPtr, IsNull, PtrToInt/IntToPtr), explicit Cast nodes aligned with clang CastKind for oracle diffing, overflow semantics on BinOp, bool-typed comparisons, non-desugared compound assignment, and a worked example. Open questions listed at the end.
