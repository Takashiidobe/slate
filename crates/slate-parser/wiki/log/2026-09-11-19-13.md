# Wire -isystem into FileCheck harness; add.c parses real stdio.h

_2026-09-11 19:13:38_

Added a SLATE-FILECHECK-ISYSTEM fixture directive (tests/filecheck.rs, tools/update_filecheck.py) so a fixture can name -isystem search paths that the harness passes to the slate-parser CLI (and to the SLATE_CLANG_ORACLE clang comparison, and update_filecheck.py's regeneration). Reverted tests/fixtures/add.c to its upstream form (real #include <stdio.h>, no hand-written printf prototype) pointed at ~/Projects/slate/libc-shim/include and /usr/lib/clang/22/include, and regenerated its FileCheck output -- it parses cleanly through the real header chain and decl[] no longer shows a synthetic printf declaration, since it now comes from stdio.h itself. Decided (with user) to keep this as a local machine-specific path rather than vendoring/submoduling libc-shim, since this is local-only for now -- no CI reproducibility attempted. Swept a handful more tests/upstream/fixtures under the real header chain (aligned_struct.c, bool.c, atomic_widths.c, c11.c, atoi_atol_prelude_dynamic.c) and found two new real gaps, filed as slate-parser-wf8.1.14 (#include_next unsupported, blocks stddef.h/stdatomic.h/float.h/string.h) and slate-parser-wf8.1.15 (__has_include_next unsupported in #if, blocks clang's builtin stdbool.h). Also confirmed via git-stash bisection that SLATE_CLANG_ORACLE already panics on add.c's 'return c;' (non-literal return) prior to any of this change -- pre-existing, left alone.
