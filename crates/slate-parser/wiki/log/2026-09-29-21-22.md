# Refocus on the real-world corpus: clang x86_64-linux

_2026-09-29 21:22:18_

Goal: every TU of the ~/c-corpus projects that clang -fsyntax-only accepts goes through slate-parser ir (clang flavor, x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu, each project's compile_commands.json flags). Real-world bugs are P1.

- Lowered the dyd, cxg and v74 sweep epics and their 41 open P1/P2 children to P3.
- Baseline from a scratch harness (clang-accepted TUs): sqlite 102/102, cJSON, chibicc, giflib, libyaml, lz4, utf8proc and yyjson all pass. musl 1321/1324, curl 249/359, redis 141/291, lua 13/33, libuv 5/244, zstd 33/41.
- Root causes found:
  - About 342 TUs fail on one glibc signal.h line (__attribute_deprecated_msg__ with its '(' on the next line). The preprocessor expands per logical line with a paren-depth merge and a scoped disabled set instead of per-token hide sets.
  - 'expression typer disagrees with lowering' (zlib, zstd, mbedtls, expat). Lowering re-derives types; for enum ?: it keeps enum e, while clang and the typer give unsigned int.
  - __m128i is parsed as a TargetBuiltin type, which breaks emmintrin.h use (v74.1.13, raised to P1).
  - __has_declspec_attribute breaks 105 curl TUs (dyd.67, raised to P1).
- Filed epics: slate-parser-ryfo (pull-based pp with Prosser hide sets and a clang -E differential oracle), slate-parser-ygrj (lowering consumes checker facts and derives no types; single initializer walk), slate-parser-6x05 (corpus: sweep tool, libpng/musl/tinycc bugs, corpus growth list). 6x05 is blocked on ryfo and ygrj.
- Gotcha: bd create --deps blocks:X means the new issue blocks X. Use bd dep add <blocked> <blocker> for step chains.
- README gained a Trophy case section listing the fully passing projects.
