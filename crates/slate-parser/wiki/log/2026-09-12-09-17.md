# Clean up Lexer struct: owned tokens, peek/try_consume, Comment token

_2026-09-12 09:17:13_

Reworked the Lexer struct per feedback that the first pass wasn't idiomatic:
- tokens: Vec<Span<Token>> now lives on the struct; emit(token) just pushes to self.tokens instead of taking a &mut Vec param, and tokenize() returns self.tokens at the end.
- Added peek()/peek_at(offset)/peek_str(s)/try_consume(s) helpers. Comments, '...', and all multi-char operators now go through try_consume/try_consume_op instead of hand-rolled chars[i+1]/chars[i+2] lookahead + matches! tuples.
- MULTI_CHAR_OPS and SINGLE_CHAR_OPS are now static tables (longest-match-first) instead of two giant match expressions duplicated across a 3-char/2-char/1-char cascade.
- Dropped the unused 'a lifetime/_src field once chars/byte_offsets fully owned their data.
- Added Token::Comment(String) (raw text incl. delimiters) -- comments are now emitted as tokens instead of silently skipped, since Slate wants to use them for documentation later.

lexer.rs compiles clean standalone. Still 86 errors in parser.rs + 12 in pp.rs from downstream consumers expecting the old Vec<Token> lex(src) signature (now lex(file, src) -> Vec<Span<Token>>, and now emitting Comment tokens they don't filter yet).
