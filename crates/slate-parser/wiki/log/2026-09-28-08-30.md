# thumbv7a-pc-windows-msvc target

_2026-09-28 08:30:56_

slate-parser-78x3.7. New TargetSpec thumbv7a-pc-windows-msvc, named after the slate-sysroots directory and Rust's triple (clang treats thumbv7 and thumbv7a identically here). TargetInfo::thumbv7a_windows_msvc is windows_msvc(Arm32) with 4-byte pointers and stack alignment 8 (clang datalayout S64); char stays signed, unlike Arm32 Linux. va_list is char *, convention aapcs32 (hard float): clang's signatures for every shape in the armv7 gnueabihf abi_target.c plus HFAs, large records, complex, vectors and variadics are identical between the two triples.
Predefines: clang-22.1.8_thumbv7a_windows_msvc.h is raw clang -dM minus the ISA-generated macros; msvc flavor uses the previously unreferenced msvc-19.44.35228_arm_windows.h (19.44 is the last toolset with Arm32; msvc-wine has no arm cl, so it is not re-verified).
ISA: clang's Windows driver makes Arm32 Thumb-2 only (-marm ignored), rejects -mfloat-abi=soft/softfp, and defaults the FPU to neon-fp16 (__ARM_FP 0xe, __ARM_NEON_FP 0x6, no FMA) on armv7-a but plain neon on armv8-a. ArmIsa::resolve keys this off TargetEnvironment::Msvc; ArmFpu::NeonFp16 is new and parseable as -mfpu=neon-fp16 (matches clang and arm-linux-gnueabihf-gcc on Linux too). Verified with #if probes against clang -dM for 13 option combinations.
Fixtures under {clang,msvc}/windows/thumbv7a-pc-windows-msvc and error/clang/... (softfp). windows.h, threads.h, tgmath.h, intrin.h and arm_neon.h parse in both flavors; windows.h sizes match clang.
Not done: MS bit-field layout is not the default on any *-windows-msvc triple (clang: struct{char a; int:0; char b;} is 2, {char a:3; int b:5;} is 8; slate 5 and 4) - noted on slate-parser-7y5, kept out of layout.c. Records containing arrays stay native_c on every aapcs32 triple (documented abstention).
