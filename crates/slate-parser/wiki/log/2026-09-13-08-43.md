# Remove ConstExpr::IntegerLiteral(String) in favor of WideInteger

_2026-09-13 08:43:25_

Extended the WideInteger path from wxs.5.1 to cover every integer literal whose
magnitude exceeds u64::MAX, not just wb/uwb-suffixed _BitInt literals, and deleted
ConstExpr::IntegerLiteral(String) entirely (parse_wide_bit_int_literal renamed to
parse_wide_integer_literal, no longer gated on a wb suffix -- returns WideInt
unconditionally instead of Option<WideInt>).

Rationale: IntegerLiteral(String) was a stringly-typed dead end -- anything
consuming it had to re-parse the spelling (including stripping the suffix) to get
a usable value. WideInt gives downstream consumers (sema, and eventually Slate's
bytecode/Rust lowering) a structured value+width+signed triple they can convert to
their own wide-int representation directly.

Verified no fixture in the corpus actually has a non-wb-suffixed literal exceeding
u64::MAX (checked via grep across tests/fixtures); all matches were either
wb-suffixed BitInt literals already covered, or false positives inside string/float
literals. cargo nextest run passes unchanged with no fixture regeneration needed.
