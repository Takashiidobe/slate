# Recognize __int128_t and __uint128_t as type names in expression context

_2026-09-28 12:27:41_

slate-parser-cxg.3. __int128_t/__uint128_t were only special-cased inside DeclaratorParser::parse_other_type, so file-scope declarations worked (always parsed as declarations) but anything that first asks const_expr::starts_type_name failed: block-scope declarations (stmt dispatch), casts, and sizeof(__int128_t), which surfaced as 'unexpected tokens after expression', 'expected ;' and 'unresolved ordinary name __int128_t' (the latter was not c89-specific; it was sizeof parsing the name as an expression).

Fix: one builtin_integer_typedef(name) -> Option<IntegerType> in src/parser/declarator.rs, used by both parse_other_type and starts_type_name, so the two can't drift again.

_Float32 at block scope (also listed in the bead) was already fixed by cxg.9.

Migrated 10 gcc.dg files to tests/fixtures/suites/gcc-dg/gcc/linux/x86_64/. Leftovers belong to other clusters: builtin-bswap-11.c (__builtin_bswap128) and builtin-tgmath-2.c (__builtin_tgmath) -> cxg.12; torture/float*-basic.c are K&R definitions under a C23 default -> cxg.16.
