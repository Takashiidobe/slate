# Macro expansion profiling and persistent origins

_2026-09-22 15:04:31_

Profiled the 59-deep object-macro benchmark inside #if. Borrowing MacroEntry instead of cloning it reduced 10,000 uses from 1.241s to 1.175s. Heaptrack recorded 1,281,561 String::clone allocation calls; CPU sampling attributed 1.61% to Token::clone. Replaced recursive MacroOrigin::append, which copied the whole origin chain at each nested expansion, with shared inner links while keeping name/definition on the outermost macro. The 50,000-use benchmark in tools/bench_macro_expansion.py ran in 6.241s on the baseline and 1.301s after the change (best of three release runs for each). Validation: cargo clippy --allow-dirty --fix --all-targets -- -D warnings; cargo fmt; cargo nextest run --release.
