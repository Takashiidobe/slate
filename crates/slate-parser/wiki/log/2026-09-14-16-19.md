# Readable floating IR constants

_2026-09-14 16:19:24_

Represent IR floating types explicitly as f16, f32, f64, f80, and f128.
Print f32/f64 numerically and f16/f80/f128 with rustc_apfloat; retain exact bits internally and in NaN dumps.
Extend generated sema fixtures for decimal precision, subnormals, f16, and f128 addition.
Document overflow policies and leave target-dependent long double resolution for the next sema change.
