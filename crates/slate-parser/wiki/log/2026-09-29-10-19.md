# Architecture principles page and concise configuration-threading

_2026-09-29 10:19:06_

Added wiki/concepts/architecture.md from the user's answers to the doc-gap questions:
- The checker validates declarations and expressions; lowering trusts it and reports Internal errors. Duplicated rules should move into the checker or a shared rule function.
- IR boundary: emit only what the Rust side can use. Semantics-changing labels must be in the IR. Lean on Rust extern where it can express the C (MSVC fastcall). Otherwise the IR carries the resolved meaning (e.g. gcc vs clang _Atomic).
- Unimplemented = LLVM-style NYI, not a rejection.
- Strictness: too strict is a bug; too permissive is at most P4; error fixtures only assert rejection.
- Priorities: clang flavor first; zstd as the real-world yardstick, then gcc/MSVC; rerun sweeps after a good share of an epic closes.
- Oracles: clang 22.1.8, gcc 16.2, MSVC 19.51.36257 for x86/x64/ARM64 (verified via tools/cl.exe with MSVC_ARCH). Arm32 predefines are frozen at 19.44.35228 (no local arm cl). Predefines are hand-captured -dM with varying macros computed in Preprocessor::configure. Sysroots/compiler headers come from ../slate-sysroots.
