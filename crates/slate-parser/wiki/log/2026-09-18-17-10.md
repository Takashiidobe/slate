# Lower \_Decimal32/64/128 to IR

_2026-09-18 17:10:13_

slate-parser-ixa.38: added FloatType::D32/D64/D128 (printed d32/d64/d128, size/align 4/8/16 on all targets, ungated) and Number::DecimalFloat(String) so DF/DD/DL literals keep their digit spelling. Decimal arithmetic is never folded (fold.rs returns None for decimal formats). Integer->decimal exactness uses FloatType::exact_integer_bits (23/53/112, replacing two duplicated precision matches). Mixed decimal/binary operands in arithmetic or comparison are rejected via reject_mixed_decimal (C23 6.3.1.8); integers convert to the decimal format. New ConversionKind::FloatConvert (float_convert) for explicit casts across families, since FloatType's derived Ord would otherwise call f128->d32 a widen (FloatType::widens_from guards this). Zero constants for conditions use Number::float_zero. Fixtures: sema/ir_decimal_float.c, error/decimal_binary_mix.c; removed the obsolete DECIMAL case from sema/unsupported.c. ir-spec.md updated. Not done: decimal ABI classification (no abi= shown for decimal params) and decimal constant folding by design.
