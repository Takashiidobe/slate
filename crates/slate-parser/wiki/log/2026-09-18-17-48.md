# Token pasting lexes with active standard features

_2026-09-18 17:48:21_

Fixed slate-parser-8c5. pp::lex/lex_spanned built a Lexer with default (c23) StandardFeatures, so a '##' paste forming a keyword (constexpr, restrict, inline, typeof) was classified against c23 regardless of -std. They now take StandardFeatures; substitute_function_macro receives self.features from the expander. Fixture paste_keyword_uses_active_standard.c pastes const##expr under -std=c17 and expects an identifier.
