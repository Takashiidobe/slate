# Resolve enumerator references without IR node identity

_2026-09-19 15:42:41_

Follow-up to slate-parser-xf9. Enumerator references in expression.rs found their value by scanning every enum definition for an IR Enumerator whose span id equalled the AST enumerator's NodeId, so the IR enumerator had to keep its AST id, the one exception to IR nodes owning their ids.
Now TypeResolver.enumerators maps the AST enumerator NodeId to its constant Value, filled where the enum is resolved (next to the Ordinary::Constant declaration), and the reference path reads it directly. IR enumerators take a fresh id via Span::derive like every other IR node. The lookup is also O(1) instead of a scan over all type definitions.
No fixture output changed. ir-spec updated to drop the exception.
