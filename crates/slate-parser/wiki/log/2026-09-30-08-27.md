# pp: Prosser hide-set expander over a pushback stream (ryfo.3)

_2026-09-30 08:27:10_

slate-parser-ryfo.3. Macro expansion now follows Prosser's algorithm over the token stream instead of expanding one merged line with a scoped disabled set.

- src/pp/hide_set.rs: interned HideSet ids; with/union/intersection memoized.
- src/pp/expand.rs: PPToken (token + hide set) and Stream (pushback stack in front of an optional FileInput). next_raw pulls further physical lines only while reading an invocation, running directives met there (#ifdef inside arguments). expand_token: painted names are final; object-like gets hs(name)+{name}; function-like gets (hs(name) & hs(rparen))+{name}, and only when the next token is ( (peek crosses lines, stops at a # line). Unterminated or wrong-arity calls push their tokens back. expand_isolated serves argument prescan and #if/#include/#embed/#line operands. Removed tail, rescan, wants_more, Invocation::from_tail, the disabled set.
- src/pp/syntax.rs: the temporary paren-depth merge is gone; next_line returns physical lines; peek_token added.
- src/pp/mod.rs: process groups a text line with the lines its invocations pulled; comments of pulled lines and nodes from directives inside arguments follow the Code node. MacroEntry.definition is Rc<MacroDef>.
- Wiki: preprocessor.md Pipeline/Expansion rewritten; pp-logical-line-merging.md deleted (AGENTS.md, index).

Validation: 7464 tests pass. The five epic repros and glibc signal.h with -D_GNU_SOURCE pass under ir. pp_diff: libuv 5->178 same, lua 13->33 (clean), musl and curl clean, redis 136->147; the rest is ryfo.6 (__LINE__, about 180 TUs), ryfo.7, ryfo.8 and new ryfo.9 (pp-number paste). sqlite3.c/deflate.c/cJSON.c parse --show-comments unchanged; bench_macro_expansion 0.88s -> 0.85s; sqlite3.c ir 2.13s -> 2.12s.

Fixtures: cpp__strp1 and cpp__macro1 (gcc, clang) regenerated; they had captured wrong expansions, and the new output matches clang -E. Migrated cpp__19921210-1 (cxg.7.2) and cpp__macro4 (cxg.7.5, fixed as a side effect).
