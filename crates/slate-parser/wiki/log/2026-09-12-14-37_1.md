# Support #pragma push_macro / pop_macro

_2026-09-12 14:37:41_

record_pragma dispatches push_macro("X") and pop_macro("X") (define.rs).

push_macro records a PushedMacro { active, order: macro_order } on a
per-name stack. pop_macro finds the most recent push made under the same
active Condition and removes the macro-table branches for X recorded since
that push whose condition implies the active one (equal, active statically
true, or an And containing it). This restores whatever was visible at the
push, including 'undefined' when X had no definition. Pops with no matching
push, or with a malformed argument, are ignored as clang does (it warns).

Because the restore removes only entries under the pushing condition, a
push/pop inside '#ifdef WIDE' leaves definitions in other configurations
untouched.

Fixture: pp-push-pop-macro.c (DEFAULT, WIDE); array sizes match
clang -E output in both configurations.
