# Sized __sync_*_N spellings lower like the unsuffixed builtins

_2026-09-20 14:37:33_

gcc's legacy size-suffixed `__sync_` builtins (`_1`, `_2`, `_4`, `_8`, `_16`)
now parse. `sync_builtin` strips the suffix before parsing the operation and
then requires that the operation actually has a sized spelling.

The suffix has no semantic effect: verified that clang takes the width from
the pointee and ignores it entirely -- `__sync_fetch_and_add_8` on a `char`
object emits `atomicrmw add ... i8`, and `__sync_fetch_and_add_1` on an `int`
emits `i32`. So the existing lowering is already correct and nothing but the
name parsing changed.

Not every operation has one. `min`/`max`/`umin`/`umax` and `synchronize` have
no sized spellings, and clang rejects `__sync_fetch_and_min_4` and
`__sync_synchronize_4`; `SyncBuiltin::sized` is a positive list, exhaustive
over the enum. Verified agreement with clang 22.1.8 across all six fetch
operations in both fetch forms, both compare-and-swaps, `lock_test_and_set`,
`lock_release` and `swap`, plus the rejected spellings and the invalid widths
`_3` and `_32`.

Covered by a new `sized` function in `tests/fixtures/sema/ir_atomic_sync.c`,
including the `_8`-on-`char` case that shows the suffix being ignored.
