# closed y0qs.2, skipping touched-item threading

_2026-08-27 09:04:08_

Closed slate-y0qs.2 without implementing. Scoped to revive TouchedItems-style diffing in
SalsaFacts::set_program, gated on y0qs.1 profiling. That profiling (libexpat, see
2026-08-27-09-01 log) showed set_program's diff is only 9.5% of fixpoint round time, capping
the win. More importantly, wiki/concepts/salsa-migration.md's "Where it landed" section
records that this exact mechanism (TouchedItems, threaded through EditSet/ItemPlan::apply)
was already built once and deleted as dead weight (slate-kby1.6.11) after never being wired
into actual invalidation -- the always-rescan-and-backdate replacement was deliberately
sanity-checked against a realistic fixture (slate-kby1.6.10) and accepted. The libexpat
numbers are a second, independent confirmation of that same tradeoff at real-codebase scale.
Redoing it now would re-litigate an already-correct decision for a sub-10% ceiling. Next:
y0qs.3 (worklist/dispatch-table engine), where the other ~90% of round time actually is.
