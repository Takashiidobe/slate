# Parallel per-TU frontend fan-out (slate-9an4.1/.2)

_2026-08-27 21:10:49_

New epic slate-9an4 (separate from slate-y0qs): measured libexpat's ~10.5s
wall time is dominated by clang/cir-opt subprocess spawns (3/TU, 21 TUs,
sequential, no threading anywhere in the codebase), not the rewrite/fixup
pipeline -- confirmed via SLATE_RAW_LOWER=1 (skips all fixups) landing at
~10.5s, same as the full run.

Split translate*project_lib_crate_with_compile_commands's per-TU loop
(main.rs) into phase 1 (load_library_variant: read/preprocess/emit-cir/
cir-opt/ast-dump, pure, no shared state) and phase 2 (sequential merge into
variant_facts/shared_records/record_shape_conflicts -- order-sensitive
conflict detection, must stay ordered). .1 landed as a no-op refactor
first (verified libexpat wall-clock and generated output unchanged), then
.2 added load_library_variants_parallel: a bounded rayon thread pool
(par_iter over the BTreeMap-ordered entries, ordering preserved by
`.collect::<Vec<Result<*,\_>>>().into_iter().collect()`, first-error-wins
semantics, no cancellation of in-flight siblings on error).

Default worker count: half of available_parallelism(), not all of it --
each worker spawns its own clang process, and ClangIR has a known upstream
memory blowup on large macro-heavy TUs (slate-wcf7.2, not our bug to fix)
that full-core-count fan-out would multiply exposure to. SLATE_JOBS
overrides.

Results on libexpat (21 TUs, 12-core machine): SLATE_JOBS=1 ~12.1s
(matches old sequential baseline), =3 ~7.3s, =6 (default) ~5.6s, =12
~5.6s -- plateaus past 6, doesn't scale to full core count (subprocess
spawn + disk IO contention, not naive Amdahl). Generated output byte-
identical to the pre-parallel run. rewrites 110/110, lowering 10/10,
fmt/clippy clean.
