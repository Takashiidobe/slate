# Exclude catchable divide-trap torture case

_2026-08-30 20:41:00_

Moved gcc-torture 20101011-1 from unsupported to ignored.

The case requires target-specific integer division by zero to raise catchable SIGFPE. Slate emits Rust division, whose zero check panics instead, and intentionally does not emulate hardware trap and signal delivery semantics.

Removed the case from the unsupported triage mapping. The lowering-profile gcc_torture_suite passed (2/2).
