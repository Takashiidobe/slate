# Drop redundant -S from clang -emit-cir

_2026-08-29 19:02:17_

The CIR emit path in src/frontend/toolchain.rs (emit_generic_with_args_and_cir_opt_flags) passed both -emit-cir and -S. -emit-cir is a complete action flag; -S is redundant and projects built with -Werror (cJSON) fail: 'argument unused during compilation: -S' [-Wunused-command-line-argument]. Removed -S. Verified emit-cir on add.c gives identical CIR (101 lines), translate add.c works, lowering profile passes 10/10.
