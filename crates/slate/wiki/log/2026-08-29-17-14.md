# Selective lowering FileCheck annotations

_2026-08-29 17:14:31_

Extended tools/update_filecheck.py with paired @lowering and @lowering-not annotations. @lowering emits normalized LOWERING-DAG patterns from raw-lowered sentinel regions. @lowering-not is symmetric with @rewrite-not: it emits normalized rewritten patterns absent from the raw-lowered region as LOWERING-NOT. Mixed lowering/rewrite annotations now generate independent profile blocks; profiles without annotations are omitted. Instrumented lowered and rewritten output is cached per command/environment so --profile both translates each target only once per side. Extended filecheck_annotation_regions.c with positive and negative lowering regions. Validation: py_compile, idempotent regeneration, cargo fmt, clippy with warnings denied, focused lowering and rewrite fixture runs, full lowering (10 passed), and full rewrites (110 passed).
