# delete redundant EarlyInlineTemps pass

_2026-08-31 19:35:49_

## What
Deleted `EarlyInlineTemps` (priority 3) from the rewrite engine. It was fully
subsumed by `LateInlineTemps` (priority 41), which has strictly more capability.

## Why / evidence
While extending inline-temps to inline string/format-arg temps into calls, added
`unwrap_single_stmt` so the argument-use gate descends through single-statement,
tail-less `unsafe {}`/block wrappers (the printf/puts consumer shape). Wired it
into both Early and Late.

Then compared generated Rust for all 428 tests/fixtures with Early on vs off:
byte-identical. add.c output unchanged; atomics + add differential pass. So Early
never produced output Late didn't — pure dead weight, removed.

## Removed
- `EarlyInlineTemps` struct+impl and its sole helper `early_effectful_consumer`
  (inline_temps.rs)
- registry() entry (rules/mod.rs)
- `Pass::EarlyInlineTemps` + name mappings (backend/trace.rs)

## Known gap
printf format-string temp still not inlined: an effectful `add(...)` arg temp
sits between the `let` and the call; EffectfulTempForward inlines the add temp
later but the format-string `let` is never re-queued. Separate worklist re-touch
issue, not format-string handling.
