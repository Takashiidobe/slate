# Classify libexpat generated-crate compile failures

_2026-08-29 09:02:30_

Re-ran the 21-TU libexpat benchmark. Translation remains healthy at 4.957s, 5.143s, and 5.280s (5.127s average), but cargo build --release on the generated crate reports 36 diagnostics.

Classified them into three roots under parent slate-ft0v:

1. slate-ft0v.3: same-named function-local record identities collide. 21 aggregate type mismatches plus 9 missing-field errors. This likely regresses or incompletely preserves the a0b73e16 local_record_extras fix.
2. slate-ft0v.2: pointer lattice misses writes through generated alloca-frame fields and lifts five flattened out-parameters to shared references.
3. slate-ft0v.1: a by-value named record return receives distinct nominal ABI-wrapper types in caller and callee translation units.

All tickets require focused C fixtures and make successful generated-crate compilation part of acceptance. No implementation changes made.
