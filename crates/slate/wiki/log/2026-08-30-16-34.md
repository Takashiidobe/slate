# Use unaligned access for packed bitfield storage

_2026-08-30 16:34:59_

Packed bitfield wrapper methods previously borrowed storage fields directly. When a wrapper backed by u16 or wider storage sits in a repr(C, packed) record, those method calls create an invalid unaligned reference and rustc rejects them with E0793.

Lowering now compares the wrapper backing alignment with the record packing. Unaligned getters copy the wrapper through ptr::read_unaligned before calling its accessor. Setters perform an unaligned read-modify-write through addr_of!/addr_of_mut! and ptr::write_unaligned. Alignment-1 byte-array wrappers retain direct access.

Promoted packed_mixed_bitfields.c with signed fields, a zero-width boundary, u16 and byte-array storage units, runtime differential coverage, and lowering/rewrite FileCheck assertions.
