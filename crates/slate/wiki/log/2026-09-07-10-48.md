# AArch64 FP constraint boundary

_2026-09-07 10:48:23_

Documented that Clang accepts AArch64 w/x/y constraints, while Slate's fixed-width vector lowering currently produces Rust arrays that Rust asm! rejects for SIMD operands. Scalar w is covered; x remains a Slate vector-lowering gap and y requires scalable SVE support.
