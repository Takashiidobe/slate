# unify libc function identity catalog

_2026-09-01 17:15:36_

Replace the duplicated Known enum, symbol, header, and trusted-header classifier tables with one macro catalog. Generate Known plus C-symbol, header, and reverse lookups from it. Route conventional __builtin_<symbol> names through the same catalog, retaining only bit_cast, bcopy, and bzero as exceptional aliases. Remove the unused known_call helper.
