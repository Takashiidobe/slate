# Add ARM32 and i686 syscall tables

_2026-09-07 19:37:35_

Added libc-shim syscall tables sourced from standalone_musl for ARM32 and i386, including SYS_* aliases and architecture dispatch. Removed the obsolete ARM32 gnu_libc_platform skip because SYS_gettid is now provided by the shim. Header probes for both targets, cargo fmt check, and cargo clippy pass. The i686 isolated fixture now reaches the known CIR cir.alloca verifier failure instead of missing SYS_gettid.
