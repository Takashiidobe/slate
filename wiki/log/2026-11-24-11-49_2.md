# slate-e36k.18 intrinsic adaptation: wide _BitInt min and max hit lowering barriers

_2026-11-24 11:49:14_

- bead: slate-e36k.18
- kind: bug
- area: crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported

Outcome: The unsupported ratchet covers signed and unsigned elementwise comparisons on _BitInt(65).

The signed max compares -1 with 2^63. The unsigned min compares
2^64 with 1. Using the full widths matters for both comparisons,
and Lean verifies the expected selections. Clang runs the fixture
successfully on all four Linux targets.

Slate rejects signature adaptation for both LLVM intrinsics because
the wide _BitInt operands use the bitint representation. The
unsupported triage reports the signed max barrier first.

Fixture: crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported/bitint_elementwise_minmax.c
Limits: Wide elementwise min/max support remains open in slate-e36k.18.
