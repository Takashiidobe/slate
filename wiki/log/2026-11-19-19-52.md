Outcome: CTypeKind is #[slate::sensitive] and cargo dylint reports zero hits; every catch-all over it is either gone or an expect whose reason states the assumption.

Triage of 41 hits, by resolution:

- New audited views on CTypeKind (listed once, sema/ctype/mod.rs): `sugared()`
  replaces the unused `is_sugar` and drives `desugar` / `unqualified`;
  `integer() -> Option<IntegerKind>` and `complex_domain() -> Option<ComplexDomain>`
  (both small, also sensitive). Moved onto them: arith `integer`, convert
  `integer_shape`, `is_integer`, `is_floating`, `is_complex_domain`, compat
  `promotes_to_itself`, rotate/stdc operand checks (`IntegerKind::is_explicitly_unsigned`),
  bit-field `-funsigned-bitfields`, `_Complex` component and vector element
  validation, operand complex domains.
- Listed exhaustively (they define a per-kind decision): `canonical_form`,
  `type_class`, mangler `ty_uncached` and `is_builtin`.
- Reused an existing helper: mangle `adjust_parameter` now calls `CTypes::adjust_parameter`.
- Rejecting arm made explicit: initializer `subobject_type` names `Array` for index targets.
- Expects with reasons: structural recursions (`typedef_alignment`,
  `with_convention`, `any_variable_extent`, compat pair matches, `composite`,
  `typedef_chain`, `constexpr_literal_type`), pass-throughs
  (`arithmetic_component`, render `name()` fallback, SVE pair fall-through),
  and the slate lowerer's `lower_c_type` / `known_c_name` / libc catalog.

Bugs found: `BlockPointer` was missing beside `Pointer` in `canonical_form`
(a block pointer to a typedef never canonicalized), `composite` (no composite
pointee), and `typedef_chain` (aka chain stopped at `^`). All three fixed.

Policy in wiki/concepts/linting.md: list the variants; if the list is unseemly
or recurs, change the representation (audited view returning a small enum, or an
existing helper); otherwise expect, with the reason as documentation.
type-family-touchpoints.md points at the views and the expects.

Limits: no fixture isolates the BlockPointer fixes (parser and slate profiles
pass). Other enums (ir::Type, ValueKind, VaListKind, CompilerFlavor) not yet marked.
