# Parse an asm label after attributes on a declarator

_2026-10-30 20:53:25_

slate-e36k.4: bionic bits/ioctl.h declares 'int ioctl(...) __overloadable __enable_if(1, "") __RENAME(ioctl);' and the parser expected ';' at the asm label. parse_declarator in parser/decl.rs now takes an asm label after the trailing attributes, then more attributes. Clang allows this order only on function declarators and gcc rejects it; we also accept it on non-function declarators and under gcc, since too permissive is allowed. Fields stay rejected.
