# GCC assertions: #assert, #unassert, #if #pred(answer)

_2026-10-09 19:17:04_

slate-parser-cxg.7.9. GCC-flavor only, following libcpp directives.cc. clang rejects both forms, and so do the clang and msvc flavors.

- pp/syntax.rs: DirectiveName::Assert / Unassert. run_directive handles them under gcc; otherwise they stay unsupported directives.
- Preprocessor.assertions maps a predicate to its answers. An answer is the tokens up to the first ), each kept with whether it has leading space; the first token's space is dropped (parse_answer). Duplicates are ignored; #unassert pred(ans) removes one answer and #unassert pred removes all.
- #if/#elif: expand_assertions runs before expand_condition and replaces #pred / #pred(ans) with 0 or 1, so neither the predicate nor the answer is macro-expanded, as with libcpp's prevent_expansion.
- Errors match gcc's text and location: missing ( after predicate, empty answer, missing ), predicate must be an identifier (PPErrorKind::Assertion).
- configure seeds gcc's Linux assertions by #assert: system(linux|unix|posix), plus cpu/machine = x86_64, i386 or arm (none on aarch64), from config/linux.h, i386-c.cc and arm-c.cc. Checked against gcc and gcc -m32.
