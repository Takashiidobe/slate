Outcome: the IR's one general traversal is now `ir::Visitor` / `ir::walk_body` / `ir::walk_value` in crates/slate-parser/src/ir/visit.rs, no longer named after macro stripping.

Before: the walker lived in ir/macro_strip.rs as `MacroStructureVisitor`,
`walk_macro_structure`, `walk_value_structure`. Most users had nothing to do
with macros (sema/asm_constants.rs x5, sema/folded_branches.rs, sema/asm.rs,
slate lowerer/switch.rs), so it looked like there was no IR visitor.

After: trait and walker moved to ir/visit.rs unchanged apart from the names.
ir/macro_strip.rs keeps only `KeepOnly` and the strip entry points
(`Module::strip_macro_structure`, `strip_all_macro_structure[_in_value]`).
Call sites renamed mechanically. No behavior change. The build is clean, and
no suites were rerun.

Docs: wiki/concepts/ir/pipeline.md "Walking the IR" (hook order, exhaustive
matches, use this instead of a new traversal).
