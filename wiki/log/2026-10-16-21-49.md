# Drop the stdout flush after println!

_2026-10-16 21:49:27_

printf_format: extend_flushed now skips a std::io::Write::flush when the previous statement is a println!. Rust's stdout is a LineWriter even when piped, so println! (always ending in a newline) leaves the buffer empty and the flush is a no-op. This covers both the flush after a converted println! and the one before an unconverted libc printf. print! keeps its flush: with #![no_main] Rust's runtime never flushes stdout at exit, and unconverted printf ordering needs the buffer empty. Regenerated the FileCheck blocks of 37 fixtures; every diff only removes a flush line. Full slate profile otherwise green.
