- tools/generate_gcc_builtins.py expands gcc's builtins.def, builtin-types.def and builtin-attrs.def with gcc -E under x86-64 linux defaults and writes src/sema/gcc_builtins.rs (GCC_BUILTINS, records are BUILT_IN_* names).
