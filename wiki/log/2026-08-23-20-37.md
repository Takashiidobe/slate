_2026-08-23 20:37:56_

Added libc-shim/bionic-stdio-locale-headers.txt manifest and bionic_stdio_locale_header_manifest_compiles_for_64_bit_targets test in tests/header_compilation.rs, mirroring the bionic-basic-headers.txt pattern from slate-sfzn.7.2. Full ABI probes (FILE identity, fpos_t, mbstate_t layout, lconv fields) against the NDK oracle remain open for a follow-up.
