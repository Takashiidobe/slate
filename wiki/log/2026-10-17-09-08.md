# Make gnu_library_builtins portable to ILP32

_2026-10-17 09:08:54_

slate-c0b3: gnu_library_builtins failed to build on i686 with 'attempt to shift left by 40' (deny arithmetic_overflow). The cause was the fixture, not the open-coded **builtin_ffs lowering: `**builtin_ffsl(1L << 40)` shifts a 32-bit long by 40 on i686, which is undefined behavior in C (clang -m32 warns -Wshift-count-overflow), and rustc rejects the same constant shift at compile time.

The fixture now uses `__builtin_ffsl(1L << 20)` and moves the 40-bit case to `__builtin_ffsll(1LL << 40)`, keeping `__builtin_ffsll(0)`. Regenerated the x86_64 FileCheck block and removed gnu_library_builtins from fixtures.unsupported/i686-unknown-linux-gnu.txt. Passes on x86_64, i686 and aarch64; slate-i686 profile green.
