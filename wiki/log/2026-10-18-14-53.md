# Skip extern decls for open-coded GNU setjmp builtins

_2026-10-18 14:53:15_

__builtin_setjmp/__builtin_longjmp are open-coded since 14d6f3a65 (frame-local buffer + _setjmp/_longjmp), but their extern declarations (link_name setjmp/longjmp) were still emitted. They're now in values::open_coded_builtin, so lower() skips the decl. rustc never warns on unused foreign fns, so #[allow(unused)] would not have changed anything. pr64242 and built-in-setjmp still pass.
