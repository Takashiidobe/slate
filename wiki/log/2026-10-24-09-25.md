_2026-10-24 09:25:42_

slate-parser-7xh6. SQLite then picked **atomic_load_n with **ATOMIC_RELAXED (unresolved in 9 TUs).

- expand*condition (pp/expand.rs) protects a \_\_has*\* operand only for owned names, so a user macro of that name expands. msvc owns only **has_include and **has_c_attribute.
- cl.exe checked: shims take the false branch; defined(**has_builtin) && **has_builtin(x) is false.
- Unguarded '#if \_\_has_builtin(x)' without a shim still errors where cl warns C4067 and takes the false branch; that is the general undefined-call case, filed as slate-pola.
