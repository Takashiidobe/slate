# Expand clang hand-lowered vector builtins in sema

_2026-10-02 20:14:04_

slate-dghn.14. Builtins that clang's CGBuiltin expands by hand (no TableGen alias) now expand in slate-parser sema instead of becoming an extern with vector parameters.

- New IR value intrinsic<T, llvm.name>(args) (ValueKind::Intrinsic). Slate finds the catalog entry by LLVM name and reuses lower_intrinsic, which now takes an error label instead of a BindingId.
- __builtin_reduce_* become llvm.vector.reduce.* (s/u/f picked from the element; maximum/minimum are fmaximum/fminimum). __builtin_elementwise_popcount/max/min/fma become llvm.ctpop, llvm.{s,u}{max,min} or {max,min}num, and llvm.fma.
- ia32 extract* and vextractf128_* become one-operand shuffles. pternlog{d,q}*_mask[z] becomes llvm.x86.avx512.pternlog.*. Masked forms expand only for an all-ones constant mask; otherwise they fall back to the ordinary builtin call (follow-up slate-dghn.15).
- reduce_f{add,mul}_p{s,d}512 become a halving tree of vector ops, with each level bound once by capture<%t>, matching clang's reassoc order. An intrinsic call cannot carry reassoc.
- slate-intrinsic-gen renders IIT VecElement as element:N, and slate resolves it to the overload's lane type, so llvm.vector.reduce.* now carry signatures. The catalog regenerates from the system LLVM 23.1.1 (--llvm-build /usr); the stale ~/llvm-project/build-cir does not reproduce it. The only unrelated diff is the loss of 4 RISC-V pssh* entries that the system LLVM lacks.
- Fixtures: slate x86_64/vector_reduce_builtins.c and parser ir_vector_builtin_expansion.c; ir_reduce_builtins.c regenerated.
- Redis: 38 -> 12 blocked functions, 10 -> 8 TUs. bitops.c and hyperloglog.c are clean. Remaining: VLAs (.6), __thread (.5), attributes (.8).
