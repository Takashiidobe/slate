# gate libc ABI extension probes

_2026-09-08 08:07:16_

Extended linux-libc-abi with procfs, resource, event, TCP, and glibc-specific extension records. Passed Slate libc defines to oracle and shim probes, gated extension headers and records by __SLATE_LIBC_GLIBC/__SLATE_LIBC_MUSL, and marked shim compilation explicitly. Validated all eight matrix combinations: musl passes on four targets; glibc compiles and reports expected missing extension declarations plus rusage and tcp_info layout fallout.
