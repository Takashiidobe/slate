Outcome: The library ratchet covers strong and weak long double aliases called through a callback table in a second translation unit.

Clang exits 0. The C driver linked against the translated static
archive exits 3, indicating both calls returned the wrong value.
Lean checks the expected x87 significand for 1 + 2^-63 and models
the alias as inheriting its target entry's ABI.

nm shows both aliases at __slate_ld__step__rf80_f80. The native
step wrapper has a different address. lower_function_alias
emits its .set against the Rust function item, which takes the
storage representation. The caller's x87 trampoline uses the
native convention. The strong and weak bindings survive.
