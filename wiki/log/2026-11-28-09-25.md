# slate-8ge7.26.7 pointer lift shims: shelved, aliasing through loaded pointers needs access-type effects

_2026-11-28 09:25:35_

- bead: slate-8ge7.26.7
- kind: bookkeeping
- area: wiki/concepts

Outcome: the C-ABI shim lift epic (slate-8ge7.26.7) is deferred with all open children; wiki/concepts/pointer-lift-shims.md now has a "Why this was shelved" section listing the analyses a sound version needs.

Why: outside callers give no provenance, so each Rust reference guarantee must come from the body alone. Nullability (all Option) and validity (caller contract + --no-lift-shim opt-out) were cheap; extent (slices, &CStr) needs an every-path proof including longjmp, already split off as .8. Aliasing stopped it: the planned params + named globals rule misses writes through pointers loaded from memory, lua's normal shape (push_copy(L, src) writing through L->top while src is &TValue).

A sound rule needs effects::Effects extensions it does not have (today: named internal mutable statics, callees, opaque): per-function read/write lvalue-type sets through untracked pointers and globals of every linkage, closed over callees; containment-aware type compatibility; and likely indirect-call resolution, since frealloc makes most of lua opaque. The .26.6 counts are an upper bound under none of these rules, so the real yield is unknown.

To restart: undefer, run .2 (measurement under the real rules) first, and build nothing until it shows the yield.
