# clang -fasm-blocks

_2026-10-04 09:28:50_

slate-parser-asmblk: clang flavor accepts -fasm-blocks / -fno-asm-blocks (Opt::AsmBlocks, clang-only; gcc and msvc reject it as an unknown option).
The last occurrence wins and sets MicrosoftFlags::asm_blocks. Parser::ms_asm_enabled accepts __asm on x86/x86_64 when MS extensions or asm_blocks is on, so -fms-extensions -fno-asm-blocks still parses __asm, as clang 23 does. No predefines change.
