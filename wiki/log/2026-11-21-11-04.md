# slate-8ge7.22 inline_temporaries: goto/label functions inline IR temporaries; lower_dispatch pre-records inlined temps and drops their slots; DefUse::ordered removed

_2026-11-21 11:04:04_

- bead: slate-8ge7.22
- kind: rewrite
- area: crates/slate-analysis/src/def_use.rs
- area: crates/slate-analysis/src/inline_temporaries.rs
- area: crates/slate/src/frontend/lowerer/control_flow.rs

Outcome: IR temporaries in functions with goto, labels or computed goto are inlined at lowering like any other; `DefUse::ordered` is gone and `inline_temps::late` fires 181 -> 9 over the fixture suite.

Before (C `sum += observed;` in a goto state machine, observed volatile):
    let __v1: i32 = sum + core::ptr::read_volatile(core::ptr::addr_of!(observed));
    sum = __v1;
After:
    sum += core::ptr::read_volatile(core::ptr::addr_of!(observed));

Rule: no function-wide order or CFG was needed. `inline_temporaries` already rejects any label, case, goto, computed goto, break/continue or asm between the producer and its read, so no jump can enter or leave that segment; the whole-function `ordered` gate was stricter than the proof. Removing the gate changed nothing by itself, because the goto lowering never consulted the fact.

Implementation:
- crates/slate-analysis/src/inline_temporaries.rs: no `ordered()` early return.
- crates/slate-analysis/src/def_use.rs: `ordered` field and method removed (no other reader).
- crates/slate/src/frontend/lowerer/statements.rs: `record_inlined(statement)` shared by `lower_statement` and the dispatch path.
- crates/slate/src/frontend/lowerer/control_flow.rs `lower_dispatch`: before lowering any block, runs `record_inlined` over every `Node::Statement` (block index order is not source order, so the read can be lowered before its producer); drops inlined temporaries from `graph.locals` so they get no `MaybeUninit` slot; emits nothing for them and does not attach trailing comments to them.

Fixture: regenerated FileCheck in 9 slate fixtures (goto_basic_block_entries, goto_project_control_flow, mem2reg_computed_goto, block_goto, cleanup_*, asm_goto_loop_exit_label, object_alignment, gcc/nested_functions; the rest is slot/cleanup renumbering) and merge macro_values. slate and merge profiles pass, including gcc-torture and gcc.dg.

Remaining `inline_temps::late` fires (9 in 7 fixtures): reads inside statement expressions or macro args (statement_expr_local_label, asm_goto_statement_expr*, null_deref_macro_args), vla_bound_side_effects, align_builtins, goto_basic_block_chain; plus lowerer `__t` temps (slate-8ge7.9.2).

Limits: no corpus measurement of unordered functions was needed, so none was done; a consumer that needs reaching definitions across labels (dead_store over a goto loop) still has no CFG and must prove straight-line ranges the same way.

See: slate-8ge7.9.1, slate-8ge7.9.2, wiki/concepts/slate-analysis.md#def-use.
