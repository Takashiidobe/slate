# slate-p58o.3.33: field address places

_2026-09-30 21:23:55_

Closed slate-p58o.3.33 (Lower plain record field places).

Field reads, writes, and array decays already landed with 3.15/3.16. The
remaining gap was address-of: `&outer->id`, `&outer->inner.value`, and
`&local.link->value` lowered to `addr_of_mut!((*p).field)` outside unsafe,
which is E0133 because the place dereferences a raw pointer.

Change (lowerer.rs, ValueKind::AddressOf): a non-Deref place is wrapped in
an unsafe block when place_is_unsafe holds (deref in the chain or a static),
matching how ValueKind::Read is lowered.

Fixture: field_address_places.c (failed with E0133 before the fix) covers
&field on locals, nested fields, array members, pointer-linked fields,
returned field addresses, and static struct fields.

Promoted: enum_field_ptr_call_arg, pointer_select_field_address,
ptr_member_store_unsafe; gcc-torture 20070212-1, 20070212-3, pr44468,
pr44555, struct-aliasing-1; gcc-dg torture__pr33870.
