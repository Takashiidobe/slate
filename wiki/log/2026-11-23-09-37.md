Outcome: `VaListKind` requires an explicit variant decision in both type and calling convention selection, and Slate Dylint supports opt-in exhaustive checking.

## Before

`VaListKind::CharPointer => VaConvention::Target, _ => VaConvention::Win64` silently inherited Win64 for any future kind. The lint treated it as a projection.

## After

`X86_64Sysv | AArch64Aapcs | ArmAapcs` explicitly selects Win64. `va_list_type` names the same three kinds for the opaque type. Existing variants keep their previous behavior.

## Implementation

- `#[slate::sensitive(exhaustive)]` reports wildcard-only and single-variant matches, including `matches!`.
- A `let … else` that omits a strict enum variant and falls back without rejecting also reports.
- `crates/slate-lints/ui/exhaustive.rs` covers the strict cases, nested patterns, rejecting fallback, and full variant enumeration.

## PlaceKind audit

- A temporary marker found 28 distinct sites with the ordinary rule and 97 with strict checking. Additional hits include simple binding and dereference projections.
- Start at addressability, unaligned access, and def-use traversal. Compare lane, swizzle, and field projections in each.
- Clang accepts lvalue swizzle reads and writes. Slate emits `PlaceKind::Swizzle` and then fails to lower the place to Rust for both aligned and packed vector fields. Single-lane controls translate. Filed as slate-yar4.61.

Limits: `PlaceKind` remains unmarked pending triage. `CompilerFlavor` and the remaining `ValueKind` hits stay with slate-5q63.25.2.
