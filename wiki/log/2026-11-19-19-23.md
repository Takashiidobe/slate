Outcome: `crates/slate-lints` is a dylint library with two tool lints, `slate::sensitive_wildcard` and `slate::sensitive_field_rest`, runnable with `cargo dylint --all` on slate-parser and slate; no enum is marked yet.

- Excluded from the Cargo workspace; registered under `[workspace.metadata.dylint]`. `slate-parser` and `slate` lib roots add `#![feature(register_tool)] #![register_tool(slate)]`, so `#[slate::sensitive]` and `#[expect(slate::..., reason)]` compile in normal builds and are checked only under dylint.
- `sensitive_wildcard`: per match arm. Each arm's pattern becomes leaves keyed by a field path (variant, field index). A `_`/binding leaf fires when any arm has a constructor of a sensitive enum at or below that path, so `(Kind::Int, _)`, a trailing `_` over a tuple, nested matches and `matches!` (reported at the call site) all fire. Arms whose body is `Err(..)`, `return Err(..)` or a diverging non-control-flow expression (`unreachable!`, `panic!`) are exempt. External macros and derives are skipped.
- `sensitive_field_rest`: struct and tuple-struct patterns with `..` that skip a field carrying `#[slate::sensitive]`.
- Lints are emitted at the arm/pattern HirId (`emit_node_span_lint`), so an arm-level `#[expect]` is fulfilled, unlike clippy's `wildcard_enum_match_arm`.
- No `clippy_utils`: plain rustc_private APIs only, to avoid a second toolchain pin.
- Toolchain: dylint 6.4 derives clippy's extra symbol list from the dated toolchain name, and on nightly-2026-10-25 that list (ceil, floor, powf, powi, round, sqrt) duplicates rustc's own, panicking the driver. Fix: the lint crate uses `slate-nightly-x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`, a `rustup toolchain link` alias to the same nightly; setup in the crate README.
- Probe: marking only `CTypeKind` gives 91 hits (82 slate-parser, 9 slate). Clippy reported 43 for the same enum because it counts matches, not arms/matches!/nested positions.
- UI test: `crates/slate-lints/ui/main.rs`, `cargo test` in the crate.

Limits: let-else and `if let` are not checked yet; no variant-count fingerprint in `reason`.
