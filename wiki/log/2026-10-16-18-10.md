# SQLite fuzzer: db images, extensions, jobs mode

_2026-10-16 18:10:41_

tools/fuzz/sqlite.py gains a libraries step that rebuilds native and translated SQLite with FTS3/4/5, RTREE, geopoly, dbstat, dbpage, bytecode/stmt vtabs, carray, STAT4 and ordered-set aggregates, and fuzz now uses -jobs/-workers with divergences saved under target/sqlite-fuzz/divergences. The harness decodes fuzzcheck's dbsqlfuzz format (hex image, \n--\n, SQL) and loads images with sqlite3_deserialize, applying the header db_config and optimization bits; seeds add 10.4k fuzzdata3/7 images paired with their probe scripts (46,391 total). Coverage on seed replay rose from 20.9k to 33.8k edges. Allocations are zero-filled per library. Corrupt images still show rare native-vs-native divergences that never reproduce alone; documented in tools/fuzz/README.md as known noise. No translation divergence found.
