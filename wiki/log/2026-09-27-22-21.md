# Static assert conditions keep header names reachable

_2026-09-27 22:21:53_

Fixes slate-parser-4opf and its duplicate slate-parser-btxg.

Reachability pruning dropped header typedefs, enumerators and other
declarations used only in a _Static_assert condition, so sema failed
with 'static assertion requires an integer constant expression: unknown
typedef' (e.g. sizeof(size_t) after <stddef.h>, sizeof(HANDLE) after
windows.h, sizeof(uint_fast8_t) after <stdint.h>). Both the
DeclKind::StaticAssert and StmtKind::StaticAssert arms of
src/reachability.rs were no-ops; they now walk the condition with
mark_expr.

Fixture: tests/fixtures/sema/static_assert_header_names.c with header
tests/fixtures/static_assert_header_names.h covers the file-scope and
block-scope forms. Also clears the uint_fast8_t item of
slate-parser-cxg.6.
