# Lower AArch64 SVE asm clobbers and aarch64 target features

_2026-10-28 14:32:08_

slate-3f8g.4.28: SVE predicate and ffr clobbers barriered with 'clobber pN'/'clobber ffr', and target("+sve") barriered with 'target feature +sve'. z<n> clobbers already canonicalize to v<n>; Rust out("v<n>") _ covers the whole z register since LLVM gives Z<n> no register units beyond Q<n>'s. Lowerer (lowerer/asm.rs): p0-p15 and ffr clobbers become out("pN") _/out("ffr") _ (Rust's clobber-only preg class; rustc accepts them without the sve feature). target_features.rs: aarch64 feature table (sve, sve2*, aes, sha2/3, crc, lse, dotprod, fp16, i8mm, bf16, rcpc, rdm, sm4, f32mm/f64mm, neon; gcc rdma->rdm, simd->neon), leading + stripped, lowered to #[target_feature(enable)]. SVE instructions need it: rustc rejects ptrue without sve. za, zt0, vg, fpmr clobbers stay barriers. clang rejects an ffr clobber (gcc accepts), so ffr is tested in gcc flavor; the parser accepting it under clang is slate-3f8g.4.30. Fixtures: aarch64/asm_aarch64_sve_clobbers.c (whilelo/ld1d/st1d loop, cntp, z0/z31 clobbers around a live double), gcc/aarch64/asm_aarch64_sve_ffr_clobber.c (setffr/rdffr). qemu-aarch64-static runs SVE by default.
