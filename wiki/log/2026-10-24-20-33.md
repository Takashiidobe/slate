_2026-10-24 20:33:53_

slate-3amj.3: -ffp-contract=off|on|fast|fast-honor-pragmas now accepted (gcc rejects fast-honor-pragmas, msvc rejects the flag).
compiler_args.rs: Opt::FpContract, FpContract enum, ParsedCompilerArgs::fp_contract resolves it into MathOptions::contract (new field, compiler_options.rs). sema/numeric.rs Context::for_dialect seeds region.contract from it, falling back to default_contraction; pragma FP_CONTRACT DEFAULT follows.
Oracle semantics (clang 23, gcc 16): clang processes argv in order; -ffast-math/-funsafe-math-optimizations set fast, their -fno- restores the last -ffp-contract= seen or on (clang Driver restoreFPContractState). fast and fast-honor-pragmas both honor the pragma in clang 23. gcc: explicit flag wins regardless of order; otherwise unsafe math gives fast (matters in ISO modes, where the default is off); -ffp-contract=on is implemented (within an expression).
