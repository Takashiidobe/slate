# slate-8ge7.26.6 pointer lift: SLATE_LIFT_REASONS would_lift_with_shim counts for exported and address-taken functions (lua, redis)

_2026-11-27 18:54:17_

- bead: slate-8ge7.26.6
- kind: feature
- area: crates/slate-analysis/src/pointer

Outcome: `SLATE_LIFT_REASONS` now also prints `lift-shim` lines: for every exported or address-taken function, whether its pointer params/returns would lift behind a C-ABI shim, and otherwise the first blocker; the counts say a shim design would roughly double closed redis lifts and multiply open-mode lifts by 4-16x.

Gap: slots in `not_candidate` functions (address_taken, ExportPin) were counted but never analysed, so nobody knew whether a shim (lifted body + `export_name` extern "C" wrapper for the symbol and function pointers, direct internal calls) would pay.

After (stderr, measurement only; facts and output byte-identical, lua checked with diff):

    lift-shim role=parameter pin=address_taken outcome=would_lift_with_shim reason=ref via=- count=288
    lift-shim role=parameter pin=address_taken outcome=raw reason=shim.aliased_mut via=local count=111
    lift-shim-caller role=parameter change=gained reason=mut count=35
    lift-shim-site function=rioBufferWrite pin=address_taken slot=r outcome=would_lift_with_shim reason=mut via=-   (=sites only)

Implementation: after the real solve, `Summaries::assume_external_callers` makes every blocked function a candidate unless it is main, variadic, naked or interrupt, and marks it `external`. Flow, ownership, length, c_string and emit run again. External-caller rules: caller-side unproven reasons (macro_call_site, argument, part_offset, raw_argument) and unproven propagation over argument edges skip external callees; external functions get no caller-proven length pairs; &CStr needs only the body string use. Nullability is Option where the body null-checks or an internal caller passes null. Post rules in `reasons::report_shims`: many without a body-proven count (`length::accesses_within_count` against an unwritten integer param) -> `shim.unbounded_many`; a &mut param when more than one param of that function lifts -> `shim.aliased_mut`. `lift-shim-caller` counts params/returns of real candidates whose lift changed in the rerun (callers no longer escape into a non-candidate callee). None were lost.

Verdict: worth a design bead. Closed redis would go from 575 to about 1160 lifted params; open redis from 116 to about 1865; lua open from 41 to about 170. Closed lua gains little (11).

Limits: the shim nullability trusts that external callers do not pass null where the body does not check, the same contract as C. `abi_pass`/`target_feature` pins (33 slots) are counted but may not be shimmable. Locals are not reported.
