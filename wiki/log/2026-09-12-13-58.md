# Split preprocessor into src/pp modules

_2026-09-12 13:58:12_

Moved src/pp.rs into src/pp/ with no behavior change, as step 1 of epic slate-parser-11p (token-based directive parsing and error threading). mod.rs keeps Preprocessor, the line-based block/conditional visitor, define recording and text helpers; condition.rs holds Condition simplification (conjunction, simplify_condition, replace_subterm, is_statically_true/false); expand.rs holds macro expansion, divergent-condition detection and function-macro substitution; include.rs holds include directive parsing and search-path resolution; error.rs holds PPError/PPFailure and source rendering. Cross-module methods are pub(super). Planned follow-ups: Loc-carrying errors replacing panics, lexer splice/start-of-line support, then a macro-state-free directive syntax tree plus walker.
