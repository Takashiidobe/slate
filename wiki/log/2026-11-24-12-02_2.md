# slate-e36k.23 vector conditions: scalar condition check rejects Clang vector expressions

_2026-11-24 12:02:45_

- bead: slate-e36k.23
- kind: bug
- area: crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported

Outcome: The unsupported ratchet covers Clang vector ternary and logical expressions.

The fixture uses zero, positive and negative condition lanes.
Clang selects a ternary arm by the sign bit, so a positive lane
selects the false arm. Logical operators test the lane for
nonzero and produce either zero or all ones. Lean checks a
positive witness that distinguishes the rules.

Clang exits 0 on all four Linux targets. Slate's scalar condition
check rejects the ternary, AND, OR and NOT before emitting IR.

Fixture: crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported/clang_vector_conditions.c
Limits: Vector condition support remains open in slate-e36k.23.
