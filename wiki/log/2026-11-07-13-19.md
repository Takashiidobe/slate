# Predeclare clang's aarch64 ACLE types

_2026-11-07 13:19:32_

slate-e36k.9: clang flavor rejected **SVFloat32_t and the other SVE/NEON ACLE
type names that clang predeclares on aarch64, so glibc bits/math-vector.h
(typedef **SVFloat32*t **sv_f32_t) failed sema under --all-declarations.
gcc_target_type (sema/builtins.rs) now also resolves, for clang on AArch64, the
subset of GCC_AARCH64_TYPES clang 23 accepts: **SV\*, multi-lane **<Elem>NxM_t
vectors and **mfp8. GCC-internal names (\_\_builtin_aarch64_simd*_, x1_t
vectors, scalar \_\_Poly_\_t, un-prefixed sv\*) stay unknown, matching clang
exactly across all 118 table names. Clang on arm32 predeclares none. Fixtures:
clang/linux/aarch64/predeclared_acle_types.c,
error/clang/linux/aarch64/gcc_only_neon_type.c. aarch64 glibc math.h now parses
fully; regenerated libc catalogs are unchanged (no sizeless types in libc;
libc-gen fallback still needed for slate-e36k.10).
