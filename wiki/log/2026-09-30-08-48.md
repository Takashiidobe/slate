# pp: builtin macros are defined, per flavor (ryfo.7)

_2026-09-30 08:48:16_

slate-parser-ryfo.7. Builtin macros now live in Preprocessor::macros as entries with MacroDef::builtin set, seeded first by configure (seed_builtin_names), so defined(), #ifdef, #undef, #define and push/pop_macro treat them like other macros and nothing can drift between two lists.

- Per flavor, checked against clang, gcc and tools/cl.exe: all nine (__LINE__, __FILE__, __FILE_NAME__, __BASE_FILE__, __INCLUDE_LEVEL__, __COUNTER__, __DATE__, __TIME__, __TIMESTAMP__) are defined for clang and gcc, and #undef/#define take effect. msvc lacks the three gnu-only names (slate used to expand them there) and ignores #define/#undef of its builtins with C4117 (is_reserved_macro).
- expand_token expands a builtin only through its table entry; may_expand no longer guesses from a __ prefix.
- __TIMESTAMP__ is new: the current file's modification time as asctime text, like clang. The date helpers became a free calendar_time(SystemTime).
- Wiki: preprocessor.md Expansion.

Validation: 7482 tests pass; new fixtures builtin-macros-defined.c under clang/linux/x86_64, gcc/linux/x86_64 and msvc/windows/x86_64, each matching its compiler. pp_diff mbedtls: 198/8 -> 206/0.
