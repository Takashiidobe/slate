# Lower clang-flavor Win64 va_arg

_2026-11-08 19:25:23_

slate-j3by.11: with the clang flavor on x86_64-pc-windows-msvc, va_list is char * and va_arg carries VaConvention::Target with a pointer list, which values.rs only lowered for i386, so every va_arg was a 'cannot lower VaArg' barrier. The Win64 VaArg arm (already used for __builtin_ms_va_list) now also takes any pointer-list VaArg on TargetFamily::X86_64 and reads through __slate_win64_va (8-byte slots, aggregates not of size 1/2/4/8 by reference). Removed the _WIN32 guard around scaled_sum/vcall in fixtures.dylink/long_double_abi and regenerated its FileCheck block; dylink_windows passes.
