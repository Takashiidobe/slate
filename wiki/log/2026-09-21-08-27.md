# Unsequenced evaluation order follows clang, and says so in the IR

_2026-09-21 08:27:19_

An unsequenced modification is UB (C11 6.5p2), so there is no order to reproduce and lowering silently picked one. Measured on all three oracles with f(i++, i++) and i starting at 1: clang gives 12 = f(1,2), gcc 21 = f(2,1), MSVC 19.51 11 = f(1,1), all with final i = 3. Slate hoists left to right and so matches clang.

Decision: keep one order rather than thread flavor into IR generation. Emulating per flavor would pin three compiler versions' codegen accidents rather than documented semantics; gcc's order falls out of its argument-pushing convention and is not guaranteed between releases. Left to right is also Rust's own argument order.

What was missing was the record of the choice. Statement::Temporary and Statement::Write gained an unsequenced flag, printed as 'let %n: T [synthetic, unsequenced]' and 'write<T, unsequenced>(...)', following the pattern of every other UB the backend consumes as an attribute rather than a diagnostic (overflow=ub, by_zero=ub, fallthrough=ub_if_used). No -Wunsequenced.

New src/sema/sequencing.rs decides it. A location is a binding, or the object one pointer binding points at, plus the path of members and constant subscripts inside it: x.a++ and x.b++ do not conflict even sharing a storage unit, b[0] and b[1] are distinct, and a computed subscript compares equal only to another computed one. Two locations conflict when one is a prefix of the other and at least one access writes. Groups are a call's callee and arguments, both operands of an arithmetic or comparison operator, and an assignment's target against its value, the last restricted to a second side effect on the target since C11 6.5.16p3 leaves i = i + 1 well defined.

Validated against gcc -Wsequence-point over the corpus: 1963 configurations both accept, zero marked-here-silent-there and zero the reverse. clang's -Wunsequenced is narrower, missing g.a++ and p->a++, so gcc is the detection oracle while clang remains the order oracle.

ir-spec.md gained the three-compiler table and the policy; ir-grammar.md the two new spellings. Fixture sema/ir_unsequenced.c pairs marked and unmarked cases. Gate, clippy, fmt clean; sweep 2624/2698 -> 2625/2699 with no group changes.
