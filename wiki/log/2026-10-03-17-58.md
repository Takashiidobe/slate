# 0o octal prefix (slate-parser-cxg.40)

_2026-10-03 17:58:59_

- C2y 0o/0O octal constants (N3319) lex as integers in C and #if for gcc and clang in every mode (both oracles accept, pedwarn before C2y). MSVC rejects ('bad suffix on number'), so StandardFeatures::octal_prefix is off for msvc and the lexer keeps such spellings FloatLit there.
- The radix split was copied four times (Token::integer_value, integer_value_i128, the lexer's int/float decision, const_expr::parse_integer_literal); all now use Lexer::integer_radix, which knows 0x/0b/0o/leading 0.
- gcc.dg migrated for both flavors: bitint-112.c c23-octal-constants-1/2.c c2y-digit-separators-1.c c2y-octal-constants-2.c octal-constants-2/3.c. Error fixture: msvc octal_prefix_unsupported.c.
