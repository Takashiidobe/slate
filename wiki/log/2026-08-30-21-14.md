# Block packed-over-aligned bug on upstream CIR gap

_2026-08-30 21:14:15_

slate-os0h.3.1.11 (20040709-2, 20040709-3: packed structs with over-aligned
bitfield/long-double members) traced to clang -fclangir -emit-cir itself
failing, not a Slate bug: 'x86_64 calling-convention lowering not yet
implemented' for at least 13 of the fixture's 26 macro-generated packed
structs. Reproduced with bare $SLATE_CLANG -fclangir -emit-cir, no Slate
involved.

This is the same root cause already tracked by slate-os0h.22.1 (P0): CIR's
x86-64 ABI eightbyte classifier doesn't handle the 'pad' member kind that a
recent upstream CIRGen commit started emitting. Added 20040709-2/-3 to that
bug's case list and added a `bd dep add` blocking edge instead of duplicating
tracking. slate-os0h.3.1.11 marked BLOCKED; both cases stay in
tests/fixtures.gcc-torture.unsupported/ until CIR fixes the classifier.
