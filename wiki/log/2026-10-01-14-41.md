# Derive __builtin_reduce_* signatures from the vector operand

_2026-10-01 14:41:56_

slate-parser-6x05.14: __builtin_reduce_{add,mul,and,or,xor,max,min,maximum,minimum} are CustomTypeChecking, so sema rejected them as unsupported Clang builtins. Redis hnsw.c reaches them through clang's avx512 intrinsics headers.

sema/builtins.rs adds DerivedSignature::Reduction(OperandClass): one vector operand, result = element type. Integer elements for add/mul/and/or/xor, arithmetic for max/min, floating for maximum/minimum (matches clang 22 diagnostics). A non-vector or wrong-class operand is Rejected("reduction builtin operand type"). Calls lower like the elementwise builtins: a direct call to an implicit fn with the per-call signature.

__builtin_reduce_assoc_fadd/in_order_fadd stay unsupported: they're in the generated table, but clang 22 rejects them as unknown.
