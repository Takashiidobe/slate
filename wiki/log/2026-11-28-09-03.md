Outcome: wiki/concepts/pointer-lift-shims.md records the C-ABI shim design for lifting exported and address-taken functions (lifted Rust fn + extern "C-unwind" wrapper that keeps the symbol and every function-pointer use); linked from pointer-capability-lattice.md.

Covers the wrapper shape and attribute placement, wrapper conversions (as_ref/as_mut only, lifted returns back to raw), outside-caller rules (all lifted params Option, aliasing with compatible params and mutable globals, caller-side rules kept for internal callers), routing (internal direct calls -> lifted fn, address-of -> wrapper), and the v1 shimmable set.

New decision (2026-11-28): shimmability is a deny-list checked on the function's own attributes, not on Program::export_pin, because open mode collapses every export to ExportPin::Exported. Closed-mode section/used/retain/dll_storage/selectany/constructor/destructor/weak_odr/discardable are shimmable (attribute moves to the wrapper); fallback and declared_other_prototype join the epic's exclusions. The slate-8ge7.26.6 open-mode counts therefore include abi_pass/target_feature/alias exports that v1 will not shim.

Revision (same day): shimmed functions lift only Option<&T>/Option<&mut T>. Slices and &CStr stay raw (shim.no_slice, shim.no_c_str): from_raw_parts(p, n) and CStr::from_ptr claim more memory than C needs (find_zero(a, 1000) on int a[2] = {1, 0} with an early return is fine in C, UB behind a slice wrapper); an every-path proof is slate-8ge7.26.7.8. Costs about 7% of redis open and 10% of redis closed would-lift params per the .26.6 counts. The whole-object contract (non-null pointer = live whole T) is accepted by default, with --no-lift-shim <name> / --no-lift-shims as the escape hatch (.3). Units with null_pointer_is_valid (-fno-delete-null-pointer-checks, the kernel) are not shimmable.
