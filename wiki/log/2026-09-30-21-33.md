# slate-p58o.3.39: variadic function definitions

_2026-09-30 21:33:13_

Closed slate-p58o.3.39 (variadic definitions) and slate-p58o.3.38 (va_list,
lowered in def0421ab).

Change (lowerer.rs):
- lower_function no longer refuses variadic=true. A variadic definition
  becomes unsafe extern "C-unwind" fn f(fixed.., mut __va_args: ...), the
  stable C-variadic form (1.99). va_start clones __va_args.
- FunctionName.is_unsafe (extern or variadic) marks direct calls that need
  an unsafe block; is_extern still gates function decay.
- Pointers to variadic function types are still refused by lower_type.

- variadic_definitions.c covers a definition that ignores its varargs,
