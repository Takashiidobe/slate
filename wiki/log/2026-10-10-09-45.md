# Embed parameters in __has_embed

_2026-10-10 09:45:29_

slate-parser-cxg.7.12: `__has_embed("f" limit(1))` failed with 'expected ), found limit' because only a bare resource name was matched, and `__has_embed` operands were protected from macro expansion (so `__FILE__` and `limit(L)` never expanded).

- New `pp/embed.rs` holds `#embed` and `__has_embed` with one parameter parser: `limit`, `prefix`, `suffix`, `if_empty` (and `__x__` spellings), plus `offset` under the flavor's vendor prefix (`gnu::` gcc, `clang::` clang). Two Colon tokens spell `::`; lexing it as one token is slate-parser-cxg.7.22.
- `limit`/`offset` are `#if`-style constant expressions via new `const_expr::Parser::evaluate_directive_operand` (the wide value; `evaluate_with_defined` now wraps it). Offset applies before limit.
- Missing clause or negative operand errors in both forms; unknown or foreign-vendor parameters error in `#embed` (new `UnknownEmbedParameter`) and make `__has_embed` 0. `__has_embed` is 2 when the window is empty, else 1.
- `__has_embed` added to `expands_has_operand`.
