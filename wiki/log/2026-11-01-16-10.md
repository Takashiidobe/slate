# Keep macros that goto out of their body inline

_2026-11-01 16:10:28_

slate-f2zu.9.5.12.19: decided not to lift macros whose body jumps out with goto; no code change. They stay inline, which is always allowed. Affects PULL, PULLBYTE, NEEDBITS and ROOM in zlib (20544 inline expansions at the time the bead was filed), which read caller locals and goto inf_leave directly or through a nested macro. Lifting would mean passing a state change and a jump through the template, because inflate lowers through dispatch, and passing the label as a parameter, because Rust labels are hygienic. The local_in_dispatch_function rule in macro_keep stays open under .22.
