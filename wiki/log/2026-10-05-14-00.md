# zlib e2e (slate-dghn.30)

_2026-10-05 14:00:18_

zlib translates with 0 barriers (15 TUs of libz.a; the 4 global visibility barriers were cleared by the Lua work) and passes as a CMakeLibrary recipe.

Harness (tools/corpus/e2e.py):
- CMakeLibrary.variants: other builds of the library (zlib's libz.so next to libz.a) that relink also swaps for the translated archive, so the shared-linked examples and minigzip test translated code (ldd shows no libz; deflate is the Rust symbol).
- ctest cases whose command is not an in-build executable (cmake packaging tests, llvm-cov summary) are reported as skipped rows and counted in ctest_skipped.
- CMakeLibrary.benchmark runs after the test stage against test-tree/. zlib_benchmark pipes a 64 MiB input (sources + zlib.3.pdf, repeated) through minigzip, minigzip -9, and minigzip -d, requiring identical native/translated output.

Results: 5/5 ctest (zlib_example, example64, examplestatic, example64static, coverage/infcover), 210/210 byte-identical minigzip -c runs over 35 inputs x 6 flag sets; benchmark compress 1.04x, -9 1.02x, decompress 1.04x native. libyaml and cJSON reruns still pass.
