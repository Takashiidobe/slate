# Lower asm goto through the dispatch CFG

_2026-10-10 08:18:15_

slate-3f8g.4.17.8. An asm with labels now forces lower_dispatch and becomes Node::AsmGoto(stmt, asm, fallthrough, label entries), a multi-successor node like Branch/Switch. Lowering assigns __slate_state = fallthrough, emits the asm! with one label { __slate_state = N; } operand per IR label (template %lN prints the positional slot; unused labels get a '# {slot}' comment), runs output writebacks, then continue '__slate_dispatch. A ()-typed label block resumes after the asm!, and rustc assigns outputs before entering it, so writebacks run once on every path. rust_ast AsmOperand::Label dropped its destination field. Any Out/InOut operand plus labels sets #![feature(asm_goto_with_outputs)]. lower_asm takes the label states; called outside dispatch it barriers. Promoted gnu_asm_goto, asm_goto_statement_expr, x86_64/asm_goto_statement_expr (renamed _if_zero: same basename collided in the harness output dir), gcc-dg torture__pr58670.
