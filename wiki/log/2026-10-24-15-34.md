# Policy: ignore rustc gaps for Windows \*call conventions

_2026-10-24 15:34:32_

Decision (user, 2026-10-24, after slate-ck8y): for **vectorcall, **stdcall, **fastcall, **thiscall and similar Windows \*call conventions, slate lowers only signatures whose pass shapes rustc already handles like clang/MSVC and barriers the rest permanently. No workarounds (signature flattening, c_shim bridges) and no rustc patches.
Why: compensating in slate would rebuild clang's front-end ABI classification (HVA register allocation, SysV record coercion), and fixing rustc is out of scope.
Beads: closed slate-hxtv (wait-for-rustc for vectorcall barriers); slate-7h9e kept only for default-convention divergences (abi_rust_divergence.c), barrier first; slate-lc0y kept as the precise-barrier prerequisite for i686 vectorcall; filed a bead to map i686 stdcall/fastcall/thiscall for rustc-native shapes. Recorded with bd remember.
