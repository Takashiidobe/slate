# Lower volatile and const volatile bit-field members

_2026-11-09 11:28:37_

slate-54ed: records with a volatile (or const volatile) bit-field failed with 'cannot lower record to Rust' because lower_record (lowerer/types.rs) rejected every volatile bit-field outright. The rejection was the only blocker: bit-field place reads/writes already lower volatile accesses as read_volatile of the storage unit plus getter, and read_volatile/set/write_volatile read-modify-write for stores (what clang emits), and aggregate initialization stays a plain build. Dropped the check. const_volatile (const fn read_volatile) is not involved: C never performs volatile accesses during constant initialization. Fixture fixtures/volatile_bitfield_members.c (locals, globals, through volatile pointers, const volatile reads, packed [u8;8] unit with 40/20-bit fields). Promoted gcc-dg.gcc-only torture__pr48124-1/-4 and volatile-bitfields-1/-2. slate profile green.
