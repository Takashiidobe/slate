# Generate clang __builtin_neon_* builtins from arm_neon.td

_2026-11-07 13:50:15_

slate-e36k.11: clang flavor had no NEON builtin table, so arm_neon.h (and clang 23's arm64_neon.h, which includes it) failed with unresolved __builtin_neon_vld1q_x4_v.
- generate_clang_builtins.py --neon-table NAME=a.td,b.td runs clang-tblgen -gen-arm-neon-sema (arm_neon.td, arm_fp16.td: the two clang builds into NEON::BI*) and decodes the Builtin::Info type strings (V<n>, S/U/L/W/I, h/x/y/m, C/*). W becomes int64_t (FixedInt, long on LP64, long long on Windows); m (__mfp8) is unsigned char like the gcc table. Builtins listed in GET_NEON_OVERLOAD_CHECK get a _Constant trailing type code.
- CLANG_NEON_BUILTINS (1173 rows) lives in clang_aarch64_builtins.rs with shared AARCH64_PROTOTYPE numbering (hence renumber churn; without --neon-table the generator reproduces HEAD byte for byte). target_builtin resolves it for AArch64 (after CLANG_AARCH64_BUILTINS) and Arm32.
- Generated from llvmorg-23.1.4; generated-sources.md updated.
