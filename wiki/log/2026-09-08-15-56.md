# oracle macro inventory and presence probes

_2026-09-08 15:56:35_

Added an oracle-only preprocessor collector for header macro surfaces. It invokes configured target Clang with -E -dD, follows line markers to retain definition-file attribution, applies redefinitions and undefs, classifies object-like/function-like and __private macros, and writes a structured JSON manifest plus raw output under target/libc-declaration-probe. A generated shim-only presence probe uses #ifndef for every public oracle macro; function-like macros are intentionally presence-only until dedicated compile/value/behavior strategies land. The glibc fcntl.h test verifies O_RDONLY collection and source provenance. The manual fcntl probe now reports real missing macros, including AT_EXECVE_CHECK and F_DUPFD, rather than relying on AST expansion.
