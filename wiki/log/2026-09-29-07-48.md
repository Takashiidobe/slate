_2026-09-29 07:48:47_

slate-parser-qokl. The bug (a tag first named in a typedef or a prototype and completed later printed as 'struct incomplete', losing its body) no longer reproduces: 79d2cdd0 (cc94.6, one shared TypeResolver) removed Lowerer::type_spans, the snapshot map, and resolve_module now emits each definition from lower.types.definitions with its span from types.owners or tag_span.

Verified the bead's repros, gcc-torture 930408-1 (enum foo keeps e0/e1) and the stdio fixtures (_IO_FILE prints its body). The struct incomplete entries left in fixtures are genuine: glibc's opaque _IO_marker/_IO_codecvt/_IO_wide_data, prototype-scope struct stat in <glob.h>, and block-scope forward declarations in ir_tag_scopes.c and block_forward_tag_hides_outer.c.

Added tests/fixtures/clang/linux/x86_64/ir_tag_completed_after_use.c covering a struct completed after a typedef, a struct completed after a prototype names it, and an enum completed after a typedef. No source change.
