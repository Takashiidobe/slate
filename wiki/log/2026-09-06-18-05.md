# slate-3f8g.4.15.7: n and I/J/K/L/M/N/O as const-eligible input constraints

_2026-09-06 18:05:04_

Extended immediate-operand recognition in intrinsics.rs::lower_extended_asm
past the hardcoded literal "i" check.

- New constraint_is_constant_only(&str) in asm.rs: true when every char of the
  constraint parses (via the existing parse_constraint_atoms) to
  Constraint::ConstantEligible -- which already covered i/n/I/J/K/L/M/N/O, it
  just wasn't consulted anywhere outside constraint_allows_generic_reg.
- Replaced the three `*constraint == "i"` exact-match checks in intrinsics.rs
  (input_specs classification, the known-constant guard, and the AsmOperand::Const
  build) with calls to constraint_is_constant_only.
- No per-letter GCC encoding-range validation (I = 0-31 shift count, etc.) --
  matches the bead's accepted initial scope; an out-of-range immediate fails to
  assemble downstream instead of being caught here.

New fixture tests/fixtures/gnu_asm_constant_letters.c: two functions using "n"
and "I" respectively as the immediate operand of addl/sall. Both lowering and
rewrites differential profiles pass; the 9 pre-existing asm fixtures are
unaffected.
