# x86 asm rbp operands and rbp/rsp clobbers

_2026-11-01 21:23:40_

slate-3f8g.4.18: rustc rejects rbp/rsp as asm operands and clobbers. lowerer/asm.rs now routes x86 rbp operands through the existing reserved-register path (scratch save, copy in/out, restore) with per-dialect mov syntax and r/e width, and saves/restores rbp and rsp clobbers through an early out(reg) _. rsp operand stays a barrier. Fixture: x86_64/asm_rbp_operand.c (AT&T and Intel). Clobbers have no differential fixture because the clang -O0 oracle itself uses rbp as frame pointer; checked by hand with translate.
