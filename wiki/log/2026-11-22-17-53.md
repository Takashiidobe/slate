Outcome: Mutability facts reach nested function and block scopes through
the shared read-only IR visitor. Aligned array field reads leave their
base binding immutable.

Before: checksum emitted `mut s` for `s.data[i]`. A block incrementing a
by-reference capture emitted `mut` on the capture pointer and increment
temporary.

After: checksum emits `s: Sample`. The capture pointer remains `*mut`,
and both the pointer binding and increment temporary are immutable.

Rule: The mutable and read-only visitors share the exhaustive IR child
dispatch. for_each_function_ref enumerates top-level and nested scopes,
including block bodies in global initializers. DefUse reads one scope at
a time. Copied block captures have facts scoped to the invoke function.
By-reference capture writes go through the pointer loaded at entry.

Aligned field reads follow fixed-array projections rooted in a binding
when each enclosing record has natural field alignment. Packed and
underaligned layouts retain the conservative address-use fact.

Fixtures: aggregate_zero_fill.c, block_basic.c, block_byref.c, and
block_globals.c. The packed GCC fixture torture__pr52402.c exercises the
raw mutable address path.

Validation: Clippy, formatting, and the release slate, parser, and merge profiles passed.
