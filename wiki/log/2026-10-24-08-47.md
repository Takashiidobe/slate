# Rewrite unsigned pointer offsets to add/sub

_2026-10-24 08:47:28_

slate-o3gw.6: .offset(x as isize) -> .add(x as usize) and .offset(-(x as isize)) -> .sub(x as usize) when x is provably unsigned (unsigned var, unsigned cast, unsigned arithmetic) or a representable non-negative literal (reuses constant_index_casts::representable_literal). Signed indices are left alone. New rule rules/ptr_offset.rs (PtrOffset, after NullCompare). Fixture: ptr_offset_unsigned.c, plus 82 existing fixtures regenerated.
