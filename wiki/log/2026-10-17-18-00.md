# Lower _BitInt across call boundaries

_2026-10-17 18:00:43_

Variadic call arguments of _BitInt narrower than 32 bits are cast to i32/u32 slots (calls.rs). va_arg of small _BitInt reads an i32/u32 or i64/u64 slot and renormalizes through narrow_integer; i16/u16 join i8/u8 in the promoted read types. va_arg of _BitInt wider than 64 bits reads the bitint::BInt/BUint type through the target shim: __slate_sysv_va::arg with two INTEGER eightbytes up to 16 bytes and memory beyond, __slate_i386_va::arg on i686, __slate_aapcs_va::gp on aarch64 (WideInt::fits_registers). New fixtures bitint_calls (params, returns, struct members, function pointers, small/64-bit va_arg) and bitint_va_wide (100/128/200/300-bit va_arg). bitint_va_wide is listed unsupported on i686 because clang -m32 itself mis-reads _BitInt(100) va_arg, and on aarch64 because clang rejects _BitInt wider than 128; bitint_calls is unsupported on aarch64 for the same reason. Remaining items from the bead stay as filed follow-ups: bitint-38 constexpr global read, torture bitint-22 f80 negation in global initializers, torture bitint-75 __builtin_stdc_*.
