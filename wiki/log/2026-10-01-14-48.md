# offsetof looks through anonymous struct/union members

_2026-10-01 14:48:30_

slate-parser-6x05.12: offsetof(struct s, off), where off sits inside an anonymous union, was rejected as 'unknown offsetof member'. TypeRegistry::offsetof_field searched only the record's directly named fields.
