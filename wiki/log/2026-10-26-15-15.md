# Lower memcpy_inline and the clang alignment builtins; promote gnu_builtins_memory

_2026-10-26 15:15:17_

slate-2o6o.23: __builtin_memcpy_inline lowers to core::ptr::copy_nonoverlapping (frontend/lowerer/values.rs). Aliasing it to memcpy via link_name fails: rustc rejects a memcpy extern whose void return differs from std's runtime memcpy. gnu_builtins_memory.c promoted; passes x86_64, i686, aarch64. Closed.

slate-2o6o.22: __builtin_align_up/align_down/is_aligned are CustomBuiltin::Align in slate-parser (sema/builtins.rs, typing rule in sema/typer.rs, expansion in sema/expression.rs::align_builtin). Integers: wrapping arithmetic on a captured mask in the value's type; pointers: captured once, ptr_to_int to size_t, then a byte ptr_offset so type and provenance are kept; is_aligned is _Bool. Documented in wiki/concepts/ir/builtins.md. Fixtures: slate tests/fixtures/align_builtins.c (pointers, const pointee, narrow ints, negative ints, side-effecting operands), slate-parser clang/linux/x86_64/builtin_align.c and error/.../builtin-align-operand.c. __builtin_extract_return_addr/__builtin_frob_return_addr lower to their argument (identity on every supported target). gnu_builtins_core.c now only fails on __builtin_object_size, filed as slate-yar4.1; .22 stays open, blocked on it.
