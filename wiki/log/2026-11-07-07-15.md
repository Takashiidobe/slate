# Drop deprecated core module constants in vendored num-traits

_2026-11-07 07:15:18_

Translated crates (slate-zlib) warned on num-traits' use of deprecated core::{i8..usize, u8..usize, f32, f64} modules and core::$t::NAN/INFINITY. Rewrote core::$t::X to <$t>::X in float_trait_impl! (lib.rs), core::f32::X to f32::X in lib.rs tests, and removed the core primitive-module imports from bounds.rs, cast.rs, float.rs, int.rs, ops/overflowing.rs and ops/mul_add.rs so names resolve to the associated constants. No ticket; not verified by regen.
