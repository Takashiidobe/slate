# __has_feature and __has_extension per flavor and standard

_2026-10-10 16:01:15_

slate-parser-cxg.7.20: gcc.dg has-feature.c failed in every mode but C11-C17 because slate answered __has_feature/__has_extension from one clang-seeded list regardless of flavor and standard.

Probed every name in slate's lists (plus sanitizer, cxx_* and objc candidates) with gcc and clang in c89..gnu23:
- both: c_alignas, c_alignof, c_atomic, c_generic_selections, c_static_assert, c_thread_local are features from C11, extensions always.
- clang: objc_c_static_assert also from C11; c_fixed_enum a feature from C23; everything else as in KNOWN_FEATURES/KNOWN_EXTENSIONS.
- gcc: features attribute_deprecated_with_message, attribute_unavailable_with_message, enumerator_attributes, tls; cxx_binary_literals a feature from C23; extensions add the C11 names, cxx_binary_literals and gnu_asm_goto_with_outputs(_full). Nothing else.

has_checks::has_feature/has_extension now take the flavor and standard; GCC_FEATURES and GCC_EXTENSIONS hold gcc's lists, and the standard-gated names left KNOWN_FEATURES. Slate matches both oracles on all 20 compiler/mode pairs of the probe.

The __has_c_attribute half of the bead (macro-expanded argument) already passed; c23-has-c-attribute-2.c is migrated as a regression test, with has-feature.c (gnu23). New fixtures: pp_has_feature_by_standard.c for gcc and clang (C99, C11, C23). Parser profile 8062/8062.
