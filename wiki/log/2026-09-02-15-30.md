# fix char-pointer recognition gap and dead Option<T> Custom arm

_2026-09-02 15:30:46_

Two fixes found while auditing Type::Custom/Type::Generic sites:

1. is_char_ptr (string_params.rs) and elem_is_byte (pointer_lattice.rs), used
   by the backend string-param/pointer-lattice passes to recognize char*
   params, checked Type::Custom name strings "libc::c_char",
   "std::ffi::c_char", "core::ffi::c_char" but never Type::CLib(CLibType::CHAR)
   - the representation function_abi.rs's repair_typedef_type actually
   produces for plain-char extern params/fields (the charness work,
   6531e13f). Any char* that went through that rewrite silently stopped
   being recognized as string-shaped downstream. Added a
   Type::CLib(CLibType::CHAR) arm to both; dropped the "std::ffi::c_char"/
   "core::ffi::c_char" string arms since no site ever constructs
   Type::Custom with those strings (only "libc::c_char" is real, via
   Type::parse("*mut libc::c_char") in lowerer.rs:941).

2. is_option_like_type (inline_temps.rs) had a
   Type::Custom(name) => name.starts_with("Option<") arm. No construction
   site ever builds Option<T> as a Custom string - it's always
   Type::Generic{name:"Option",..} (already matched) or Type::FnPtr
   (rendered "Option<unsafe fn...>", matched separately). Dead arm, removed.

Verified: cargo check/clippy clean, `rewrites` profile 92/92 (all three
files are backend/interproc + engine/rules).
