Added Opt::MsvcPack (compiler_args.rs, msvc only) setting pack_struct_alignment, which feeds LayoutOptions::default_max_field_alignment. /Zp alone is 1; other numbers fall back to 8 as cl.exe does (warning D9014); a non-number is rejected. Verified with cl.exe /Zs that #pragma pack() resets to the /Zp value. Promoted crates/slate/tests/fixtures/msvc/x86_64/flag_msvc_pack.c (repr(C, packed(2))).
