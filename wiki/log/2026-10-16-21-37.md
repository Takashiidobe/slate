# Ignore gcc-dg split-5

_2026-10-16 21:37:52_

slate-2yro: split-5 failed intermittently under suite load (Rust exit by signal) and passed alone. It is not timing: down(1000) puts a 10000-byte buffer in every frame on 8192-byte __splitstack_makecontext stacks. The C oracle survives because clang's -fsplit-stack prologues allocate new segments; rustc has no split-stack support, so the translation runs off its context stack into adjacent memory, and whether that crashes depends on the mapping layout.

- Moved tests/fixtures.gcc-dg/split-5.c to the new tests/fixtures.gcc-dg.ignored/ (not collected; *.ignored holds features Slate will never support, per wiki/concepts/differential-fixtures.md).
- split-4 stays: its stack use is small enough not to need segment growth.
