# slate-p58o.3.3: division and remainder semantics

_2026-09-30 21:41:56_

Closed slate-p58o.3.3 (Lower division and remainder semantics) with no
lowering change.

Every ArithSema::Division the IR produces is by_zero=ub and (signed)
min_by_neg_one=ub, even under -fwrapv/-ftrapv (ir/operations.md). Rust's
plain / and % truncate toward zero and give the remainder the dividend's
sign, as C does, and panic only on the UB cases. So the existing generic
Arith lowering is already correct. UbPolicy has only Undefined, so there
is no other contract to handle yet.

div.c and modulo.c already passed. I added division_semantics.c as
edge-case coverage; it passed on first run, so it does not demonstrate a
fix. It covers sign combinations with the a == (a/b)*b + a%b identity,
INT_MIN/2, INT_MAX/-1, mixed signed/unsigned (-7/2u), LLONG_MIN, UINT64_MAX,
compound /= and %= on promoted narrow types including int8_t -128 /= -1,
and a division loop.
