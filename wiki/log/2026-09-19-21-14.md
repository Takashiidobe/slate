# Declared-entity acceptance sweep

_2026-09-19 21:14:03_

Closed epic slate-parser-8lv.

Audited every place sema builds an IR object declaration and every reader or
writer of an alignment request, and documented both greps with all survivors
in the new wiki/concepts/entity-model.md. Each declaration site derives its
properties from the entity's QualType and request; the rest are synthetic
objects (string-literal backing globals, atomic builtin trampolines, unnamed
prototype parameters) or are not declaration sites at all.

Three residues filed rather than left implicit: slate-parser-dev
(types.rs::resolve_parameters is a second copy of parameter lowering, outside
the entity table), slate-parser-eiq (ir::Field has no is_const, so a const
member is invisible in the dump although sema rejects assigning through it),
slate-parser-rjy (linkage, storage duration, definition state and symbol
attributes are merged in place on ir::Global, making module.globals a second
per-object store).

Tags deliberately are not in the entity table: they have no BindingId, so
TypeResolver is the tag entity table, with tag_names as a scope stack and
definitions holding the properties.

New fixture sema/ir_entity_sites.c is the acceptance matrix, every property at
every declaration site in one dump. Three cells cannot be filled: _Alignas on a
parameter is a constraint violation, a const field does not print, and a
compound literal prints as a place rather than a declaration.

Regenerating all fixtures produced zero diff outside the new one.
