# Merged functions and globals keep their definition's span

_2026-10-01 15:06:53_

slate-p58o.6.1. Directive translation needs each item's definition line to place it in its #if branch; it read that from c_ast under CIR.

ir::Function and ir::Global already sit in Span<_>, but sema's merge kept the first declaration's location: a prototype or extern ahead of the definition left the span outside the branch holding the definition.

- declare_function: a definition replaces the whole span but keeps the first NodeId, so metadata is unchanged.
- declare_global: the location moves to the declaration with the initializer, else the first non-extern one; definition is OR-merged (still recomputed from entities afterwards).
- --show-spans now also works with ir/--dump-ir and prints each global's and function's span. Expression and module printers share module_print::locations.
