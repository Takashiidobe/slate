# Compile fewer assignments in --merge by refining until conditions settle

_2026-10-31 08:22:09_

slate-nfq8.6: --merge compiled every assignment of each unit's relevant features. It now seeds with no features, each feature alone and all on, then repeats merge and compile until the conditions settle. New rows come from: another condition no more complex than the chosen one that fits the compiled rows, the smallest and largest assignment of each chosen term, all-on minus each positive literal, and item variants that overlap or leave a hole. --merge-full-table keeps the old behaviour. Targets now compile in one parallel job list.

Departure from the bead: the planner's cover is not compiled and there are no trace conditions. Trace conditions need Rust-to-C mapping and miss type-only dependencies. Fixed sets measured on zlib linux-x86_64 with --check-projection all: at most 1 feature plus all-on, 61 compiles, 2416 mismatches; at most 2, 111 compiles, 96 mismatches; at most 3, 155 compiles, 0 mismatches. Only 151 of the 186 programs were distinct, so compiling each variant cannot go below 151.

Also fixed: splice folded variants in mask order, so disjoint insertions from {a} and {b} could get an order the all-on variant could not align with, splitting one statement into odd conditions (flags += 1L<<17 gated on no-gzip and not no-gzcompress). Functions and records now fold the largest variant first. This changes merged output and simplifies it (splice_statements: value *= 2 is now unconditional).

Results, zlib: 105 compiles per target vs 186; 16 targets 1696 vs 2976, 61s vs 73s. Refined output is byte-identical to --merge-full-table on 16 targets. linux-x86_64 512/512 configurations 0 mismatches. validate_merge.py 78/78. Slate profile 30 passed. Fixture: fixtures.merge/cover_refine.c (type-only sizeof, nested, 3-way AND, OR of AND, whole-function variants; 27 of 32 per target, new // merge-features: directive); it caught a real miss before the witness rows were added.

Limits: this is inference, not proof. A condition more complex than the alternatives left open can be generalized wrongly; --check-projection is not on by default. Signature check and link_section protection only see compiled rows. Wall time fell much less than compile count (not profiled). Follow-up beads filed under slate-nfq8.
