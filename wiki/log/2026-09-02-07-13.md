# Generalize SingletonScopes to ScopeFlatten

_2026-09-02 07:13:12_

Replaced WhileLoopUnwrap/DoWhileLoopUnwrap/SingletonUnwrap (singleton_scopes.rs)
with a single ScopeFlatten rule. cir.scope always lowers to a plain Stmt::Scope
(control_flow.rs) regardless of contents -- a for-loop's induction-variable
scope is just one instance, not a special case. The three old rules only
covered: exactly-one-statement scopes, and scopes sitting adjacent to a loop's
negated-break guard inside a Loop body. Neither covered a multi-statement scope
wrapping a whole loop, e.g. array_init.c's `{ let mut i; i = 0; loop {...} }`.

ScopeFlatten splices a scope's children into whatever body Vec<NodeId> its
parent exposes via NodeKind::child_lists_mut() -- Loop, If, Match arms, Unsafe,
While, Block (the function root included, since it's itself a Block node).
This subsumes all three old rules as special cases, so they're deleted rather
than kept alongside it. Removed reparent_children (arena.rs), dead after this
change since SingletonUnwrap was its only caller.

Safety: no name-collision hazard (Rust `let` shadowing tolerates re-declaring
a flattened scope's locals), no Drop-order hazard (grepped: no `impl Drop`
anywhere in src/backend).

Verified: array_init.c's @rewrite FileCheck region regenerated via
tools/update_filecheck.py, now shows the loop directly in main() with no
wrapping scope. Full rewrites (92/92) and lowering (10/10) nextest profiles
pass; cargo fmt/clippy clean.
