# Complex integer statics, complex_to_imag, and long double library builtins; close slate-oreg.5

_2026-10-18 13:25:40_

slate-oreg.5: promoted gcc-torture 20041124-1, 20041201-1, 20050121-1, ieee__cdivchkld, plus fixtures.unsupported/gnu_integer_complex.c (passes on x86_64, i686, aarch64). New fixture x86_64/complex_long_double_builtins.c.

- Complex integer static initializers emitted num_complex operator calls (scalar + Complex), and those trait operators are not const (E0015). In a static initializer, complex add, sub, and real-operand mul/div now lower component by component via lower_componentwise_arith. The f80 complex path shares its mixed-operand cases.
- ConversionKind::ComplexToImag was unhandled (only ComplexToReal), so __imag of complex<f80> fell into the f80 conversion barrier. Both now read .re/.im.
- lower_long_double_bridge rejected __builtin_fmaxl because the callee contains __. It now bridges to FunctionName::library (the IR's c_builtin_library metadata, i.e. clang's Library builtin), so __builtin_X calls libm X.

slate profile green (the two ratchets cancelled by the gnu_integer_complex promotion were rerun alone).
