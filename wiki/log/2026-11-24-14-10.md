# slate-3amj.63 lower_wide_int_conversion: x87 _BitInt cast ignores upward rounding

_2026-11-24 14:10:08_

- bead: slate-3amj.63
- kind: bug
- area: crates/slate/src/frontend/lowerer/bitint.rs
- target: x86_64

Outcome: The unsupported ratchet covers dynamic rounding of a 65-bit integer to x87 long double.

The fixture casts 2^64 + 1 with -frounding-math after setting
FE_UPWARD and FE_DOWNWARD. The x87 significand has 64 bits.
Lean checks that the adjacent values are 2^64 and 2^64 + 2,
and that nearest-even selects the lower one.

Clang exits 0. Slate exits 1 because the upward conversion
returns 2^64. The typed IR carries rounding=environment.
lower_wide_int_conversion emits to_f80_bytes, which always
rounds to nearest-even.

Fixture: crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported/x86_64/long_double_bitint_dynamic_rounding.c
Limits: Clang i686 needs __floatuntixf to link this input. The fix remains open in slate-3amj.63.
See: slate-3amj.47 and slate-6uvv.3.
