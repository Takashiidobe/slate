# Type family touchpoints page

_2026-09-20 19:20:57_

Wrote wiki/concepts/type-family-touchpoints.md, the sema/IR counterpart to ast-enum-touchpoints.md, which stops at TypeSpecifier. Covers the five-layer chain (TypeSpecifier, CTypeKind, ir::Type, StorageLayout, the two printers), the six sites the compiler catches when a variant is added to ir::Type or CTypeKind, and the larger set that accepts a new family silently: types.rs::base, the convert.rs predicates behind is_arithmetic, arith.rs usual_real_type/arithmetic_component, layout.rs::numeric's int fallback, operand.rs binary_operand branch ordering, the numeric.rs emit_* family, and the second condition() in expression.rs. The caught-site list is empirical, from adding a throwaway variant to each enum and collecting E0004. Linked from wiki/index.md, back-linked from ast-enum-touchpoints.md, and paired in the AGENTS.md wiki list directly after the ast-enum-touchpoints bullet.
