# Align flexible array members and aligned externs of incomplete type

_2026-10-05 09:06:45_

Closed slate-parser-6x05.38.20 and slate-parser-cxg.20.7. The bead's 21 kernel TUs had two causes. (1) crypto/api.c: include/linux/crypto.h's __alignof__(tfm->__crt_ctx) of a flexible array member was rejected 'incomplete field type'; clang, gcc (and C2y alignof(int[])) give the element's alignment, and for a member the field's laid-out alignment (16 with aligned(16); a long long FAM is 4 on i686 in both compilers). The six sizeof/alignof layout sites (typer layout_query/operand_layout, lowering SizeOf/AlignOf type and expr, constant_value_with_context) now go through TypeResolver::measured_storage, which for alignof strips VLAs and incomplete arrays to the element (replacing typer::fixed_element); object_alignment still picks the member's alignment. sizeof of a FAM stays rejected. (2) lib/scatterlist.c: 'internal error: incomplete field type' with no location came from extern __typeof__(struct rq) runqueues __attribute__((aligned(64))) with struct rq never defined in the TU. object_alignment_override compared the request with the natural alignment, which an incomplete type lacks; it now returns the request as is. clang ignores a typedef's aligned on an incomplete type (LLVM IR align 1), gcc keeps it and takes the max. Lowering's finish_module errors now keep a location (SemaError::resolve_error), and the alignment error is located at its global (error.checked().at(loc)); checked() must not recurse into Located, since located lowering rejections (offsetof, MSVC asm) are real rejections. Fixtures: clang/linux/{x86_64,i686}/ir_alignof_flexible_array.c, {clang,gcc}/linux/x86_64/ir_incomplete_aligned_extern.c, values checked against clang -O1 IR. Both TUs lower. Filed .45 (i686 __alignof__ of a type gives ABI not preferred alignment, pre-existing) and .46 (gcc flavor rejects __alignof__ of an incomplete-typed object). Parser profile: 7755/7755 pass.
