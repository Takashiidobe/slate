# Darwin compiles as -fno-blocks

_2026-10-30 20:53:25_

slate-e36k.3: Darwin <stdlib.h> failed on block pointer declarators under __BLOCKS__, stopping 13 of 15 zlib sources. Removed __BLOCKS__ and __block from clang-22.1.8_{x86_64,aarch64}_apple_darwin.h, so Darwin parses as clang -fno-blocks and the SDK hides those declarations. Chosen over parsing '^' as a pointer because Slate can't lower blocks, and a call through a block lowered as a function pointer would silently be wrong. Source using blocks still fails at '^', as it did. Fixtures: clang/darwin/{x86_64,aarch64}/no_blocks.c, and nullability_headers.c now includes <stdlib.h>. Noted in generated-sources.md.
