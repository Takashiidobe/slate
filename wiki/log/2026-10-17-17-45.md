# Overflow and generic bit-count builtins on \_BitInt (slate-6uvv.4)

_2026-10-17 17:45:33_

**builtin\_{add,sub,mul}\_overflow with a \_BitInt (or **int128) operand/result of any width now lowers through a signed accumulator bitint::BInt wide enough for the exact result (2\*max(operand widths)+2, at least result+1): convert both operands, wrapping op, truncate to the result type (stored), convert back and compare. The <=64-bit i128 fast path also normalizes odd \_BitInt(N) results now.

Bit counts: the crate gains count_ones/leading_zeros/trailing_zeros (+ BInt::redundant_sign_bits). **builtin_popcountg/clzg/ctzg/ffs(g) work on small odd widths (unsigned view masked to N, leading_zeros minus the container slack) and wide types; **builtin_parityg and \_\_builtin_clrsbg are new. clang has no parityg/ffsg/clrsbg, so those are only exercised via gcc-dg.

\__builtin_elementwise_add_sat/sub_sat on scalar ints and \_BitInt use saturating_\* (small widths clamp to the N-bit range).

Fixtures: bitint*builtins (aarch64-listed), x86_64/int128_overflow. Promoted torture**bitint-41/91/92 and gcc-torture pr84169. Filed beads for constexpr reads in global initializers (bitint-38), **builtin_stdc*\* (bitint-75) and vector elementwise sat (saturating_arith).
