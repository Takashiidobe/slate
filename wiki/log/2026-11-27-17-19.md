Why each stays:
- return tail: returns are built at ~6 sites in lowerer/functions.rs plus statement lowering and macro bodies, so lowering would need a body-finalize step with the rule's `copy_type` check at each, and the rule must remain for the 6 returns exposed by UnreachableAfterReturn / dead-code rules. Lowerer grows, rule stays.
- flatten: deciding needs def-use across later siblings (`shadow_outlives_scope`) and drop guards (`guard_outlives_scope`); lowering has neither, and 80 fires are on scopes other rules create (it also runs in control_flow and unsafe registries).
- branch_init: spans two statements and needs "name untouched in earlier statements" over the arena; 1 fire on a rule-made shape (after slot_promote/decl_sink).

pattern_range: only switch lowering builds `Pattern::Or`, so `case_ranges::fold_case_runs` (crates/slate/src/frontend/lowerer/case_ranges.rs, the old rule's fold helpers, `MIN_RUN` 3) runs where switch arms are finished: the simple switch arms and the fallthrough dispatch `ExprMatchArm`s (from `switch_arm_pattern`, which already sees merged empty-arm values), and the case-loop arms after empty arms merge (folding before that merge would split runs). Output unchanged on switch_case_range_fold, switch_case_range_fold_wide, switch_case_u128_values, switch_dispatch_shapes, switch_empty_label_or_pattern. The fallthrough dispatch `match` (expression arms) is now folded too; the rule never visited it.
