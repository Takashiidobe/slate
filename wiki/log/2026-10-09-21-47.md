# x86 flag outputs =@cc<cond>: parser class and Slate setcc lowering

_2026-10-09 21:47:18_

slate-parser-asmcc + slate-3f8g.4.17.11.

Parser: =@cc<cond> on x86 resolves to AsmOperandClass::Flags(cond), printed cc(e). It used to misparse as letters and select {cx} (a silent miscompile). target::x86::FLAG_CONDITIONS lists the 28 conditions both clang and gcc accept; probing showed both reject pe/po, contrary to the bead's audit. The parser rejects, as both compilers do: @cc on an input, an unknown condition, and @cc with , alternatives. Permissive where they differ (+@cc, float, %0 reference: clang only). @ on non-x86 stays unresolved (AArch64 has its own flag outputs). Fixtures ir_asm_flag_outputs.c and error/clang/.../asm-flag-output-{input,unknown-condition,alternatives}.c.

Slate: a Flags output becomes out(reg_byte) into a u8 temp, with set<cond> {N} appended after the template, then asm_decode into the place. Read-write flags and template references are barriers. gnu_asm_flag_outputs.c is promoted, with a _Bool carry case added. It dropped its %V Intel-switch case (filed slate-3f8g.4.17.21; asm_dialect_switch.c keeps the coverage) and avoids =&@cc because gcc drops the setcc for it (gcc bug: reads a garbage register).
