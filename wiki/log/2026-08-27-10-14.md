# rewrite engine v2: slice 1 (arena+worklist core, singleton_scopes ported)

_2026-08-27 10:14:29_

Deleted src/backend/query/ (QueryRule/Matcher/EditSet/Plan/rules/\*) and
src/backend/salsa.rs wholesale per wiki/concepts/rewrite-engine-v2.md.
apply_with_logger's 65-stage sequence is gone; src/backend/mod.rs now just
calls the new src/backend/engine module.

New engine (src/backend/engine/): a per-function arena (arena.rs) with
generational NodeIds mirroring Stmt's shape but with Vec<IndentStmt> bodies
replaced by Vec<NodeId>, built/reified once per function; a worklist
(mod.rs) seeded by one traversal, popped in ascending NodeId order, tier-1
dispatch by node-kind via each rule's matches(), first matching rule (by
registration/priority order) applies and requeues itself + structural
parent. Edit-count safety valve (200k) replaces FixpointLimit::Rounds.

Ported query::rules::singleton_scopes (3 cases: while/do-while loop scope
unwrap, singleton scope unwrap) as the first NodeRule impls -- pure
structural, no facts needed, good first proof the worklist core actually
drives a rewrite.

facts/\*.rs and support/walk.rs/runtime.rs are now unreferenced: facts/ was
built against the deleted salsa FixupDb trait and nothing calls it yet
(dropped `mod facts;` from mod.rs, left the files on disk per the doc's
"port the logic later" -- they weren't removed, just unwired); support/ and
runtime.rs were only reachable from the now-deleted StringLibc pass, so
those got dropped as orphans (CLAUDE.md orphan-cleanup rule) rather than
kept half-wired.

trace.rs trimmed to just the Pass enum (kept for SkipSet/CLI/`fixup-debug`
--pass-name compatibility); the CollectingLogger/TraceLog/RewriteEvent
tracing machinery was only consumed by the deleted query rule bodies and is
gone. debug_with() no longer produces a real trace -- it's a placeholder
(apply + emit) until the new engine grows its own logger.

Validated: cargo build/test/clippy/fmt clean. All three nextest profiles
green (rewrites 110/110, lowering 10/10, libc 19/19) -- differential
(stdout+exit code) correctness held with only 3 of the old ~65 rules
ported, confirming fixups are optional idiom polish over already-correct
baseline lowering, per CLAUDE.md. tests/differential.rs's ~25 fixup-debug/
trace-identity assertions were already `/* ... */`-commented out in a prior
commit (not something this session broke) -- not evaluated either way.

cache: binding decl/uses/values/effects -- the doc's other "structurally
different" suggested-first-slice rule), then work through passes.md's
