# Parse, check, and lower MS SEH statements

_2026-10-11 15:58:53_

slate-parser-v74.1.7: **try/**except/**finally/**leave are keywords under microsoft_extensions (msvc, clang on \*-windows-msvc, -fms-extensions).

AST: StmtKind::SehTry { body, handler: SehHandler::{Except { filter, body }, Finally(body)} } and StmtKind::SehLeave; every body must be a compound statement.
Checker (sema/assertion.rs), matching clang text and location: **leave needs an enclosing **try body (handlers do not count, so **leave in a nested **finally leaves the outer **try); filter must be integral; \_exception_code needs an **except filter or block, \_exception_info the filter. \_abnormal_termination is unchecked, as in clang.
IR: Statement::SehTry { id, body, handler } and SehLeave(id). The filter is an Evaluation like a loop condition, so its effects stay attached. \_exception_code/\_exception_info/\_abnormal_termination lower to ValueKind::Seh(SehIntrinsic), effect-free reads, not calls to a nonexistent symbol.
Also: builtin type msuint32_t resolves to unsigned long where long is 32 bits, else unsigned int (it was why \_exception_code was unsupported; also used by \_Interlocked\*). \_\_noop lowers to const<i32>(0) without evaluating its arguments, and rejects void arguments like clang.
Slate: no change; its catch-all makes a function with SEH a lowering barrier.

Gotcha: Checker::statement has a catch-all, so a new Stmt variant compiles but is never checked. Added to ast-enum-touchpoints.

Fixtures: clang/windows/x86_64/ir_seh.c, error/clang/windows/x86_64/seh_misuse.c, and 11 CodeGen**exceptions-seh\* suite fixtures migrated by llvm_lit_sweep.py (exceptions-seh and -nested-finally on both oracles; -finally and -leave clang only, since cl.exe rejects their **attribute**).
SQLite MSVC wal.c now gets past SEH_TRY and stops at **ATOMIC_RELAXED (slate-parser-7xh6).
Follow-up: slate-parser-v74.1.19 (goto into SEH scopes, -Wjump-seh-finally).
Validation: parser profile, 8142 tests: 8131 passed; the 11 failures were the new suite fixtures before their checks were generated. After update_filecheck, all 13 SEH fixtures pass.
