# Convert between \_BitInt and floating types (slate-6uvv.3)

_2026-10-17 17:37:01_

vendor/bitint gains const float conversions: to_f32/to_f64/from_f32/from_f64 (round-to-nearest-even via a 64-bit top word with a sticky bit, exact power-of-two scaling, truncation toward zero for float->int), and a generic to_ieee_bits/from_ieee_bits plus to_f80_bytes/from_f80_bytes for \_Float16, \_Float128 and x87 long double. Encoding rounds to nearest even and overflows to infinity.

The lowerer routes any Convert touching a wide \_BitInt ahead of the long-double shim path: f32/f64 use the direct methods, f16/f128 go through f16::from_bits/f128::from_bits, and f80 wraps LongDouble([u8; 10]) / reads .0. Small bit-precise types keep the native-container path.

New fixtures bitint_float and bitint_float_ext (aarch64-listed; bitint_float also i686-listed because the i686 C oracle lacks **floattidf). Promoted torture**bitint-3/4/21/23/24/25/87 and long_double (listed on i686/aarch64). Note: clang miscompiles (long double)(unsigned \_BitInt(16319)), printing a negative value, so the fixture avoids it. Filed a bead for torture\_\_bitint-22's long double global initializer.
