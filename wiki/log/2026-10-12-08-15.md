# Lower \_InterlockedCompareExchange128 and aarch64 \_Interlocked\*\_acq/\_rel/\_nf

_2026-10-12 08:15:04_

slate-parser-v74.1.20. The registry had no aarch64 table, so the aarch64-only MS intrinsics (\_InterlockedAdd[64], \_InterlockedAnd8/16_acq..., \_InterlockedCompareExchange128_acq, ...) were implicit external calls.

- generate*clang_builtins.py gained --table NAME=SOURCE.td and --prototype-prefix; sema/clang_aarch64_builtins.rs (CLANG_AARCH64_BUILTINS, AARCH64_PROTOTYPE*\*) is generated from BuiltinsAArch64.td in ~/llvm-work/agent-2/src. The main registry is not regenerated: no available clang-tblgen reproduces it (it carries ~150 newer entries). BuiltinAttribute gained RuntimeLibraryFunction.
- builtins.rs target_builtin returns the aarch64 table for TargetFamily::AArch64 (Arm32 still none).
- ms_expansion now trims trailing digits from the record (aarch64 records are per-width, e.g. InterlockedAnd8_acq) and maps InterlockedAdd to MsIntrinsic::Add (returns the new value) and InterlockedCompareExchange128 to MsIntrinsic::CompareExchange128.
- Lowerer::compare_exchange_128 (sema/atomic.rs): casts dest/comparand to unsigned \_\_int128 pointers, builds (high<<64)|low from zero-extended halves, emits a write_back compare-exchange (release fails relaxed), converts the bool to unsigned char.
- Fixtures: clang/windows/aarch64/ms_interlocked_ordered.c (all widths, \_acq/\_rel/\_nf, pointer, Add, 128) and x86_64/ms_interlocked_cmpxchg128.c. The clang -O0 IR has the same atomic op count (108) and the same ordering multiset.
- Not done: ms-intrinsics.c and arm64-microsoft-intrinsics.c were not migrated (llvm_lit_sweep finds no configurations for their i686--windows style triples); Slate's Rust lowering of u128 compare_exchange is unchecked. Parser 8247/8247.
