# static_assert in struct and union member lists (slate-parser-cxg.20.6)

_2026-10-03 18:03:10_

- C11 6.7.2.1 allows a static_assert-declaration as a struct-declaration; slate parsed every member as a field and failed with 'expected declaration type'. New FieldItemKind::StaticAssert (ast-spec already listed MemberItem::StaticAssert; ast-grammar updated).
- The member assertion is evaluated during record resolution, where the record is still incomplete, so sizeof of the enclosing struct is rejected as in clang. Checker::assertion's body moved to assertion::static_assertion_error(types, assertion) so TypeResolver can call it; failures go to TypeResolver::diagnostics and the checker loop promotes them to errors per item (works for local structs too).
- Name resolution, visit::walk_tag_definition, and reachability now walk the condition.
- Fixtures: static_assert_member.c (gcc, clang), error static_assert_member_failed.c.
- c2y-static-assert-2/3.c still fail on gcc's static assertions in expressions (filed cxg.20.12, gcc only).
