# Unnamed definition parameters

_2026-09-19 14:01:22_

slate-parser-lh7.2.16. sema/module.rs no longer errors with 'unnamed definition parameter': a definition parameter without a name gets a fresh binding and lowers as `%N <unnamed>: T`.

Gating: the issue asked for pre-C23 rejection, but clang 22 accepts `int f(int) {}` in c89..c17 with a default-on warning (-Wc23-extensions, ExtWarn), and gcc accepts it silently. Per the feature-availability rule, StandardFeatures gains `unnamed_definition_parameters` (Standard in c23/gnu23, Extension before), and validate.rs raises the new Warning::C23Extensions for each unnamed parameter of a definition while it is not Standard. It is in the pedantic group, so -pedantic-errors makes it an error; lowering is unaffected by severity.

Fixtures: sema/ir_unnamed_definition_parameters.c (c23/gnu23 IR), sema/unnamed_definition_parameter_warnings.c (c89/c17 warnings; named params and prototypes do not warn), error/unnamed_definition_parameter_pedantic_errors.c. wiki/concepts/diagnostic-severity.md lists the warning.
