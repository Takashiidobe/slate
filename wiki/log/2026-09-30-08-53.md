# pp: GNU and MSVC comma elision before __VA_ARGS__ (ryfo.8)

_2026-09-30 08:53:12_

slate-parser-ryfo.8. substitute_function_macro now drops the comma before __VA_ARGS__ per flavor (Arguments::elide_comma, from Preprocessor::comma_elision):

- gcc and clang: the comma of ', ## __VA_ARGS__' goes when the variadic argument is omitted (F(a)); an empty one (F(a,)) keeps it. For a macro whose only parameter is ..., H() counts as omitted in gnu modes and as one empty argument in iso modes (h(0) vs h(0,)), as both compilers do.
- msvc (traditional preprocessor, cl.exe's default): a replacement-list comma before any __VA_ARGS__ that is empty after expansion goes, with or without ##. Emptiness is checked on the expanded argument, so F(d, E) with an empty E elides too.
- Wiki: preprocessor.md Expansion.

Not covered: GNU named variadics (args...), where N(a) is still not invoked (slate-parser-cxg.7.3).
