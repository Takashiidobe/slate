# Use peek/peek_str idioms in prefix-length helpers

_2026-09-12 09:27:31_

string_prefix_len/char_prefix_len were still doing manual char_at(at)==Some(x) chains instead of the peek/peek_str/try_consume idioms already established. Since both were always called with at == self.pos (right at the top of scan_one, before pos moves), dropped the at parameter entirely and rewrote them against self.peek()/peek_str()/peek_at() -- both now delegate to one shared literal_prefix_len(quote: char) helper (parameterized on the closing quote char instead of duplicating string vs. char logic). Also replaced the redundant self.chars[i]/self.chars[i]=='u' rechecks in the string/char literal branches with the already-captured c variable. lexer.rs still compiles clean.
