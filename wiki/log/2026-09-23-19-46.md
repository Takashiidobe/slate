# MSVC corpus oracle

_2026-09-23 19:46:47_

Added an optional MSVC runner to tools/corpus_sweep.py. cl.exe uses /Zs and batches fixtures with matching flags (up to 32 per invocation); failed batches split to recover per-fixture results. A single-file --fixtures path supports quick checks. C89/C99/C23 modes map to C17 because this cl.exe supports C11/C17 only. MSVC totals, compiler acceptance differences, diagnostics, and version are included in reports. Verified with add.c (accepted) and gnu_auto_type.c (rejected); no full corpus run.
