# Checker owns expression operand checks

_2026-09-28 20:46:34_

slate-parser-cc94.5.5. The checker now calls TypeResolver::typed on every expression it walks and reports typer rejections (Rejected, InvalidOperands, InvalidOperand) once, at the innermost expression (TypeResolver::rejected_at).
The typer now rejects what lowering did: assignment/increment targets, & of bit-field/register/vector element/rvalue, members, -> on non-pointers, non-function callees, argument counts, subscripts and pointer arithmetic, non-scalar conditions, conditional mismatches, sizeof/alignof of incomplete types and bit-fields, bit casts, convertvector, va_arg and va_* builtins, custom and atomic builtin arity/operands.
Binary/unary arithmetic share numeric::binary_rule/unary_rule with emit_binary/emit_unary_arith (called through checked); atomic fetches share atomic::fetch_rule; AtomicBuiltin::operands drives object checks and recorded value conversions; asm operand rules are TypeResolver::asm_operand_rule; typeof of a bit-field is typeof_expression. record_conversion rejects differently sized vector casts.
Lowering copies in expression.rs/atomic.rs/asm.rs/numeric.rs are Internal. sizeof of a record whose definition failed is left to lowering so the definition error is not masked.
Too-strict fixes (clang+gcc verified): file-scope __func__/__FUNCTION__ are "" and __PRETTY_FUNCTION__ "top level"; a void conditional arm makes the result void (gcc-torture 20000211-1 now lowers); unary plus on vectors.
Fixtures: error/clang/.../expression_operand_rules.c (33 clang errors), clang/.../expression_operand_extensions.c. Follow-ups: cxg.46 (pre-existing too-strict operand rules), cc94.5.7 (int-conversion accepted by plain parse).
