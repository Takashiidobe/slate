# Carry typedef alignment through sema into IR layout

_2026-09-25 21:04:30_

Typedef aligned attributes were refused. CTypeKind::Typedef now carries alignment (from the typedef's aligned attributes); ir::Type stays structural. CTypes::typedef_alignment walks sugar and arrays, outermost aligned typedef wins, and returns None under _Atomic (clang lays _Atomic A out from the canonical type: _Alignof(_Atomic A) == 4 for A = int aligned(16)). TypeResolver::typedef_storage replaces natural alignment (it may lower it, e.g. aligned(1)); MSVC flavor still maxes with natural via declared_alignment. Applied at record field placement (layout_record takes field QualTypes), all four _Alignof paths, and global/local [align=N] via object_alignment_override, which feeds typedef alignment as the natural input to effective_alignment. Arrays whose element size is not a multiple of a typedef alignment are rejected, as in clang and gcc. _Alignas on a typedef is now Invalid (new attributes::Use::Invalid). Verified against clang -emit-llvm and -fdump-record-layouts: new fixture typedef_alignment.c plus 8 repros now lower (align-1, 20050215-1, pr52419, pr57748-1..4, attribute-expressions); layout-attributes.c now stops at mode(QI) (dyd.31). Access alignment through pointers is not representable in IR (no load/store alignment); filed as a follow-up.
