# Model x86 APX flags and accept -mvzeroupper

_2026-11-08 20:45:43_

slate-parser-6x05.36: -mapxf, -mno-apxf, -m[no-]apx-features=, -mapx-inline-asm-use-gpr32 and -m[no-]vzeroupper were rejected as unknown options in gcc and clang flavors. APX is modeled by hand in target/x86_isa.rs (ApxFlag parsed in compiler_args.rs, Apx resolved in X86Isa) since its features have no -m flags of their own and are absent from the generated tables. clang: nine features (egpr push2pop2 ppx ndd ccmp nf cf zu jmpabs) with their own macros, folded in order; -mapxf toggles all but cf; __APX_F__ when those eight are on; -march=diamondrapids seeds them from its generated CPU macros, which are now filtered out and re-derived so -mno-apxf removes them; __APX_INLINE_ASM_USE_GPR32__ needs EGPR. gcc: __APX_F__ follows the last -m[no-]apxf only, -mapx-features= is validated (none/all, no cf/jmpabs) but defines nothing, the gpr32 macro needs no EGPR and has a -mno- form. Positive forms error on i686 like both oracles. -mvzeroupper is a GNU codegen-only flag. Checked 76 flag combinations against gcc 16 and clang 23 (macros and accept/reject), and x86_isa_diff.py (596 cases, 0 mismatches). Fixtures: {clang,gcc}/linux/x86_64/x86_isa_apx.c, error/clang/linux/i686/x86_isa_apx_32bit.c. Wiki: compiler-flags.
