# Diagnose extra ';' in struct/union bodies

_2026-10-24 19:23:16_

slate-parser-6x05.38.26: a stray ';' in a record body (accepted for gcc/clang since .38.11) is now recorded as FieldItemKind::ExtraSemi (ast.rs), one per token, from parse_field_list (parser/declarator.rs). New Warning::ExtraSemi ('extra-semi', default off, pedantic) in diagnostics.rs. check_extra_semicolons (sema/validate.rs) walks every TranslationUnit tag, so nested and block-scope records are covered, and sorts the results by location (tag ids are in completion order, inner first). clang: 'extra ';' inside a struct/union', one per run of ';' (comments between are transparent); gcc: 'extra semicolon in struct or union specified', one per ';', filed under extra-semi rather than -Wpedantic. Verified against clang 22.1.8 / gcc 16.2.1 for counts, columns and order; silent in system headers like other sema warnings.
