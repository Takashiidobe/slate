# Lower GNU vector comparisons; close slate-oreg.1

_2026-10-18 12:59:39_

Created P0 epic slate-oreg for the 41 remaining gcc-torture unsupported cases, split into 10 root-cause sub-epics (slate-oreg.1-.10). Deleted the stale TRIAGE.md and TRIAGE.log.gz; the beads now carry the case mapping.

slate-oreg.1 (9 cases promoted: ieee__fp-cmp-cond-1, pr105613, pr109040, pr110817-1/2/3, pr71626-1/2, pr123625):
- Vector Compare was an unconditional barrier. lower_vector_compare binds each operand with its vector type (so splats keep their lane count), converts to arrays, and builds the result with std::array::from_fn producing -(cmp as lane), C's all-ones/zero mask. Works for std::simd and __SlateVector.
- Function pointers whose signature has vectors now lower with Abi::Rust (variadic stays a barrier), and address-taken vector functions are kept off the C-unwind ABI. Vector C ABI externs and exports remain barriers, so Rust-ABI pointers never reach C.
- Lane reads/writes on goto-dispatch slot bindings go through read_unaligned on the slot pointer, avoiding rustc's implicit autoref through a raw pointer.

slate profile green.
