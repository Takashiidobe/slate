# Honor declared and x86-64 large-array alignment for globals and locals

_2026-10-02 21:05:02_

slate-dghn.17. redis ./runtest crashed in crccombine.c: static uint64_t combine_cache[64][64] is read through 16-byte vector loads, and slate lowered it 8-aligned.

- Tables::over_alignment(ty, alignment) no longer exempts the x86-64 large-array ABI alignment (arrays of 16+ bytes are 16-aligned; clang relies on it). lower_static drops the matching exemption from its barrier check.
- Locals: Let lowering wraps a variable whose alignment exceeds the type's natural alignment in __SlateAlignN<T> (init wrapped too) and records it in FunctionLowerer.aligned_locals; lower_binding projects .0 for those, as for statics.
- Dispatch slots: graph.locals carries the variable's alignment; the MaybeUninit storage holds __SlateAlignN<T> and the slot pointer is cast to *mut T.
- Shared helpers align_wrapped / align_wrap in globals.rs.
