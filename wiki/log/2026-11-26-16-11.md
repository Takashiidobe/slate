# slate-3amj.5.26 Dialect: thread /Za through ms_extensions

_2026-11-26 16:11:46_

- area: crates/slate-parser/src/pp/mod.rs

Outcome: MSVC /Za reaches the lexer, parser and sema configuration through `StandardFeatures::ms_extensions`.

Before: `MicrosoftFlags::strict_ansi` controlled predefines, and the shared `microsoft_extensions` feature was always on for MSVC.

After: `StandardFeatures::for_compiler` resolves `ms_extensions` from the MSVC flavor and `strict_ansi`. Default MSVC and /Ze enable it. /Za disables it. `Preprocessor::msvc_option_predefines` uses it to remove `_MSC_EXTENSIONS`. Shared Microsoft keyword and builtin feature gates retain their existing defaults.

Fixtures: `msvc/windows/{x86_64,i686,aarch64}/strict_ansi.c` cover /Za predefines. `msvc/windows/x86_64/stdc_macro_default.c` covers the default.

Limits: /Za diagnostics are tracked in slate-3amj.5.26.1 through slate-3amj.5.26.6.
