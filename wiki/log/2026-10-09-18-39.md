# #if accepts the comma operator

_2026-10-09 18:39:49_

slate-parser-cxg.7.4. #if 1 ? 2 : 3 , 0 and #if 2 , 3 (a comma kept by , ## __VA_ARGS__) failed with 'unexpected tokens after expression'. gcc and clang accept the comma in #if and warn only under -pedantic.

The #if entry point, Parser::evaluate_with_defined in const_expr.rs, parsed from parse_conditional, so a top-level comma ended the expression. It now parses from parse_comma. evaluate_wide and pp_unsigned already handled ExprKind::Comma (value and signedness come from the right operand). Assignment still parses at that level but evaluates to NotConstant as before.

Checked against gcc -E: #if 0, 1 and #if 1 ? 0 : 1, 2 are true, #if (1, 0) is false, and a comma under a false && is not evaluated. gcc.dg/cpp/vararg3.c, vararg4.c and if-oppr2.c pass. Migrated vararg3.c to suites/gcc-dg/gcc/linux/x86_64/cpp__vararg3.c (-std=c99). The parser profile passes: 7967/7967.
