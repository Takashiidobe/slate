# _Decimal64x type and D64x suffix (cxg.8.2)

_2026-10-10 18:47:35_

slate-parser-cxg.8.2: _Decimal64x is a distinct type with decimal128 format. New Keyword::Decimal64x (gated like the other _Decimal keywords), FloatingType::Decimal64x, FloatKind::Decimal64x erasing to d128, and FloatSuffix::DecimalF64x for the d64x/D64x literal suffix (value stored as Decimal128). In float_rank Decimal64x ties like _Float64x, so it loses to _Decimal128 and beats _Decimal64 by format width, which is what the gcc tests expect.

Also added __builtin_{inf,nan,nans,fabs}d64x and fabsd32/64/128 to GCC_DECIMAL_BUILTINS, and __has_builtin reports them for gcc. The __DEC64X_* predefines and float.h macros already came from the gcc snapshot. Fixture gcc/linux/x86_64/decimal64x_type.c plus six migrated gcc-dg dfp/*decimal64x* files. Parser profile 8129 passed.
