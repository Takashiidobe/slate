# slate-dghn.53 sema vector builtins: pmuludq, pshufd and select* lane selects expanded for xxHash

_2026-11-13 17:12:34_

- bead: slate-dghn.53
- kind: feature
- area: crates/slate-parser/src/sema/vector_builtins.rs
- area: crates/slate/src/frontend/lowerer/vectors.rs
- area: tools/corpus/e2e.py

Outcome: `python3 tools/corpus/e2e.py xxHash --mode clang` translates `libxxhash` and `xxhsum` with 0 barriers, and the translated `xxhsum` matches native on 53 differential hashes and a 12-case checksum suite.

Probe 2026-10-05 had 94 barriers; after slate-dghn.33/.32/.34 the remaining 20 were x86 SIMD builtins in `xxhash.c` and `xxh_x86dispatch.c` (the corpus build uses `DISPATCH=ON`, so SSE2, AVX2 and AVX-512 bodies all translate).

Gap: clang expands `__builtin_ia32_pmuludq*`/`pmuldq*`, `pshufd*` and the `select{b,w,d,q,ps,pd}_*` family by hand, and sema had no expansion, so each stayed a vector-C-ABI extern (`function __builtin_ia32_pmuludq128: vector C ABI`).

After (slate-parser `sema/vector_builtins.rs`, `sema/builtins.rs`):
- `Expansion::WidenMultiply { signed }`: bit-cast to 64-bit lanes, shift left then right by 32 (zero or sign fill), wrapping multiply.
- `Expansion::ShuffleDwords`: constant-immediate `Shuffle` repeating the immediate per 128-bit group; non-constant immediate stays a call.
- `Expansion::LaneSelect`: `intrinsic<T, slate.vector_select>(mask, a, b)`; Slate lowers it in `lowerer/vectors.rs` (`lower_vector_select`) to `std::simd::Select::select(Mask::<iN, L>::from_bitmask(mask), a, b)`. This is the runtime-mask select that slate-dghn.15 describes, for the integer and float lane forms.

Recipe (`tools/corpus/e2e.py`): `CMakeLibrary(library="libxxhash.so", tools=["xxhsum"], translate_tests=True, suite=xxhsum_suite)` plus four `-H#` differentials. `xxhsum_suite` hashes edge-size inputs with `-H0..-H3` in plain, `--tag` and `--little-endian` formats, checks each checksum file with both binaries (not `--little-endian`, which native cannot `-c`), compares stdin hashing, and compares `-b -i1` exit codes.

Fixtures: `crates/slate/tests/fixtures/x86_64/x86_widening_multiply.c`, `x86_dword_shuffle.c`, `x86_masked_lane_select.c` (runs only with avx512vl and avx512bw), and the parser IR fixture `ir_vector_builtin_expansion.c`.
Limits: `select*` on `__SlateVector` (over-aligned or wide) vectors is unsupported; half and bf16 `select` forms and scalar `selects*` are not expanded; `pshufhw`/`pshuflw` are not expanded; the xxhsum benchmark is compared by exit code only, not timed.
See: slate-dghn.14, slate-dghn.15
