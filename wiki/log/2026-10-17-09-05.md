# Lower record va_arg on i386 and AAPCS64, and char* va_list on i686

_2026-10-17 09:05:10_

slate-kih1.2.
- slate-parser va_passing now covers X86Cdecl (every record = memory) and Aapcs64: HFA -> new VaPassing::Homogeneous{count,width} (printed hfa=NxW), >16-byte non-HFA -> new VaPassing::Indirect (printed indirect), else regs=[int; ceil(size/8)]. Records containing vectors stay Scalar (barrier) off SysV64.
- slate: __slate_i386_va::arg (4-byte slots on the char* cursor) and __slate_aapcs_va::{gp, hfa, indirect}, mirroring clang EmitAAPCSVAArg (gr/vr offset stored before the stack fallback, 16-byte alignment for over-aligned types, HFA members from 16-byte vector slots). VaList is repr(transparent) over the C va_list on both.
- i686 va_list is ptr<i8> in the IR (C-accurate); va_start now transmutes VaList to the place's lowered type, and scalar va_arg on a pointer list goes through __slate_i386_va::arg instead of next_arg. This fixed i686 variadics generally.
- New target-independent fixture va_arg_records; parser FileChecks clang/linux/{aarch64,i686}/va_arg_passing.c.
- i686 list: removed c89, mem2reg_va_list_ssa, va_arg_function_pointer, va_list_forwarding, va_list_vfprintf, varargs, variadic_definitions, variadic_function_pointers.
