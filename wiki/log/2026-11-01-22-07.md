# vg clobber barrier honors -march sve

_2026-11-01 22:07:01_

slate-3f8g.4.32 follow-up: the vg clobber barrier also fires when the module ISA (tables.target.isa, resolved from -march) enables Sve/Sve2/Sve2p1, not just per-function target attributes. Checked by hand: -march=armv8-a+sve gives 'vg clobber in an SVE function', plain gives a dropped clobber. The parser ISA model has no SME feature, so -march=...+sme is not seen.
