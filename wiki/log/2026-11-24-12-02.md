# slate-e36k.21 lower_call: scalar and vector funnel shift builtins have no lowering

_2026-11-24 12:02:45_

- bead: slate-e36k.21
- kind: bug
- area: crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported

Outcome: The unsupported ratchet covers elementwise funnel shifts across odd scalar widths and vector lanes.

Lean checks both result halves by concatenating the operands and
reducing the shift count modulo the operand width. The fixture
includes a 17-bit count of 18, independent vector counts of 0, 1,
32 and 33, and a 65-bit count of 2^64. The 65-bit case runs on
64-bit targets because Clang i686 otherwise needs __umodti3 at link.

Clang exits 0 on all four Linux targets. Slate reports missing
lowering for fshl/fshr on x86_64, i686 and aarch64. On armv7,
the vector C ABI check blocks translation earlier.

Fixture: crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported/builtin_elementwise_funnel_shift.c
Limits: Funnel shift lowering remains open in slate-e36k.21.
