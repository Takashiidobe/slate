# Delegate target-only libc constants to bits headers

_2026-08-31 17:59:22_

Moved Darwin feature-selection macros from public features.h to bits/darwin/features.h. Moved the Darwin, FreeBSD, and Bionic nl_langinfo constant tables to platform bits headers, and moved the Darwin and FreeBSD pathconf/sysconf constant tables to platform unistd bits headers. Public headers now expose the target dispatch without carrying hundreds of target-only definitions. All new bits headers have direct-inclusion guards. cargo fmt, Clippy with warnings denied, and the release libc nextest profile pass (32/32).
