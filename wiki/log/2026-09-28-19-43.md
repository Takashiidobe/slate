# Checker classifies assignment, argument, return, cast and initializer conversions

_2026-09-28 19:43:56_

slate-parser-cc94.5.1 (first child of cc94.5; cc94.5 split into .5.1-.5.6).

The static-assertion checker records a Conversion (CastKind + warning) per operand NodeId in TypeResolver::conversions at simple assignment, call arguments (prototyped and default-promoted varargs, via TypeResolver::argument_signature), return, explicit casts and a declaration's top-level non-array initializer. Ill-formed conversions are SemaErrors from analyze, so plain parse now rejects what only --dump-ir rejected (fixture error/clang/linux/x86_64/checker_conversions.c; matches clang's five errors). Lowering's convert_recorded consumes the record at those sites; a missing record is Internal. Braced-initializer, builtin/atomic and usual-arithmetic conversions still classify in lowering (cc94.5.4/.5.5).

Measured with a temporary cross-check log (lowering classified and compared to the record): 262 missing records, 0 kind mismatches at first; driven to 0/0 before switching. Fixes on the way:
- checker declares VLA objects/parameters/casts with provisional_extents; VLA typedefs get a checker-only alias (declare_provisional_alias, no TypeId, no memo)
- checker records linkage (builtin_callee depends on it: strlen redeclared with size_t typedef), keeps array-parameter qualifiers, declares implicit functions (CTypes::implicit_function, shared with lowering) and extern void objects
- checker walks case labels, asm operands, declarator array sizes, typeof operands
- typer: pointer arithmetic no longer accepts nullptr_t operands; call_signature falls through to the declared callee when a builtin has no signature, matching lowering
- same-type pointer-to-VM conversions classify as Pointer (extent bindings differ only in lowering); emit_cast drops such a cast when IR types are equal

Fixtures: transparent_union_call.c now fails in plain parse too (existing too-strict bug, cxg.22; clang and gcc accept); ir_nullptr_invalid.c FROM_POINTER points at the returned operand like clang. Samples cxg.15/.31/.42, v74.1.18 unchanged (separate bugs). Wiki: c-type-layer, entity-model. Gate 7276/7276.
