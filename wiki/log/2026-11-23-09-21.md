# slate-5q63.25.6 crate_wide_allow: reject lint allowances at the crate root

_2026-11-23 09:21:32_

- bead: slate-5q63.25.6
- kind: feature
- area: crates/slate-lints

Outcome: Dylint rejects crate-root `#![allow(...)]` by default through `slate::crate_wide_allow`.

## Gap

A crate-root allow can override a lint denied on the command line.

## Before

`#![allow(dead_code)]` at the crate root passed without a Slate lint diagnostic.

## After

The same attribute produces a `slate::crate_wide_allow` error. The help recommends putting a reasoned `#[expect]` on the smallest item that needs it.

## Implementation

`CrateWideAllow::check_crate` checks expanded crate-root attributes, so active `cfg_attr` branches and allow lists with reasons are covered.

## Fixture

`crates/slate-lints/ui/crate_wide_allow.rs` exercises single and grouped lint allowances, active and inactive `cfg_attr` branches, and allowances scoped to an item or module.
