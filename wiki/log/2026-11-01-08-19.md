# Nest macro_rules calls when an inner template matches exactly

_2026-11-01 08:19:46_

slate-f2zu.9.5.10 (still open): macro_fold::nest_macro_calls replaces a statement window of a chosen template with a call to a macro named in that macro's #define (MacroRegion.mentions) when the callee template, locals renamed and holes filled, equals the window. Comparison runs on raw templates, then the outer template is renormalized. Rejected: list-shaped callee params, args reading names the callee binds, later statements reading those names, callee free names bound by the outer template. No definition reordering is needed: macro_rules names resolve at the expansion site, and each merge row is checked against its own inner macro. The bead's plan of nesting lowering regions does not work alone: pp/invocations.rs does not record calls spelled in a macro body and their tokens carry the outer site. Not done: send_code still inlines send_bits because (ush)(value) lowers to nothing for a ush argument, so the expansion differs from send_bits' int-argument template by a cast round trip whose safety needs the argument's type. A rule rerun on the expansion was tried and removed: sema elided the cast, and the rerun inlined temporaries the original kept. Macros reached only through another macro stay inline (new follow-up bead). Fixture: fixtures.merge/macro_nesting.c. zlib: 39745 to 39714 lines, send_bits! -> put_short! -> put_byte!. Validation: project_merge 5/5, validate_merge 78/78, projection 1024/0, signatures 676/0. slate, parser and flavor profiles not run because regions exist only under --merge.
