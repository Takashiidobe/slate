# Emulate cl.exe predefine-only options

_2026-10-25 14:33:46_

slate-3amj.5.11: msvc flavor now emulates cl.exe's predefine-only options, checked against cl 19.51 on x64, x86 and arm64 (750 random option sequences: 0 accept/reject or predefine diffs).
- CRT /MT /MTd /MD /MDd /LD /LDd (MicrosoftFlags::runtime_library + debug_dll): _DEBUG, _DLL, _MSVC_MD, drops _MSVC_MT. Last /M wins; /LDd adds _DEBUG until a later /M, and /MT /LDd keeps _MSVC_MT.
- /RTC[1suc]+ (__MSVC_RUNTIME_CHECKS, c adds _RTC_CONVERSION_CHECKS_ENABLED), /volatile:iso|ms (_ISO_VOLATILE; ms removes arm64's default), /sdl[-], /kernel, /fsanitize=address|kernel-address|fuzzer, /guard:cf[-], /Zl, /favor:blend|AMD64|INTEL64|ATOM (__ATOM__, INTEL64 _NO_PREFETCHW), /fpcvt:IA|BC (__FPCVT_IA__ 1/0). Predefines in pp/mod.rs msvc_option_predefines.
- /favor and /fpcvt are x86/x64 only; x86 rejects /favor:AMD64|INTEL64 even when overridden (check_msvc_target_options walks occurrences).
- check_msvc_conflicts replays argv for cl's D8016 pairs, eagerly as cl does: /RTC vs /O1 /O2 /Ox /Og (per /O letter, so /RTC1 /O2d fails, /O2d /RTC1 passes), /ZI vs /fsanitize=address and /guard:cf, address vs kernel-address, /kernel vs /GR, /fpcvt:IA vs BC. /kernel also limits the final /arch (x86 IA32, x64 up to SSE4.2).
- Removed the /MT and /favor: CODEGEN_ONLY_FLAGS rows; error fixture favor_intel64.c became a success fixture.
Fixtures: 31 under slate-parser msvc/windows/{x86_64,i686,aarch64}/ and 17 under error/, conditions generated from cl's own output. slate: fixtures.unsupported/msvc/flag_msvc_runtime_macros.c promoted to msvc/ (x86-only options split into msvc/x86_64/flag_msvc_favor_fpcvt.c). Wiki: compiler-flags.md.
Follow-up: /sdl also promotes C4146/C4996 etc. to errors (new bead).
