# slate-78pa.10 lower_call: typed overflow aliases have no lowering

_2026-11-24 11:42:14_

- bead: slate-78pa.10
- kind: bug
- area: crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported/gcc

Outcome: The GCC unsupported ratchet covers five typed overflow aliases.

The fixture exercises signed add, subtract and multiply and
unsigned add and subtract at the destination limits. Each operation
checks the overflow flag and the stored wrapped result. Lean
verifies the signed and unsigned 32-bit addition boundaries using
widened sums and a truncate-and-extend fit check.

GCC exits 0 on x86_64, i686 and aarch64. Slate emits ordinary
builtin calls where overflow IR is needed. lower_call reports a
missing lowering for each alias in isolation and the GCC triage
reports the first barrier on all three targets.

Fixture: crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported/gcc/builtin_typed_overflow_aliases.c
Limits: The typed aliases remain open in slate-78pa.10.
See: slate-dghn.34 covers the unsigned multiply aliases.
