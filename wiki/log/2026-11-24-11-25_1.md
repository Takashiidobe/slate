Outcome: The unsupported ratchet covers runtime memory-order operands.

__atomic_fetch_add and __atomic_load_n take an order function that
returns __ATOMIC_RELAXED and increments a counter. Clang and GCC
evaluate it once per builtin. The typed IR preserves each call as
atomic=dynamic. lower_atomic_update and lower_atomic_access require
a statically resolvable order, so both calls hit lowering barriers.
The differential check reports the update barrier on all four Linux targets.
