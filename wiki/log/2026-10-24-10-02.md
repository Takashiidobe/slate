# GCC optimize pragma updates __OPTIMIZE__

_2026-10-24 10:02:01_

slate-1vcv: #pragma GCC optimize with an O-level ("O2", "-Os", "s", "2", or number form 'optimize 2'; last wins) now redefines __OPTIMIZE__ (unset for O0) and __OPTIMIZE_SIZE__ (set only for Os/Oz/s), probed against gcc. __NO_INLINE__ is never changed by the pragma in gcc, so the bead's claim was wrong and slate leaves it alone. push_options/pop_options/reset_options save and restore the level. Command-line level now lives in CodegenOptions::optimization (new public Optimization enum in compiler_options.rs, moved from compiler_args.rs); PragmaMath (pp/math.rs) tracks current, pushed, and command-line levels and diffs optimization_macros alongside the math macros.
