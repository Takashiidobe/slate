# slate-3f8g.4.17.14.1 MS asm shell lowers on i686

_2026-10-12 08:49:14_

Slate now lowers Statement::Asm for TargetFamily::X86 (was X86_64 only): lower_asm and lower_asm_block accept is_x86(); on 32-bit the %V candidates drop r8-r15 and bx operands or clobbers are rejected. slate-parser's ms_asm.rs now sets operand.selected from the kind and alternative=Some(0) when operands exist, so Slate's 'no usable constraint alternative' check passes; 14 parser fixtures lost their [alternative=none] tag. Harness: -fms-extensions and -fasm-blocks are semantic dg flags. Fixture i686/ms_asm_registers.c covers a Symbol call, clobbers and push/pop; no nostack, default intel dialect, memory=Any. Clang rejects 'offset global' in -fasm-blocks on i686, so offset is not in the fixture. Memory operands, labels and implicit returns are .14.2-.14.5.
