# Target-aware FileCheck and per-target profiles

_2026-10-16 22:20:22_

generated_differential now runs once per target in SLATE_TEST_TARGETS (default x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu): tests/fixtures/*.c plus tests/fixtures/<arch>/. i686_differential is gone; i686 is a target. .config/nextest.toml enables setup-scripts and adds slate-i686, slate-aarch64 (qemu-aarch64-static, aarch64-linux-gnu-gcc) and slate-targets profiles. CrossTarget::for_triple maps triples to toolchains. FileCheck block headers list covered targets (// SLATE-FILECHECK-BEGIN <triple>...). A run for an unlisted target checks runtime parity only, so routine x86_64 regeneration never needs qemu. update_filecheck.py --target merges per-target outputs: identical lines get CHECK, differing lines the target's prefix; FileCheck variables are tracked per target. Header targets are kept by default. tests/fixtures.unsupported/<triple>.txt lists fixtures failing on that target, ratcheted. Moved 7 x86-only fixtures to fixtures/x86_64 and the 4 untranslatable aarch64 asm fixtures to fixtures.unsupported/aarch64. Guarded the long double shim's __int128 conversions with __SIZEOF_INT128__; without it one fixture broke the whole i686 batch. New plain_char_signedness.c covers x86_64 and aarch64. slate, slate-i686, slate-aarch64 green. Beads slate-5vj0.8-.11.
