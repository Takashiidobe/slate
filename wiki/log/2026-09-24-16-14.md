# Support x86_64 Apple Darwin sysroot

_2026-09-24 16:14:15_

Added x86_64-apple-darwin target info, the Clang x86_64 Darwin predefines snapshot, and a target fixture that includes Availability.h from the existing x86_64 Apple SDK and uses its MAC_OS_X_VERSION_10_15 macro.
