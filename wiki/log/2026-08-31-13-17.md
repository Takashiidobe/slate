# Track promoted va_list parameters as wrapper places

_2026-08-31 13:17:31_

Implemented slate-hos2.3. Function setup now emits parameters lowered to __SlateVaArgs as mutable and records their CIR IDs in va_places. Direct va_arg and va_copy consumers therefore operate on the wrapper value instead of dereferencing it, and mutable wrappers are excluded from immutable temp tracking. Added targeted lowering/rewrite and differential coverage in mem2reg_va_list_ssa.c. va-arg-20, stdarg-4, and va-arg-trap-1 pass individually. The full supported torture batch now reports only slate-hos2.4 computed-goto aborts.
