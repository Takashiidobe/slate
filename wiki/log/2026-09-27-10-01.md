# Per-target va_list kind

_2026-09-27 10:01:51_

slate-parser-v74.1.5.

On _-windows-msvc, clang's \_\_builtin_va_list is exactly char_
(CharPtrBuiltinVaList). The MSVC CRT declares typedef char\* va_list, and
clang's resource vadefs.h rewrites **crt_va_start to **builtin_va_start. So
in clang-cl mode every printf-family inline function in the UCRT failed IR
lowering with 'va builtin on non-va_list'.

TargetInfo::va_list_kind now mirrors clang's BuiltinVaListKind:

- CharPointer: Windows, i386, aarch64 Darwin.
- X86_64Sysv: other x86_64, 24/8.
- AArch64Aapcs: other aarch64, 32/8.
- ArmAapcs: armv7, pointer sized.
  CTypes::va_list_type(target) gives char* for CharPointer and CTypeKind::VaList
  otherwise. It is used for the **builtin_va_list specifier and for builtin
  prototypes that take a va_list. The va builtins and **builtin_va_arg accept a
  place whose lowered type is the target's va_list (TypeResolver::is_va_list),
  so on char* targets the IR has va_start/va_arg on a ptr<i8> place.

This also fixes va_list storage: it keyed on (family, Linux), so x86_64
Darwin, Android and FreeBSD got 8 instead of 24, and aarch64 Android and
FreeBSD got 8 instead of 32. All 12 targets now match clang's sizeof/\_Alignof.

Fixtures:

- sema/<each target>/va_list_layout.c.
- sema/x86_64-pc-windows-msvc/char_pointer_va_list.c.
- sysroot*crt_headers*{clang,msvc}.c now call snprintf.
