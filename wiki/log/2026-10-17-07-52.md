# Fold constant-string \_\_builtin_nan/nans calls to float bits (slate-mxxa)

_2026-10-17 07:52:20_

slate-parser now folds **builtin_nan{,f,l,f16,f128} and **builtin_nans{,f,l,f16,f128} calls whose argument is a plain string literal into Number::FloatBits constants (Lowerer::folded_nan in sema/expression.rs). Payload parses like clang: empty is 0, decimal, 0x hex or leading-0 octal, otherwise the call is kept. Bits come from rustc_apfloat qnan/snan, so nans keeps the quiet bit clear for f16/f32/f64/f80/f128. Static initializers stay constant and slate lowers them as from_bits without quieting. Promoted gcc-dg c23-float-4/5/6/7a/7b/7c and gcc-torture ieee**builtin-nan-1. torture**float128-extend-nan now stops at f128 (slate-kih1.1). Added fixtures builtin_nan_constants (slate) and builtin_nan_fold (parser, clang and gcc); regenerated 32 parser FileCheck fixtures (fewer builtin declarations shift ids) and two slate FileChecks.
