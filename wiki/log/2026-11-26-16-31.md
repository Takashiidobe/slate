# slate-3amj.5.26.4 dollars_in_identifiers: reject dollar signs with /Za

Outcome: MSVC /Za rejects dollar signs in identifiers that reach the parser.

Gap: cl.exe reports C2018 on leading and embedded dollar signs under /Za. Default mode accepts both. Preprocess-only output can still contain dollar signs, and stringizing a macro argument is allowed.

Before: `int em$bedded;` parsed successfully with /Za.

After: the parser points to the dollar sign under /Za. Default MSVC accepts the identifier, and /Za accepts dollar signs inside literals and comments.

Implementation: `StandardFeatures::dollars_in_identifiers` is off with /Za. The lexer keeps a standalone dollar token for preprocessing. `Parser::parse_input` rejects dollar tokens that survive expansion. The preprocessor diagnoses them in macro definitions and evaluated conditionals.

Fixtures: `error/msvc/windows/x86_64/za_dollar_{leading,embedded,paste,macro_name}.c`.
