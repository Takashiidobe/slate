# Codegen flags with predefine effects (6x05.38.3)

_2026-10-04 11:05:21_

Accepted the kernel's codegen and x86 feature flags in compiler_args, honoring only their macro effects; checked against clang 23 and gcc 16 (x86_64, i686, aarch64 cross, armv7 cross) with a per-flag -dM delta script.

- Policy (user): thread a flag only if it changes macros or the IR; otherwise parse and ignore. Backend-only validation is not emulated.
- PIC/PIE: last of -f[no-]{pic,PIC,pie,PIE} wins -> **pic**/**PIC**/**pie**/**PIE** (CodegenOptions::pic). Darwin forces PIC, windows-msvc ignores the flags. gcc aarch64 drops **AARCH64_CMODEL_SMALL** at PIC level 1.
- Stack protector: last wins; clang's plain -fstack-protector is max(on, snapshot default); per-flavor **SSP\*** values; -fstack-protector-explicit gcc only. Snapshot defaults read via Predefines::value.
- -fcf-protection: **CET** on x86 only; clang last wins, gcc ORs levels (none keeps check=8, gcc only).
- -mcmodel=: parsed (known model names) and ignored; **code*model*\*** stays the snapshot's small.
- x86: -mno-sse/-mno-sse2 no longer rejected. Disabled features' macros are removed even when baked into snapshots (Darwin/FreeBSD). gcc **MMX_WITH_SSE** needs SSE2; **FLT_EVAL_METHOD** 2/-1 (gcc x86*64), 0 (gcc i686 without x87), 2 (clang without SSE); clang drops \_\_FLT16*\*. -m[no-]80387 both, -m[no-]x87 clang, -mno-fp-ret-in-387 = clang -mno-x87 (X86IsaRequest::set_for_clang), gcc \_SOFT_FLOAT; -m[no-]3dnow[a] (gcc **3dNOW**/**3dNOW_A**). gcc may disable non-gcc features.
- clang flavor now defines **FLT_EVAL_METHOD** everywhere (builtin macro missing from -dM snapshots; cxg.32 noted, repro migration pending a gcc testsuite checkout).
- Filed 6x05.38.6: x87 excess precision not modeled in the IR.
- Fixtures: codegen_flag_macros.c (clang linux/darwin x86_64, gcc x86_64/i686), 7 error fixtures; removed two that pinned the old rejection of -fPIC and -mno-sse2.
- pp_diff keeps PIC/PIE, stack-protector, cf-protection and -mcmodel= flags. Sweep not rerun.
