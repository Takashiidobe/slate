Outcome: IR temporaries (`__v<n>`) with one nearby read are inlined during lowering from a slate-analysis fact (`UnitFacts::inline_temporary`); the engine's `EffectfulTempForward` rule is deleted and `inline_temps::late` fires 2025 -> 168 times over the fixture suite.

Before (C `total += va_arg(ap, int);`):
    let __v0: i32 = unsafe { ap.next_arg::<i32>() };
    total += __v0;
After:
    total += unsafe { ap.next_arg::<i32>() };

Rule (crates/slate-analysis/src/inline_temporaries.rs, on def_use::DefUse):
- Statement::Temporary with an initializer, not unsequenced, initializer.ty == ty, `single_read`, not captured by a VLA extent in a local type or a record field type (`def_use::Access::Capture`, `record_captures`).
- The read is in the own values of one of the next 32 siblings (or the list tail value); intervening siblings are added to a `Summary` and any control/opaque statement or a nested read rejects.
- `Summary { effects, memory, reads, writes }` conflict test between producer and (between + consumer-before-read). Calls/intrinsics/compare-exchange/atomic stores whose operands contain the read are not counted as effects before it (`effect_unless_ancestor`, `assign`).
- Effectful producers never move under `&&`/`||` rhs or `?:` arms; reads in macro args, statement expressions, or under `&` reject.
- Blocks: unordered functions (goto/labels), MacroExpansion/NestedFunction/OpenMP bodies.

Lowering: `lower_statement` records the initializer in `FunctionLowerer::inlined` and returns no statement; `lower_value_node` substitutes it at `Read{Binding}`. Emitting an empty block instead broke `structure_dispatch` (switch_torture fell back to a case-loop state machine).

Also fixed (regression from slate-8ge7.8): under `SLATE_CHECK_MACRO_TEMPLATES`, `lower_counted_for` lowered the fact's `start`/`end` with `macro_arg` holes left in, giving "macro argument hole outside its expansion" in merge fixtures macro_control_flow and macro_templates. It now strips macro structure from them in check mode.

Measured: checked `let __v` lines in fixtures 580 -> 235, no fixture gained one. Remaining `inline_temps::late` fires: 177 in goto/label fixtures (slate-8ge7.22), 4 elsewhere; lowerer `__t` temps are slate-8ge7.9.2.

Fixture: regenerated FileCheck across 126 slate fixtures and 4 merge fixtures; switch_torture keeps its tail-duplicated match.

Limits: lowerer-made `__t` temps and unordered functions still go through the engine rule.
