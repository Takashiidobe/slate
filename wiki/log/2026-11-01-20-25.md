# Report volatile access to misaligned packed fields as a barrier

_2026-11-01 20:25:25_

slate-yar4.7: write_volatile/read_volatile on a packed field at a misaligned offset is UB in Rust (debug builds abort) and no std API does an unaligned volatile access. FunctionLowerer::reject_unaligned_volatile (lowerer/places.rs) now returns Construct::Place for volatile reads and writes (values.rs, statements.rs) of a place whose type needs alignment > 1 and that sits in a packed record or has reduced alignment. One-byte fields are unaffected. Marked as never-supported: fixtures.ignored/volatile_packed_field.c, and gcc-torture 20010518-2 moved to fixtures.gcc-torture.ignored (it read a volatile long at offset 2 and only passed by x86 tolerance), each with a SLATE: note.
