# Lower \_Complex long double on x87 targets (slate-kih1.10)

_2026-10-17 09:56:55_

complex<f80> now lowers on x86_64 and i686 as num_complex::Complex<LongDouble>.
Complex is repr(C), so it matches the C shim struct \_\_slate_cf80 {f80 re, im} aligned 16.

- types.rs accepts Complex(F80) and sets dependencies.complex*long_double, which
  emits the \_\_slate_cf80*_ extern decls next to the \_*slate_f80*_ ones.
- complex.rs routes f80 complex ops through the C shims:
  - complex+complex add/sub/neg call \_\_slate_cf80_add/sub/neg.
  - mul/div call **slate_cf80_mul/div (Annex G via **mulxc3/**divxc3), or the new
    **slate_cf80_mul_basic/div_basic (naive formulas) under ComplexRange::Basic.
  - real/complex: wraps the real as (r, 0) and calls the div shim.
- Mixed real and complex add/sub/mul/div are componentwise over LongDouble ops,
  matching clang: r - z gives (r - z.re, -z.im), and z + r keeps z.im
  untouched, so -0 survives.
- conj (~z, \_\_builtin_conjl) negates im on a bound temp.
- ComplexConvert converts per component through convert*component (an as-cast,
  or an \_\_slate_f80_from*_/to\__ shim when either side is f80).
- Eq/ne use the derived PartialEq over LongDouble's eq shim.
- A global RealToComplex initializer is a constant when its operand is.
- cf80 export and function-pointer signatures remain barriers (unchanged).

Fixtures:

- aarch64/complex_long_double.c moved to the root and extended (mixed ops with -0,
  narrowing/widening, compound assignment, **real** through a pointer, a global,
  truthiness).
- c99 and complex_equality now pass on x86_64 and i686. The x86_64 unsupported list
  is deleted (empty).
- Promoted: chibicc complex; gcc-dg c23-imaginary-constants-1, c2y-complex-1,
  complex**builtin-complex-1, complex**pr47473; gcc-torture complex-6.
- float16_excess_precision FileCheck regenerated (temp renumbering only).

Validated: slate 16/16, slate-i686 2/2, slate-aarch64 2/2.
