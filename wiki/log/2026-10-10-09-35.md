# Support #import directive

_2026-10-10 09:35:35_

slate-parser-cxg.7.10: `#import` was an unsupported directive. It now follows gcc/clang semantics under both flavors: the header is entered only if no earlier #include/#import entered it (new `included_files` set keyed on the canonical path), and is then added to `pragma_once`, so a later #include is skipped even after its guard macro is #undef'd (gcc.dg/cpp/import1.c). MSVC's type-library #import stays unsupported. The deprecation/pedantic warnings are not emitted. Macro-expanded header names are shared with #include via `expanded_include_target`. Fixtures: {gcc,clang}/linux/x86_64/pp-import.c with sibling headers pp-import-guarded.h and pp-import-unguarded.h, covering import1.c and import2.c.
