# Model gcc x87 excess precision, -mfpmath and -ffloat-store

_2026-10-30 17:35:44_

slate-3amj.8: gcc -mfpmath=387|sse|both (and +/, spellings) and -ffloat-store are accepted (clang: 387 only without SSE, sse only with SSE, -ffloat-store ignored). X86Isa.fpmath drives __SSE_MATH__, __SSE2_MATH__, __FP_FAST_FMA*, __FLT_EVAL_METHOD__; an explicit -mfpmath keeps method 2 under -ffast-math.
Sema (numeric.rs X87Excess, also covers slate-parser-6x05.38.6's gcc half): float/double ops on x87 widen to f80 with reason=excess_precision, narrow at the end, and chain like _Float16. Applies to gcc i686 by default, x86_64 -mfpmath=387, -mno-sse, and double only for -mno-sse2. Comparisons, conditions, ?:, comma, neg and conversions to other types see the wide value; integer operands convert directly to f80. Standard mode (ISO default): casts round, literals parse as f80 then narrow (double rounding). Fast mode (GNU default): same-type and widening casts and (double)int keep excess, statement expressions keep it, literal operands fold in their own format unless trapping math must keep the exception; static initializers always fold. Fast + -ffloat-store: each operator rounds, double rounding stays.
Backend: float_fold folds FloatNarrow, statics fold narrowed f80 to float, int, and complex; lower_long_double_conversion marks the f80 shim dependency. That also promoted gcc-torture ieee__unsafe-fp-assoc and gcc-dg torture__bitint-21 (static narrowed long double constants).
Cost: gcc i686 float/double arithmetic now goes through f80 shim calls.
Not modeled: slate-3amj.49 (fast mode i686 st(0) returns, inlined builtins such as fabs), fast mode above -O0 (register allocation), clang -mno-sse (slate-parser-6x05.38.6).
Fixtures: slate gcc/x86_64/flag_mfpmath_387{,_standard,_fast,_gnu}.c, flag_float_store{,_standard}.c, flag_mno_sse2_excess.c, flag_mfpmath_{both,sse}.c, gcc/i686/x87_excess_{standard,fast}.c, flag_mfpmath_sse.c; parser gcc/linux/{x86_64,i686}/fpmath_macros.c, x86_64/x87_excess_precision.c, clang fpmath_ignored.c, error fpmath-*.c.
