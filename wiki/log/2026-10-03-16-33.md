# Dead-store drops inert uninit/zeroed initializers

_2026-10-03 16:33:05_

slate-dghn.19: DeadStore turned a dead let's initializer into an expression statement whenever expr_effects called it side-effecting. MaybeUninit::uninit(), unsafe { std::mem::zeroed() } and __SlateAlignN(zeroed) are calls, so they survived as bare statements and rustc failed with E0282 (the type came only from the let annotation).

Fix: dead_store.rs is_inert_init recognizes those shapes (through empty unsafe/plain blocks, and __SlateAlignN wrapping an inert value) and the rule deletes such lets outright. Kept local to DeadStore rather than marking the calls pure in call_effects: inline_temps would then move zeroed() into use sites, away from its type annotation, and hit the same E0282.
