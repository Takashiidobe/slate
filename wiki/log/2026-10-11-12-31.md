# Lower generic bit builtins clzg/ctzg/popcountg

_2026-10-11 12:31:30_

Slate now lowers __builtin_clzg, __builtin_ctzg and __builtin_popcountg (slate-yqfy).

- bit_count_method maps the generic forms to leading_zeros/trailing_zeros/count_ones; new FunctionLowerer::lower_bit_count applies the method at the operand's own type (u8 through u128).
- clzg/ctzg with a fallback bind the operand and the fallback to temps once each (operands are not hoisted: volatile reads and calls stay inline), then select the fallback when the operand is zero.
- Odd-width _BitInt operands stay a barrier; noted on slate-2o6o.16.
- The extern loop skips bit-count builtins: generic builtins get one IR declaration per operand type, which emitted duplicate extern fns.
- New fixture tests/fixtures/builtin_generic_bit_counts.c (all widths, zero fallbacks, fallback side effects).

slate profile 16/16.
