# Emit macro expansions and argument holes in the IR

_2026-11-01 11:12:13_

slate-f2zu.9.5.12.4: added Statement::MacroExpansion, ValueKind::MacroArg and PlaceKind::MacroArg. sema/macro_structure.rs decides eligibility from the AST and the cover table before lowering, using the preprocessor's labels on bindings and references for TEMP(temp + a); asm, labels, switch labels and static or extern declarations are also ineligible. Lowerer::statements opens regions by first statement id, Lowerer::expr and place add holes after lowering and before finish_expr, so conversions stay outside. Nested expansions nest, and an inner hole wraps the outer one. ir/macro_strip.rs strips rejected expansions.
Gating: Sema::with_macro_structure, on for --show-macros and for --merge's parse path, which strips everything until .5. Slate's lowerer uses wildcards, so exhaustive matches do not reach it as the ticket assumed; strip keeps holes away from it. Sema sniffers that mattered read through holes: bit-field truncation, is_constant, integer_fits, fold, object_size, sequencing roots.
Validated: zlib src and merge report byte-identical with structure on and stripped (trees.c alone has 107 wrappers, 781 holes). parser profile 8959/8959, project_merge 5/5. slate profile not run. Fixture: clang/linux/x86_64/macro_holes.c.
