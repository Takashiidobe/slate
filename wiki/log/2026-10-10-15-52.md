# Out-of-range #line numbers per flavor

_2026-10-10 15:52:00_

slate-parser-cxg.7.18: #line 18446744073709551616 failed with 'expected a digit sequence' because the line number was parsed as i64.

Oracle behaviour:
- gcc accumulates the digits in a u32 with wraparound and warns 'line number out of range'; 2^64 gives 0, 12312312312435 gives 2936042099.
- clang errors ('#line directive requires a positive integer argument') above 4294967295.
- cl rejects only a value that overflows 64 bits (C2177 constant too big) and wraps the rest to a signed 32-bit int, so __LINE__ can be negative (/EP prints -1358925197, -1, -2147483648).

Slate now parses #line digits per flavor in record_line_directive, with a new PPErrorKind::LineNumberOutOfRange for clang and msvc. A negative builtin expansion is split into a Minus token plus the magnitude pushed back onto the stream, since a single IntLit with a leading '-' lowered to 0.

Fixtures: pp_line_out_of_range.c for gcc, clang, msvc; error line-out-of-range.c for clang and msvc; gcc.dg cpp/line6.c migrated (gnu23). Parser profile 8042/8042. gcc's warning is not emitted.
