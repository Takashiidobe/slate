# Save and restore AArch64 x19/x29 asm clobbers

_2026-10-28 14:23:37_

slate-3f8g.4.27: clobbering x19 or x29/fp barriered with 'reserved register clobber' since Rust reserves them in asm!. Lowerer (lowerer/asm.rs lower_asm clobber loop): on aarch64 each x19/x29 clobber gets a non-late out(reg) _ scratch, and the template is wrapped in mov {N:x}, x19 / mov x19, {N:x} (nested for both). Non-late outputs never share a register with inputs, and Rust never allocates x19/x29. clang spills them to the stack the same way; gcc and clang both leave a clobbered x29 unrestored when it is the frame pointer (clang warns), so the fixture clobbers x29 only in leaf functions at -O0. x19/x29 operands and sp clobbers stay barriers. Fixture: tests/fixtures/aarch64/asm_aarch64_reserved_clobbers.c. Wiki ir/asm.md updated.
