# Parse #pragma omp parallel into AST and IR

_2026-10-26 11:28:20_

slate-3amj.24.2: parse #pragma omp parallel under -fopenmp and /openmp.
pp/mod.rs pragma_node macro-expands the operands of #pragma omp when OpenMP is on (gcc and clang both do).
Parser (parser/openmp.rs): a region pragma annotation in front of a statement becomes StmtKind::Omp { directive, body } (ParserInput::take_first_pragma, taken in one_stmt so if-bodies and labels work; nested directives nest). Clauses: private/firstprivate/shared (Identifier exprs), default(shared|none|private|firstprivate), num_threads, if([parallel:]). Missing/declaration body, duplicate unique clauses, unknown clauses and file-scope directives are parse errors. Other known directives: "OpenMP directive `#pragma omp X` is not supported yet" (simd stays ignored); unknown names are ignored for gcc, "expected an OpenMP directive" for clang/msvc. msvc rejects the if modifier and default(private|firstprivate), like cl.
Names (sema/names.rs): private/firstprivate variables get fresh bindings in a scope around the body (NameResolution.declarations keyed by the clause expr); every reference inside a region to an outer object/parameter is recorded per region in NameResolution.openmp_outer_references.
Checker (sema/assertion.rs): not-a-variable, duplicate data-sharing, const private, incomplete, VLA privatization (unsupported), num_threads integral (converted to unsigned int), if scalar, default(none) over outer references incl. globals and static locals; return/break/continue/named jumps/goto leaving or entering a region are "invalid branch to/from OpenMP structured block" (SehScope.openmp joins the goto scope paths).
IR: Statement::OmpParallel(OmpParallel { num_threads, condition, shared, private: Vec<OmpPrivate { original, copy: Variable, firstprivate }>, body }); shared = outer automatic locals/params referenced in the body. Operands hoisted before the region. Slate: lowering barrier until slate-3amj.24.3.
Fixtures: {gcc,clang}/linux/x86_64/openmp_parallel.c, msvc/windows/x86_64/openmp_parallel.c, 18 error/gcc, error/clang openmp-unknown-directive, error/msvc openmp-{if-modifier,default-private,unknown-directive}; regenerated error/{gcc,msvc} openmp-directive-unsupported.c.
