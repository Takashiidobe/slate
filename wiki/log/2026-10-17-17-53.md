# Lower wide _BitInt bit-fields (slate-6uvv.8)

_2026-10-17 17:53:53_

A bit-field unit that holds a bitint::BInt/BUint-backed member (N>64, or 128 off aarch64) cannot use the bitfields proc macro. lower_wide_bit_unit emits a plain Clone+Copy struct over [u8; size] plus an impl at the crate root with const fn __get_x/__set_x accessors that keep the macro's names, so every existing call site works. Members are read/written with new const bitint helpers: BInt/BUint::from_bit_range (sign-extends BInt into the padding) and write_bit_range, and load_bits/load_bits_signed/store_bits for the narrow members that share the unit. Method gains an is_const flag to emit const fn (needed for static initializers).

Bit-field assignments now bind a non-trivial value to a temp before calling the setter, which fixes two-phase-borrow errors such as r.0[5].__set_b(f(r.0.as_mut_ptr())). That regenerated bitfield_records' FileCheck.

New fixture bitint_bitfield (aarch64-listed). Promoted bitint-124 and torture__bitint-42/62/79/80/81.
