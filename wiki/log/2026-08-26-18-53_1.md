# cir.builtin_int_cast is OpenACC-only, out of scope (slate-3i0x.18)

_2026-08-26 18:53:54_

cir.builtin_int_cast's only producers are CIRGenOpenACC.cpp, CIRGenStmtOpenACC.cpp, and CIRGenOpenACCRecipe.cpp -- all inside ClangIR's OpenACC codegen, used for loop bound/index conversions within acc.* ops (parallel loop, reduction, etc). Confirmed -fopenacc is a supported SLATE_CLANG flag and pragma acc parses without error, but reaching this op meaningfully requires implementing OpenACC dialect support end to end (acc.parallel, acc.loop, reduction semantics) which has no general sequential-Rust translation and is an entirely separate initiative from ordinary scalar/vector op lowering. Matches the ticket's own anticipated 'out-of-scope dialect pipeline' fallback. Todo retained; no plan to add OpenACC support to Slate.
