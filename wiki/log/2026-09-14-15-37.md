# Control-body braces intentionally normalized

_2026-09-14 15:37:29_

Closed slate-parser-ixa.14 without implementation. Control-flow bodies remain Vec<Stmt>: an unbraced C body cannot directly introduce a declaration, nested compound scopes remain Block nodes, and Rust requires braces regardless. Null statement ';' and an empty body both normalize to Block([]). Updated ast-spec to record the normalization and removed brace preservation from the migration table.
