# Promote dispatch slots to plain locals

_2026-10-16 20:50:22_

slate-5vj0.5.5. Goto-dispatch functions spill every local to MaybeUninit storage plus a \*mut slot, and structure_goto left those slots behind after recovering loops. The bead's 'return slot' was really this pattern.

- Lowerer: dispatch storage is now named after the C binding (binding_name) instead of \_\_slate_storage_N, so a promoted slot keeps the source name.
- slot*promote::SlotPromote (prio 3): when a \_\_slate_slot* points at MaybeUninit<T> storage of the same T (no over-alignment), T is a prim/pointer/array of those, the storage is read only by the slot let, and every use of the slot is *slot or std::ptr::write(slot, v) as a statement, the storage becomes let mut x: T = zeroed(), *slot becomes x, writes become assignments, and the slot let is retired. Address-taking stays valid: addr_of_mut!(\*slot) becomes addr_of_mut!(x).
- slot_promote::DeclSink (prio 4): a zeroed let with exactly one assignment, all of whose readers are in later siblings of that assignment and neither write, borrow mutably (invariant_within) nor take its address, is declared at the assignment instead. inline_temps and compound_assign then fold slate-parser temporaries. It also folds C decl-then-assign in ordinary functions.
- return_cleanup::UnreachableAfterReturn: drops statements after a Return or a block/if that always returns, which removes the dead zeroed tail after dispatch bodies.

Records and over-aligned locals keep their slots. Fixture dispatch_slot_promote.c; 10 baselines regenerated. slate profile green.
