# Lower plain unions in the slate frontend

_2026-10-01 08:21:05_

Bead: slate-p58o.3.17 (closed). Also closed slate-p58o.3.10 and slate-p58o.3.14, whose named fixtures already passed raw lowering from earlier commits.

Change (crates/slate/src/slate_parser_frontend/lowerer):
- types.rs: build_record accepts RecordKind::Union, checks every field offset is 0 and size is the largest member rounded to align, and emits is_union (codegen prints #[repr(C)] union with Copy derives). record_fields now returns union fields; new Tables::is_union.
- places.rs: a Field projection whose base is a union is unsafe, so reads, writes, address-of, and array decay through union members get unsafe blocks.
- values.rs: union aggregates never take the struct-literal path; they zero the temp then assign the selected member, so bytes outside the active member are defined when another member is read.

Promoted: unions, union_init, union_array_member, uchar_conversions, long_double_union_representation, non_int_fields, anonymous_header_two_hop (+ its two headers), borrowck_hostile_aliasing, x86_64/long_double_memcpy, nested_anon_struct_fields, transparent_union_call, union_after_include, gcc-dg c23-empty-init-warn-17, and 30 gcc-torture cases.

Caveat: transparent_union_call passes only because the callee is static (Rust-to-Rust). Extern C ABI for transparent unions is filed as slate-p58o.4.8. Bit-field and packed unions still bail as record barriers (Phase 4).
