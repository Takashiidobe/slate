# Separate trusted declaration ownership from rewrites

_2026-09-01 21:05:22_

CallBinding now carries trusted header ownership independently of its optional Known rewrite identity.

The AST provenance parser preserves headers for every trusted direct declaration. Long-double ABI bridges transfer those headers into their C-shim declarations, where they cause the declaration header to be included and suppress a reconstructed prototype. Internal bits headers remain excluded from emitted include directives.

Extended the C23 library fixture with sqrtl, an uncatalogued trusted math declaration. The generated shim includes math.h, contains no synthetic sqrtl prototype, links, and passes its runtime test. Library FileCheck, focused rewrites nextest, cargo fmt, Clippy, and diff checks pass.
