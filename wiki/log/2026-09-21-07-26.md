# Complete GNU and complex numeric expression lowering (dyd.10)

_2026-09-21 07:26:26_

Four gaps the corpus sweep attributed to numeric lowering, all cases clang
accepts. Slate IR acceptance 2588/2689 -> 2617/2693; clang-accepted
slate-rejected 58 -> 35.

- **`wb`/`uwb` literals.** `bit_int_literal_width` in `sema/validate.rs`
  picks the narrowest `_BitInt(N)` per C23 6.4.4.1p6 (bit count for `uwb`,
  one more for `wb`, floored at 1 and 2). Used by both literal paths:
  `sema/operand.rs` for the C type, `sema/numeric.rs` for the IR type. The
  IR side must set `bit_precise: true` or every bit-precise literal picks up
  a spurious `reinterpret` from `i2` to `i2b`. 18 fixtures.
- **GNU `a ?: b`.** Lowered as `capture<%id>` around the conditional, with
  the test and the then-operand both reading `%id` — the existing VLA-extent
  let-binding node is exactly clang's `OpaqueValueExpr`, so no new IR
  variant. Everything after the binding is the ordinary conditional walk.
- **`~z` on a complex.** gcc and clang both make this conjugation; the
  `ArithSema` the `neg` arm already computed is right for it, so the fix was
  deleting the rejection. `_Imaginary` still rejects `~`, matching clang.
- **`sizeof` over members and conditionals.** `assertion_operand_type` had
  no `Member`, `Conditional`, or `AddrOf` arm, so `sizeof((c ? a : b).c)`
  fell through to the constant evaluator and failed on a non-constant
  identifier. Added a type-only field lookup (`field_of`) that walks
  anonymous members and carries the base's qualifiers.

`sema/unsupported.c` lost its BITINT expected-error configuration. Four
generated fixtures added under `tests/fixtures/sema/`.

Deliberately not covered, filed instead: `__auto_type` / C23 `auto`
(dyd.38), `__bf16` (dyd.39 — needs its own `ir::FloatType`, since aliasing
`F16` would make `widens_from` call bf16 -> f16 a widening), and the C99
condition-scope enum case (dyd.40). `__m128*` stays rejected; clang rejects
it too without the target headers.
