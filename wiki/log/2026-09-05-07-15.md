# honor gcc-torture dg-options/dg-additional-options

_2026-09-05 07:15:46_

The gcc-torture corpus keeps GCC's original DejaGnu directives verbatim, but
nothing parsed them: not filter-gcc-torture.sh, not gcc_torture_suite.rs.

Audited all four directive kinds across the corpus. dg-require-effective-target
(81 files) and nearly all dg-skip-if (29 files) are inert for us -- they gate
on other targets (avr, pdp11, msp430, freestanding) or conditions that are
trivially true on x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu. The one real category is
dg-options/dg-additional-options (~45 files): -fwrapv, -std=c89/c99,
-ffast-math, -fno-strict-aliasing, -mno-mmx, etc., silently dropped from both
the C reference compile and the CIR-emission clang invocation.

Added dg_option_flags() in tests/gcc_torture_suite.rs: parses the directive's
quoted flag string, applies a heuristic for any `{ target ... }` clause
(matches x86_64/i?86 globs, rejects ia32-only or bare keywords like `fpic`),
then filters through a small allowlist of flags that are both clang-accepted
and semantically real. Empirically verified against SLATE_CLANG which GCC-only
flags (-ftree-*, -fmodulo-sched, -mno-stv, -fno-tree-ccp, ...) clang's driver
hard-rejects -- those and all -O*/pass-toggle flags are excluded even when
accepted, to avoid changing optimization level or CIR shape.

Wired into run_cases via translate_with_args + RunConfig.c_args (both existed
already, just unused by this suite). Verified all 44 affected already-supported
fixtures still pass individually and the 1 affected unsupported fixture
(20040709-3) still fails for its original reason. slate-g23b.
