# Parse macro-expanded record fields

_2026-09-13 10:03:25_

Split record-field tokens at top-level semicolons before splitting each declaration's declarators at commas. This handles macros that expand to a complete field declaration ahead of another field on the same preprocessor node.\n\nPorted GCC torture fixtures 20040629-1 and 20040705-{1,2} with generated FileCheck coverage. Their check blocks are disabled in-source so recursive self-inclusion cannot parse expectations as comments.\n\nValidated cargo clippy --allow-dirty --fix, cargo fmt, and cargo nextest run.
