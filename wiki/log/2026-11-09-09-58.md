# msvc flavor: no GNU asm; asm/__asm__ are identifiers

_2026-11-09 09:58:25_

slate-parser-asmmsv: with --flavor=msvc, '__asm__(' / '__asm(' parsed as GNU asm. cl.exe (x64, x86, arm64) has no GNU asm: asm and __asm__ are ordinary identifiers (asm("nop") is an implicit call; operands fail with C2143 at ':'), and '__asm(' or '__asm volatile(' is an MS block: C4235 on x64/arm64, C2400 on x86.
New StandardFeatures::gnu_asm_keywords (false for msvc, standard_features.rs); parser/asm.rs is_asm_keyword now takes the features and never matches under msvc (statements, asm labels, file-scope asm, field check in decl.rs). parser/ms_asm.rs starts_ms_asm takes msvc: under msvc any __asm/_asm starts a block even before '(' or a qualifier, so x64/arm64 hit the existing 'not supported on this architecture' error and i686 reports 'expected instruction mnemonic'. clang/gcc unchanged.
Fixtures: msvc/windows/x86_64/gnu_asm_spellings_are_identifiers.c (cl-clean on all three arches), error/msvc/windows/x86_64/gnu-asm-operands.c, error/msvc/windows/{x86_64,aarch64}/ms-asm-paren-unsupported-architecture.c, error/msvc/windows/i686/ms-asm-paren.c. Regenerated suites/clang-test/msvc/windows/{x86_64,i686}/CodeGen__inline-asm-intel.c: its __asm__("...") statements are now implicit calls, as with cl. wiki inline-asm-parsing.md updated. parser and msvc profiles green.
