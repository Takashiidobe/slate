# Fold macros whose argument is used through different casts

_2026-10-31 22:38:31_

slate-f2zu.9.5.9: 12 of 14 send_bits sites in zlib trees.c stayed expanded because conversions from the macro body landed inside MacroArg, so one parameter had conflicting holes. Confirmed cause: conversion() in sema/numeric.rs copies the operand's span. Three changes. (1) sema/expression.rs: an explicit cast's conversions take the cast's location. (2) macro_fold.rs: candidate() moves casts around a parameter into its hole when all uses agree, because moving them out alone cut put_byte calls from 37 to 18 (a hole accepts any argument type, a template cast does not). (3) macro_regions.rs: merge_operator_spelled_in_argument makes one MacroArg of (int)count - 3, where the operator is in the argument's text; (a) + (a) in a body stays $a + $a. zlib: 40702 to about 40140 lines, send_bits calls 1 to 12, send_code 9 to 18; ush arguments stay inline since fold keeps one template per name. Fixture: fixtures.merge/macro_arg_casts.c. Parser profile passed. Slate profile's only failure was the new fixture overflowing i32 in its own C (UNHYGIENIC(wide)); fixed, and it passes alone. The full suite was not rerun after that one-line fixture edit, and validate_merge and check-projection on the regenerated zlib were not run.
