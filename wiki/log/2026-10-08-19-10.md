# C23 compound literal storage semantics

_2026-10-08 19:10:44_

Completed slate-parser-cxg.20.16.

C23 compound literals now lower constexpr, register, static, and thread_local.
The checker records storage duration and enforces scope and combination rules.
Register literals, members, and array elements cannot have their address taken.
Static/thread initializers require constants; constexpr rejects forbidden qualifiers.
Constexpr reads and scalar members use recorded initializer plans and values.
Inferred arrays retain const qualification; empty scalar initialization folds to zero.
Parameter-list literals retain automatic storage, including VLA bounds.
Each static/thread occurrence carries its own binding and explicit IR duration.
Promoted storage and GCC constexpr infinity/NaN fixtures to successful IR checks.
Added positive and negative GCC, Clang, and MSVC fixtures; updated IR/sema docs.

Validation: clippy fix, cargo fmt, wiki TOC/checks, GCC syntax oracle.
Release parser profile: all 7,953 tests passed.
Rust thread-local compound-literal consumption remains tracked by slate-pljq.
