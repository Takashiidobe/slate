# slate-78pa.11 GCC bit-field arithmetic: u64 addition and subtraction lose 33-bit wrap

_2026-11-24 11:49:14_

- bead: slate-78pa.11
- kind: bug
- area: crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported/gcc

Outcome: The GCC unsupported ratchet checks arithmetic at the limits of a 33-bit unsigned bit-field.

The fixture adds one to 0x1ffffffff and subtracts one from zero.
GCC gives zero and 0x1ffffffff. The typed IR uses add<u64> and
sub<u64> directly on the field read, and the Rust returns
0x200000000 and 0xffffffffffffffff.

Lean bv_decide found the full field as an addition counterexample.
Comparing 33-bit wrap then widening against widening then 64-bit wrap
also proved the only boundary disagreements for +/-1 in the model.
The GCC oracle exits 0 and Slate Rust exits 3 on x86_64, i686 and
aarch64 Linux.
