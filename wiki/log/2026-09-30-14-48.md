# Lower integer unary neg and not in slate frontend

_2026-09-30 14:48:11_

slate-p58o.3.4. Arith And/Or/Xor were already lowered; the real barrier in bitand/bitor/bitxor/bitnot was ValueKind::Unary, which also blocked every negative literal (neg<i32>(const 1)).

src/slate_parser_frontend/lowerer.rs now lowers Unary on integer types only: not -> !x (Rust bitwise not on ints), neg with overflow=ub -> -x, neg with overflow=wrap -> x.wrapping_neg() (unsigned negation arrives as wrap; Rust rejects unary minus on unsigned). Trap/other semantics and non-integer types stay barriers.

Promoted 16 fixtures (bitand, bitor, bitxor, bitnot, chars, conditionals, div, extern_decl, modulo, printf_integer_precision, printf_integer_widths, printf_narrow_length_modifiers, printf_narrow_length_modifiers_precision_alternate, return_cleanup, shorts, shr) and c-testsuite case_00133-00135. int128_arith stays unsupported: its barrier is conditional<i32> (slate-p58o.3.29). No FileCheck added per the unstable-frontend promotion gate. lowering profile green.
