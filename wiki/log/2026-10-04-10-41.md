# Language-semantic compiler flags (6x05.38.2)

_2026-10-04 10:41:15_

Emulated the kernel/musl/mimalloc language-semantic flags in compiler_args, all checked against clang 23 / gcc 16 -dM and syntax.
- Generic switches: Opt::switch names the positive spelling, parse_switch derives -fno-/-mno- and the -- alias, negation_accepted_by marks positive-only flavors (-fno-freestanding/-fno-hosted are gcc only). SWITCH_OPTS replaces FLAG_OPTS; CODEGEN_ONLY_FLAGS and CLANG_CODEGEN_ONLY_M_FLAGS hold full spellings. Removed the one-off hosted_flag.
- -nostdinc: drops compiler and sysroot headers; with -ffreestanding, removes the glibc stdc-predef.h macros baked into snapshots (__STDC_IEC_559__, __STDC_ISO_10646__, _STDC_PREDEF_H, ...).
- -ffreestanding/-fhosted: __STDC_HOSTED__ 0, library builtins off even after -fbuiltin; clang freestanding defaults -fno-asynchronous-unwind-tables.
- -f[no-]asynchronous-unwind-tables is an Opt now (it was wrongly codegen-only): drives __GCC_HAVE_DWARF2_CFI_ASM.
- -fno-builtin[-name]: CompilerOptions::library_builtins, checked in clang_builtin (now takes &Dialect); __builtin_ spellings unaffected.
- -f[un]signed-char, -f[no-]short-wchar: TargetInfo char/wchar overrides plus option_predefines in pp; -fno-short-wchar on windows-msvc gives int wchar_t like clang.
- -fms-anonymous-structs (clang): StandardFeatures::microsoft_anonymous_records, last of the ms-anonymous/ms-extensions/ms-compatibility flags; declarator-less record members become anonymous members.
- -fstrict-flex-arrays=0..3 (bare/-fno- gcc only) and -fexperimental-late-parse-attributes (clang) recorded with no consumer yet; counted_by args are never resolved (bead filed).
- strict_flex_arrays_rule is a separate validate_rules entry; nesting a pipeline doubled 'all rules failed:'.
- Fixtures: freestanding_library_builtins, char_wchar_flags (clang+gcc), fno_short_wchar (windows), ms_anonymous_structs (clang+msvc), late_parse_counted_by, six error fixtures.
- Sweep: musl and mimalloc pass with the flags kept; linux ok=54, now blocked on -mno-3dnow (2951, .3) and -m16 (54, .1).
- Wiki: compiler-flags, compiler-arg-rules, preprocessor, ir/builtins.
