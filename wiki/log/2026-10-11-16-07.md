# Resolve counted_by and sized_by counters against record members

_2026-10-11 16:07:48_

slate-parser-6x05.38.5. define_tag now checks the argument of counted_by and of clang's counted_by_or_null, sized_by, and sized_by_or_null (new Attribute::BoundedBy, parsed only under --flavor=clang) against the record's members, reached through anonymous members. Without late parsing only earlier members are visible; gcc and clang -fexperimental-late-parse-attributes see the whole record (matches gcc 16 and clang). An unnamed record that is embedded as an anonymous member defers its unresolved counters (pending_counters) to the enclosing record, which is how clang accepts a flex array in an anonymous struct counted by an outer member. A miss is 'unresolved member name'. Fixtures: clang counted_by_member_lookup.c (positive), error counted_by_later_member_without_late_parse.c and counted_by_unknown_member.c. Parser profile: 8145/8145.
