# Slate lowering barriers show enclosing context labels

_2026-10-01 07:47:06_

slate-p58o.14: lowering barriers and InvalidIr now carry a Vec<Context> of enclosing constructs, rendered as extra miette labels.

- Failure gained context + a used flag; boxed (Failure(Box<Failed>)) to keep Result small for clippy result_large_err.
- within(site, label) pushes an enclosing label: "in function `f`", "in global `g`", "in declaration of `f`", "in record `S`", "in switch".
- used_at(site) on statements: sets the site if unset, otherwise adds one "used here" label when the statement does not contain the inner site (e.g. a record defined elsewhere that fails when a statement uses it).
- Switch nested-label and fallthrough errors point at the offending arm's first/last statement.
- Global non-constant initializers are sited at the initializer value.
- Record failures are sited at the record definition and cached with their context.
- Driver: same-file contexts become labels spanning only the first line of the container (the prototype/declaration line); other-file contexts go into the help text as "label at path:l:c".
- into_public drops contexts equal to the primary site and dedups.

Not yet wired: parameter-level sites; extern/function parameter failures fall back to the function site.
