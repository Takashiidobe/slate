# Register clang x86 target builtins

_2026-10-01 14:13:48_

slate-parser-6x05.9: curl's __builtin_ia32_pause and redis's __rdtsc were unresolved under --flavor=clang on x86_64 because the builtin registry was generated from Builtins.td only.

- tools/generate_clang_builtins.py now dumps Builtins.td, BuiltinsX86.td, and BuiltinsX86_64.td and emits one table each (CLANG_BUILTINS, CLANG_X86_BUILTINS, CLANG_X86_64_BUILTINS) with a shared prototype pool.
- Generator handles RequiredNamePrefix (__builtin_ia32_), TargetLibBuiltin (kind Library), _Vector<N, T>, __bf16, 'signed int'-style spellings, and _Constant inside a type.
- BuiltinType::Vector lowers to CTypeKind::Vector (GNU vector_size); BuiltinAttribute::RequiredVectorWidth added; SChar now used.
- clang_builtin takes the TargetFamily: target tables apply only under the clang flavor, x86 table on i686/x86-64, x86-64 table on x86-64. gcc (slate-parser-cxg.43) and MSVC are unchanged. Features are recorded, not checked.
- Fixtures: clang/linux/x86_64/ir_x86_target_builtins.c, error/clang/linux/aarch64/x86_target_builtin.c.
- Remaining blocker for clang's *intrin.h wrappers: __m128i parsed as a target builtin type (slate-parser-v74.1.13).
