# Support ordered user and quote include paths

_2026-09-25 08:06:23_

slate-parser-cfa: Added -I and -iquote argument parsing in separated and attached forms, with quote-only, user, and system search tiers. Angled includes search user then system; quoted includes search the including directory, quote-only, user, then system. include_next follows those tiers and quoted local includes inherit system-header classification from their parent. Include paths beginning with = resolve under the selected sysroot. Added generated FileCheck fixtures for search order, include_next, sysroot-relative paths, and unsupported options; compared selected values with clang -E. Updated Slate src/slate_parser_frontend.rs to consume CompilerArgs::search_paths without disturbing its other uncommitted work.
