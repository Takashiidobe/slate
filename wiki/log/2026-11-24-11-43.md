# slate-j3by.24 lower_call: MSVC double-width shift intrinsics have no lowering

_2026-11-24 11:43:49_

- bead: slate-j3by.24
- kind: bug
- area: crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported/msvc/x86_64

Outcome: The MSVC unsupported ratchet covers double-width shifts at the count boundary.

On x64, __shiftleft128 returns the shifted high half and
__shiftright128 returns the shifted low half. Microsoft documents
a count modulo 64. Lean verifies that the remainder matches a
six-bit mask for every byte count and checks both directions at
64 and 65 with values that distinguish the two shifts.

The x64 cl.exe executable exits 0 under Wine. Slate recognizes both
builtins, but lower_call reports a missing lowering for each. The
MSVC triage reports the first barrier on the x64 target.

Fixture: crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported/msvc/x86_64/msvc_shift128_intrinsics.c
Limits: Both directions remain open in slate-j3by.24.
