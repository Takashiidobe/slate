# MergeFunctions vs C function identity: per-function link_section

_2026-10-02 21:27:31_

Experiment for slate-dghn.18 (no slate code change). rustc's default MergeFunctions aliased redis evalRoCommand to evalCommand (fns are unnamed_addr), breaking eval.c's c->cmd->proc == evalRoCommand check.

A/B of translated redis binaries (alternating, 10 samples, pipelined redis-benchmark, geomean of medians vs the default build):
- -Zmerge-functions=disabled: correct, 0.971 (binary +6 KB only).
- -Zmerge-functions=trampolines: incorrect; the compare operand is rewritten to the merged target, inverting the check.
- #[unsafe(link_section = ".text.slate_distinct.<name>")] on the 18 functions named in fn-pointer comparisons: correct, 1.008.

Decision: keep MergeFunctions on; slate analyzes the project IR for function-identity observations (ptr<fn> compares with a function operand protect that function; operand-less fn compares or fn ptr_to_int protect every address-taken function) and gives protected definitions a unique link_section. Projects without such checks pay nothing. Plan recorded in slate-dghn.18.
