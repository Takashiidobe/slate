# Classify declaration attributes instead of refusing them wholesale

_2026-09-20 16:12:59_

slate-parser-dyd.4. Three blanket refusals in `sema/module.rs` -- "typedef
attributes or asm label", "declaration attribute", "automatic variable
attributes or asm label" -- rejected a whole module over `[[deprecated]]` or
`may_alias` just as readily as over something that changes layout.

New `src/sema/attributes.rs` classifies every `Attribute` exactly once, with an
exhaustive match, into Symbol (folded into `SymbolAttributes`), Layout
(consumed by type resolution or the object request), Ignored, or Unsupported
with a reason. The three refusal sites now consult it. Functions keep their own
narrower filter in `function_symbol`, so nothing there changed.

Ignored covers diagnostic-only attributes, `may_alias`, and function
attributes written where they have no object meaning. `asm("sym")` on a typedef
or an automatic local is ignored, matching clang.

What is still refused now says what is missing: `machine mode attribute`,
`typedef alignment attribute`, `cleanup attribute`, `transparent union
attribute`, `scalar storage order attribute`, `address space attribute`,
`register asm label`. Typedef alignment is refused rather than dropped because
it belongs to the aliased type -- clang carries it to every object, field and
`_Alignof` of the alias, and `aligned(1)` on a typedef lowers alignment where
the same attribute on a declaration cannot -- and `ir::Type` desugars typedefs
away. Beads dyd.30..dyd.35 cover each of those.

Sweep 2498/2682: 10 configurations newly lower, none regressed. Fixture
`sema/ir_declaration_attributes.c`, verified against `clang -S -emit-llvm`.
