# slate-parser-of6j ast-spec: Migration table dropped, spec matches FieldItem/TypeSpecifier/Designator; pruning stays in parser for windows.h perf

_2026-11-18 11:36:13_

- bead: slate-parser-of6j
- kind: bookkeeping
- area: wiki/concepts/ast-spec.md

Outcome: ast-spec now describes src/ast.rs as it is; the Migration table is gone, replaced by "Known divergences" that are kept on purpose.

Closed slate-parser-of6j as won't-do. The renames it asked for (FieldItem -> MemberItem, Designator::Array/ArrayRange -> Index/IndexRange, the flattened TypeSpecifier table) bought nothing, and the spec's `Complex(FloatKind)` could not represent GNU `_Complex int`, which fixtures use (clang/linux/x86_64/complex_record_fields.c). The spec now uses the code's names, including `Complex(Box<TypeSpecifier>)` and the `Pragma` field item.

Pruning stays in the parser. On a `#include <windows.h>` TU (msvc, x86_64): `slate-parser ir` 0.79s pruned, 1.29s with `--all-declarations`; `clang -fsyntax-only` 0.14s; `slate-parser pp` alone 0.65s vs `clang -E` 0.085s. Moving pruning after resolution would add up to ~0.5s per windows.h TU, so the name-based rules in reachability.rs stay until sema is near clang's cost. The real windows.h cost is the preprocessor: slate-5q63.18.

See: slate-5q63.18, slate-parser-lh7.3, wiki/concepts/ir/pipeline.md#reachability-pruning
