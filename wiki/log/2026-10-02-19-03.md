# Packed records lower as repr(C, packed(A))

_2026-10-02 19:03:09_

slate-dghn.3. Redis's packed records (sds.h sdshdr*, server.h payloadHeader/bulkStrRef, mstr.h, glibc epoll_event) were 'record X: layout' barriers blocking 3560 functions in 71 of 120 redis-server TUs.

frontend/lowerer/types.rs build_record: record_pack picks packed(A), A = the IR record alignment, when any non-bit-field field's natural alignment exceeds A or its offset isn't a multiple of it. Fields are then placed at min(field_align, A) with the existing explicit padding; bit units are unchanged. Still barriers: packed with a raised record alignment (Rust rejects packed + align; packed_aligned_struct.c stays unsupported) and a packed record transitively containing a repr(align) record (contains_raised_align, E0588). Field updates already lower as copy read + write, so no references to packed fields are formed.

Fixture packed_sds_headers.c: sds headers reached through s - sizeof(hdr), payloadHeader written at misaligned buffer offsets with +=, packed struct containing a union, packed arrays nested in a natural struct, pragma pack(2). Promoted packed_struct, pragma_pack, zero_width_bitfield_abi, and 14 gcc-torture fixtures (packed-1, packed-2, strct-pack-1, strct-pack-4, pr29006, pr45070, pr71083, struct-ret-2, 960117-1, 20030224-2, 20040625-1, 20051113-1, 20131127-1, 20170401-1).
Wiki: ir-to-rust-lowering.md.

Redis: 76 -> 20 blocked TUs; functions ok 3531 -> 5666, blocked 3634 -> 80 (atomics now dominate). The 100 barrier-free TUs pass cargo check once the long-double shim has an ld2string prototype (slate-hi7g, raised to P1).
