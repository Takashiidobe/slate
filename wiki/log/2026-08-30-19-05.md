# Retire test-fixtures/, promote remaining probes

_2026-08-30 19:05:03_

test-fixtures/ was a holding area for exploratory GitHub-derived C probes
pending promotion into tests/fixtures/ with FileCheck regions. Most entries
had already been promoted (bitint_record_layout.c, packed_mixed_bitfields.c,
asm_dialect_switch.c, computed_goto_label_delta.c, statement_expr_local_label.c)
but the directory still carried stale duplicate copies plus 6 probes that
passed but were never registered:

  statement_expr_auto_type.c, generic_type_dispatch.c,
  vla_bound_side_effects.c, c23_typeof_unqual.c, transparent_union_call.c,
  compound_literal_address.c

Promoted all 6 into tests/fixtures/ with @lowering/@rewrite regions via
tools/update_filecheck.py; c23_typeof_unqual.c needed a -std=gnu23 entry in
fixture_clang_arg_overrides/fixture_c_ref_std_override (tests/differential.rs)
since it uses nullptr/constexpr/typeof_unqual.

Moved the two still-genuinely-blocked probes (asm_goto_statement_expr.c —
__asm__ goto shares the same repeated-__label__ pattern but through a
different CIRGen emission path than plain goto, so the getOrCreateCIRLabelName
fix doesn't cover it and it still hangs; zero_width_bitfield_abi.c — clang
-emit-cir itself rejects the aligned-tail struct) into
tests/fixtures.unsupported/ to keep them tracked without a differential
harness.

Deleted test-fixtures/ entirely; full lowering profile stays green (10/10).
