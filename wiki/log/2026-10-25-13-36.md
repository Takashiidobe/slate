# Always use cl's conforming preprocessor; drop traditional msvc comma elision

_2026-10-25 13:36:07_

slate-3amj.5.12, closed as won't do. Decision: slate always preprocesses msvc input like cl /Zc:preprocessor (\_MSVC_TRADITIONAL 0), even for flagless TUs where cl uses its traditional preprocessor; /Zc:preprocessor- stays rejected, since most libraries do not build with the traditional one.

That made one leftover traditional behavior wrong: comma_elision (pp/expand.rs) dropped the comma before any empty **VA_ARGS** for msvc, with or without ##. cl /EP /Zc:preprocessor keeps it unless it is ', ## **VA_ARGS**' with the variadic argument omitted, H() of H(...) counting as omitted (the gnu-mode rule). comma_elision now returns bool, msvc joins gnu modes for H(), and the ElideComma enum and the msvc emptiness check are gone. Regenerated msvc/windows/x86_64/va-args-comma-elision.c (checked against cl /EP /Zc:preprocessor).

Moved crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported/msvc/flag_msvc_traditional_preprocessor.c to the new tests/fixtures.ignored/msvc/ with a SLATE: comment saying why. Wiki: compiler-flags.md (always conforming, known divergence for flagless TUs), preprocessor.md (comma elision), differential-fixtures.md (ignored buckets).

Follow-up bead: stringizing drops the space before an empty argument ("m(3,)" vs "m(3, )") in every flavor.

Validation: msvc 322/322, parser 8596/8596, slate 22/22.
