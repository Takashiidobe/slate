# pp: #if condition mode, expanded #include operands, _Pragma in the stream (ryfo.4)

_2026-09-30 08:36:56_

slate-parser-ryfo.4.

- #if: expand_condition runs the stream expander over the directive. A defined or __has_* name takes its operand unexpanded from the stream, so a defined produced by an expansion works, with its operand from the expansion or from the directive after it (cxg.7.6). unexpanded_operands and the segment split are gone.
- #include: include::spells_header_name keeps a header name spelled before expansion ("...", or < with a > on the line) and ignores trailing tokens (#include <stddef.h> EMPTY). Otherwise the operands are expanded, and a <...> name from macro tokens is joined with their leading spaces (cxg.7.11).
- _Pragma/__pragma: read_piece recognizes them on final tokens as the group expands, with an expanded lookahead for ( (_Pragma LP "once")). record_pragma runs there, so a _Pragma pop_macro changes the rest of its line, as in clang. emit_line takes Pieces; pragma_operator is gone.
