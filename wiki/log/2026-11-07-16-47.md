# Move object_alignment to x86_64 fixtures

_2026-11-07 16:47:46_

slate-yar4.33: object_alignment checks the x86-64 SysV psABI rule that array objects of 16+ bytes are 16-aligned (and v2uq loads that depend on it). AAPCS64, i386 and AAPCS32 make no such guarantee: on aarch64 clang's 16-byte global alignment is a preferred-alignment choice (its own locals print 0), and on i686 the C oracle crashes. Slate does not mimic non-ABI preferred alignment. git mv to tests/fixtures/x86_64/object_alignment.c, dropped from the i686, aarch64 and armv7 unsupported lists. Passes on x86_64.
