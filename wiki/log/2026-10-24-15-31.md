# Map __vectorcall to vectorcall-unwind for rustc-native shapes

_2026-10-24 15:31:53_

slate-ck8y: __vectorcall on x86_64 now lowers to extern "vectorcall-unwind" (Abi::VectorcallUnwind, foreign block "vectorcall") with #![feature(abi_vectorcall)] (Feature::AbiVectorcall, Dependencies::vectorcall), via Tables::convention_abi.
Only shapes rustc already passes like clang are admitted: Tables::function_abi barriers a vectorcall definition/extern unless every argument is scalar/native_c and the result is scalar/native_c/void (rustc_spells_vectorcall). A void argument (Linux empty record) is rejected: clang drops it, rustc passes it byref.
Function-pointer types carry no AbiSignature, so lower_type admits a vectorcall pointer type only when it is not variadic and every parameter and the result are bool, pointer, a non-_BitInt integer of at most 64 bits, f32 or f64 (Tables::vectorcall_scalar).
Decision: no flattening and no c_shim bridges for vectorcall. rustc's vectorcall uses win64 rules on every x86_64 OS with no HVAs, so compensating in slate would rebuild clang's front-end classification; wait for rustc instead (slate-hxtv, deferred; rust-lang/rust#124485, #164749, #130196; no rustc issue yet for x86_64 HVAs or SysV records).
Fixtures: tests/fixtures/x86_64/vectorcall_scalars.c (definitions, direct and pointer calls, 9 mixed int/float args); tests/fixtures.unsupported/x86_64/vectorcall_records.c (coerce<i64, i32> argument and its pointer type).
