# msvc: recover from C4067 trailing tokens in #if

_2026-10-24 09:32:33_

slate-pola. cl.exe evaluates #if with a narrower grammar and ignores leftovers: '#if UNDEF(1)' is 0 followed by ignored tokens (warning C4067), so it is false; slate's msvc flavor errored 'call to UNDEF is not a constant expression'.

Probed with cl.exe /E: operands take no postfix operators (call, [], ., ++), there is no comma or assignment, so '0 ,1' is false and '1 = 0' true. Leftovers after a complete top-level conditional are ignored; '1 )' (first leftover a ')') and leftovers inside parentheses ('(UNDEF(1))', '(0 ,1)') are fatal C1012; '1 +' stays C1017.

- const_expr.rs: Parser::msvc_condition, set by evaluate_directive only for #if/#elif under msvc (evaluate_with_defined gets a trailing_tokens Cell; #embed operands stay strict). parse_directive_operand parses a conditional instead of a comma expression at the top level and inside parentheses; parse_postfix skips its loop. The index of the first leftover token is reported instead of UnexpectedTokens.
- pp/mod.rs evaluate_condition warns with cl.exe's message under the new named warning extra-tokens (diagnostics.rs, default on, not pedantic).
- gcc/clang unchanged.
- Fixtures: msvc/windows/x86_64/if-trailing-tokens.c (15 cases, each checked against cl.exe), error/msvc/windows/x86_64/if-trailing-rparen.c, if-trailing-tokens-in-parens.c.
