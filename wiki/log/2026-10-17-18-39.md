# Lower \_\_builtin_stdc_rotate_left/right on integers and \_BitInt

_2026-10-17 18:39:43_

stdc rotate lowers through lower_stdc_rotate (bitint.rs): plain integers call rotate_left/right with count.rem_euclid(width), odd-width small \_BitInt rotates in the container and renormalizes through narrow_integer, wide \_BitInt calls the new BUint::rotate_left_bits/rotate_right_bits with the reduced shift. Counts of any type work, wide counts via BInt::rem_euclid_u32 and BUint::rem_u32. slate-parser's **has_builtin now reports the two stdc rotate builtins. New fixture bitint_rotate (unsupported on i686 where clang cannot link the \_BitInt(100/128) rotates); promoted torture**bitint-75 and builtin-stdc-rotate-1. The rest of the stdc family (leading_zeros, bit_ceil, ...) needs slate-parser sema support and is tracked separately.
