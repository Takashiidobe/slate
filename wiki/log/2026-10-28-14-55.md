# Scope const-bound slice lifts to guarded offset sites

_2026-10-28 14:55:13_

slate-32tp, slate-rh5y: length_lattice::const_candidates keyed loop bounds by index name across the whole function and took the max, without checking where the pointer's offsets sit. p[i] after a for (i < 4) loop lifted to a 4-element &[T] and panicked on p[4] (32tp); p[i] under i < 4 next to an unrelated i < 8 loop got bound 8, so a 4-element caller array failed the proof and p stayed raw (rh5y). Now each offset site takes its bound only from loops whose body holds that site (pointer identity on the index Expr) and whose guard is on its index, min over enclosing guards, max over sites; a site with no guarding loop rejects the candidate. collect_loop_bounds also returns only the loop statements after the if !(i < N) break guard, so a do-while shape (i++; p[i]; guard) no longer counts its pre-guard access as guarded; this also tightens collect_fn.
