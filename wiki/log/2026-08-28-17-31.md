# Pointer lattice: wire Scalar+Owned lift pass, defer Cell/Buffer (y0qs.4.2)

_2026-08-28 17:31:12_

Wired pointer_lattice::solve() into a consuming lift pass (slate-y0qs.4.2),
closing the gap left by 4.1. Landed as pointer_lattice::apply(), a
whole-Program AST rewrite run in engine::apply() before the arena/NodeRule
pipeline -- not the two arena NodeRules the original ticket described (no
requeue machinery needed since there's no arena at this stage; see the
concept doc for the full rationale).

lift_kind() now produces Scalar (Ref/RefMut -> &T/&mut T) and Owned (->
Box<T>). Owned was the hard part: bridging a raw pointer into Box<T> at
call sites via unsafe Box::from_raw, and -- critically -- deleting the C
free() call that justified the FREE bit, tracing through CIR's usual
intermediate-local aliasing (let y = arg0; ...; free(y);) via a new
collect_owned_aliases pass. Skipping that deletion would double-free.

Decided NOT to wire up Cell/RefCell despite already having the rewrite
machinery for it (.get()/.set()): it only saves the unsafe keyword at the
deref site (every raw-pointer use still falls back to .as_ptr(), just as
unsafe-shaped as before), and the disprove-unique signal that gates it
proved unreliable to trigger even by hand. Buffer rows (Vec/Slice/Str)
stay deferred -- needs call-site length bridging, a structurally separate
feature, filed as slate-y0qs.4.8.

Found a real soundness gap while implementing Owned: solve()'s FREE taint
has no per-call-site attribution, so a shared helper called from both an
owning and non-owning context can get unsoundly tainted. Caught here only
incidentally by validate_plans' bare-Var-only bridging gate. Filed as
slate-y0qs.4.9 (P2).

Verified via tests/fixtures/pointer*lattice.c (new, all three lifted
shapes in one program) plus the two pre-existing ptr_lattice*\* fixtures,
all passing full compile+run+stdout/exit-code differential. Full
rewrites (110/110) and lowering (10/10) nextest profiles green, fmt/clippy
clean.
