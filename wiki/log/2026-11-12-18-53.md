# slate-dghn.41.2 slate-libc-gen: anonymous aggregate members and flexible arrays (rusage, cmsghdr) no longer canonicalized to libc

_2026-11-12 18:53:58_

- bead: slate-dghn.41.2
- kind: bug
- area: crates/slate-libc-gen/src

Outcome: slate-libc-gen no longer canonicalizes records whose C definition has anonymous aggregate members or flexible-array fields the libc crate lacks, so glibc rusage and cmsghdr stay local and libuv loses 20 of its 47 cargo-check errors.

Symptom: translated libuv failed cargo check with `no field __slate_anon_N on type rusage` (14) and `no field __cmsg_data on type cmsghdr` (3), because both were lowered to slate_support (libc crate) paths.

Reproducer: `python3 tools/corpus/e2e.py libuv --mode clang --until check`.

Cause: decide.rs skipped `<anonymous>` fields and ignored reserved fields (`__cmsg_data`) not mirrored by the provider, so offset checks on named fields were enough. Separately, c_side.rs `collect` kept the first definition per name, and sys/wait.h's forward `struct rusage;` shadowed the complete type, making glibc rusage "opaque".

Fix: decide_one blocks an anonymous aggregate member and a reserved flexible-array field (`flexible_array`); collect prefers a complete definition over an incomplete one. Catalogs regenerated: also drops android sigaction/siginfo_t, darwin mach_port_options_t/processor_basic_info, and glibc/android termios/ucontext_t/tcgetattr/tcsetattr/cfmakeraw targets.

Regression test: none new; slate profile 36/36 green after regeneration.

Limits: libuv still has 27 errors from iovec/msghdr field types (*mut c_void/usize vs *mut ()/u64), tracked in slate-dghn.41.1. Added a libuv recipe to tools/corpus/e2e.py.
