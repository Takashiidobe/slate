# gcc aarch64 -march predefines

_2026-09-27 17:22:36_

slate-parser-78x3.4. src/target/aarch64_isa.rs modeled only clang's -march resolution; gcc differed on 332 macros across 85 march sets.
ArmMarch now applies every +ext/+noext modifier under two rule sets (Rules::Clang, Rules::Gcc) and AArch64Isa::resolve picks one by flavor (TargetIsa::resolve takes the flavor; baselines stay flavorless since armv8-a is identical).
gcc rules: fcma/jscvt/frintts are features (COMPLEX/JCVT/FRINT) permanently disabled by +nosimd/+nofp, +sve enables fcma and simd; +nosimd permanently disables sve/sve2 but keeps bf16/fp16fml (only vector macros gated); +nofp drops bf16; v9 has fp16fml; no MOPS/CSSC defaults; FAMINMAX/LUT at 9.5; +crypto never adds SHA3/SM4.
gcc_predefines owns FMA, FP_FAST_FMA*, FP16_ARGS/FORMAT_IEEE, STDC_IEC_*, NUMERIC_MAXMIN (stripped from the gcc snapshot), plus GCC_IEC_559 (0 without fp), FLT_EVAL_METHOD 16 with fp16 (0 for __FLT_EVAL_METHOD__ under strict -std, threaded as strict_iso), DESTRUCTIVE_SIZE 64 on v9, SVE_BITS / PREDICATE_OPERATORS (1 with fixed vector bits).
Verified: 255 march sets (17 versions x 15 modifier sets) show zero non-__SLATE_ differences vs aarch64-linux-gnu-gcc 16.1 via tools/gcc_macro_diff.py; the same sweep against clang is byte-identical before/after. Extra checks: -std=c11, -msve-vector-bits, +nosimd+simd, +nofp+simd, +nosimd+sve, +nosimd+bf16.
Fixture: sema/aarch64-unknown-linux-gnu/arm_isa_gcc_modifiers.c. Docs: compiler-flags.md AArch64 bullet.
