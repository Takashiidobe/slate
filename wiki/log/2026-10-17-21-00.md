# Typedef aligned(N) reaches IR as Access::align; slate emits unaligned accesses

_2026-10-17 21:00:04_

Aligned typedefs that lower a type's natural alignment were dropped by the IR, so accesses through P* (typedef struct S P aligned(1)) lowered to aligned loads and crashed.

slate-parser: ir::Access gained align: Option<u64>. Resolver::access_of sets it via reduced_alignment (typedef_alignment below the natural storage alignment); Lowerer::deref stamps it on Deref places, and it flows to reads and writes of fields and lanes below. Printed as read<T, align=N>. CTypes::access (used for pointer pointee types) leaves it None, so ptr types are unchanged. typedef_alignment.c regenerated, new typedef_alignment_access.c; ir-grammar and ir/types.md updated.

slate: places.rs reduced_alignment_access treats a place as unaligned when it is rooted in a Deref/Index and its access.align is set, or when its Deref pointer derives (PointerOffset, ArrayDecay, AddressOf) from such a place or from a field of a packed record. Reads become unsafe addr_of!(place).read_unaligned(), writes addr_of_mut!(place).write_unaligned(v); lane access reuses the earlier lane path. New fixture typedef_aligned_pointer_access.c.

Promoted gcc-dg torture__pr52419, pr57748-1..4, pr58041. Regenerated FileCheck for packed_vector_field, target_feature_dispatch, vector_reduce_builtins, vector_shuffle_convert: _mm_storeu/loadu through aligned(1) packed wrappers now emit unaligned accesses instead of aligned derefs.

Closed slate-9ox1.3.1-.3.6 and .3.16. Still open in cluster .3: .3.7/.3.8/.3.17 (flexible array member initializer storage).
