# clang flavor: __real__/__imag__ lvalues and casts, -ffixed-point gating (cxg.34)

_2026-11-08 20:08:37_

slate-parser-cxg.34, remaining items from the gcc.dg clang pass. Ten of the thirteen listed files already passed at HEAD (guality noipa, excess initializers, range-test-1, builtin-sprintf-warn-3, vect-pr124802).

__real__/__imag__ (both flavors, matching clang and gcc):
- validate.rs: the syntactic asm-output lvalue checks look through __real__/__imag__; asm.rs then rejects a non-lvalue output with the typer (clang 'invalid lvalue in asm output', gcc 'lvalue required in asm statement'), so __imag__ of an int is still rejected.
- typer.rs: no integer promotion of a real operand (sizeof(__real__ short) is 2, was 4); __real__ of a real arithmetic lvalue stays an lvalue (assignable, asm output). expression.rs place(): __real__ of a non-complex place is the place itself.
- const_expr.rs: the operand is a cast expression, so __imag__ (short)3 parses.

-ffixed-point (clang only, new Opt::FixedPoint -> SourceGraphs::fixed_point): clang reserves _Fract/_Accum/_Sat and accepts fixed-point literal suffixes only with the flag; without it they are identifiers and 0.5hr is 'invalid suffix'. With it, pp/mod.rs predefines CLANG_FIXED_POINT_MACROS (48 __FRACT_*/__ACCUM_* macros, identical on every clang target). gcc unchanged. The four existing clang fixed-point fixtures now pass -ffixed-point.
