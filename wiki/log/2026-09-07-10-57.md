# AArch64 fixed-vector asm bridge

_2026-09-07 10:57:01_

Added fixed-width AArch64 SIMD bridging for inline asm: C vectors are transmuted to matching core::arch::aarch64 SIMD types for vreg/vreg_low16 operands and transmuted back to Slate arrays after outputs. Expanded the AArch64 fixture to cover x with float32x4_t and documented scalable SVE y as still unsupported.
