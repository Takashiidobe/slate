# Emulate cl.exe /arch: and /feature:APX for the msvc flavor

_2026-10-25 13:54:24_

slate-3amj.5.10. /arch:IA32|SSE|SSE2|SSE4.2|AVX|AVX2|AVX512|AVX10.1|AVX10.2 and /feature:APX.

- compiler_options.rs: MsvcArchLevel (ordered), MsvcArch { level, avx10 }, MicrosoftFlags::{arch, apx}.
- compiler_args.rs: /arch: values are parsed into a list (Opt::MsvcArch, unknown rejected) and resolved once the target is known (resolve_msvc_arch). cl keeps the highest level, not the last: always on x64, and on x86 for the AVX options, where a later IA32/SSE/SSE2 resets it. The AVX10 version is the last one given. Rejected: IA32/SSE on x64, SSE4.2 on x86, /arch on Arm, and on x86 IA32/SSE/SSE2 after an AVX10 option (cl keeps parts of AVX10 through them in no consistent way, e.g. AVX10.2 AVX512 SSE2 AVX gives **AVX** and **AVX10_VER** without **AVX2**). request_msvc_arch feeds the level into the shared x86 TargetIsa request, so vector width follows it as with -m flags. /feature:APX is x64 only; unknown features are rejected (cl ignores all of these with D9002).
- pp/mod.rs msvc_arch_predefines: **AVX**/**AVX2**/**AVX512{F,CD,BW,DQ,VL}** by level, **AVX10_VER**, \_NO_PREFETCHW for any x64 /arch, \_M_IX86_FP 0/1 for IA32/SSE, and the nine APX macros.

Verified against cl.exe 19.51: every single value on both targets, plus 180 random sequences on x64 and x86 with no differences (the only rejections are the intentional AVX10-then-legacy ones).

Fixtures: msvc/windows/x86*64/{arch,feature}\_option*_.c (12), msvc/windows/i686/arch*option*_.c (9), error/ ...(6). Promoted slate msvc/x86_64/flag_msvc_arch_avx2.c and msvc/i686/flag_msvc_arch_ia32.c. Wiki: compiler-flags.md. Follow-up bead for arm64 /arch:armv8.x.

Validation: msvc 350/350, parser 8626/8626, slate 22/22.
