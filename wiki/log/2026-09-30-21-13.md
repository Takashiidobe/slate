# Lower conditional values and numbered temps in the slate frontend

_2026-09-30 21:13:00_

slate-p58o.3.29.

- ValueKind::Conditional lowers to a Rust if expression with lower_condition on the test. Effectful ternaries were already hoisted to if statements by sema, which is why conditionals.c and assign_value_in_branch.c passed before.
- A conditional of function-pointer type binds the if to a typed let (let __tN: Option<unsafe fn(..)> = if ..; __tN), because two different Rust fn items in Some(..) arms do not unify without an expected type.
- Sequence is not lowered: sema's effects pass removes it from every function-body value (pure comma left sides are dropped), so no fixture can reach it. Capture (GNU a ?: b, VLA extents) is likewise hoisted to a Temporary.
- Generated temps now come from Context::next_temp: __t0, __t1, ... reset per function. The switch selector (__slate_switch_<id>) and the aggregate builder (__slate_aggregate) use it too. __t cannot collide with __v<binding id> names.
- New fixture conditional_values.c: nested ternaries, ternaries in arithmetic, conditions, call args and returns, pointer/double/u64/struct results.
- Promoted: conditional_values, int128_arith, ternary_function_pointer_select, function_pointer_presence (bead notes), 4 more local fixtures, 45 gcc-torture, 2 c-testsuite.
