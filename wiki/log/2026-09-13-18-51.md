_2026-09-13 18:51:22_

Rewrote wiki/concepts/ir-design.md after review of the flat slot/load form (rejected: too many allocas/temps, poor Rust lowering). Decided: bodies are statements with typed expression trees; shown types are concrete target widths (i32, f80) with C types/typedef chains as metadata; derived facts (mut, addr_taken, ranges) are a separate analysis pass after lowering; side effects (assign, ++/--, compound assign) hoisted into statements; array decay and length are metadata; a[i]/\*p/a[i]=v lower to ptr_read/ptr_write at element offset with form metadata. Documented hoisting hazards (f(i++) sequencing, short-circuit, loop conditions). Open: member access via projection paths vs field_ptr, synthetic temps for f(i++), function type parameters, metadata syntax.
