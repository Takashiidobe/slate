# Dedupe expr-fold walkers in rewrite-engine rules

_2026-09-04 12:37:43_

peel_casts.rs, constant_index_casts.rs, and cstr_literal.rs each hand-rolled an identical NodeKind-to-expr-slots match plus an identical bottom-up expr-tree fold (mut version for apply, read-only version for matches) -- ~90 duplicated lines per file, 3x over. Added shared visit_kind_exprs/visit_kind_exprs_mut and fold_bottom_up/any_collapsible generic combinators to src/backend/engine/rules/walk.rs; each of the three rules now only supplies its own single-node rewrite_here/collapse_here predicate. Net -161 lines across the 4 files.

Also collapsed libc_call.rs's peel_ptr_view and peel_call_head (the latter was a strict subset of the former: peel casts + block-tail unwrap, but not .as_ptr()/.as_mut_ptr()) into one peel_wrapper(expr, through_ptr_methods: bool).

No behavior change: cargo build, clippy, and the rewrites nextest profile (93/93) all pass. This is a pure structural cleanup, not a new pass or fixup -- prompted by user request to reduce redundancy/genericity debt in the rules/ directory, not tied to a beads issue.
