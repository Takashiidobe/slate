# Pass an empty parameter's leading space to the next token

_2026-10-25 13:41:07_

slate-da7q. A parameter with leading space that substituted to nothing dropped the space, so #define M(fmt, ...) m(fmt, __VA_ARGS__) stringized M(3) as "m(3,)" in every flavor; clang -E, gcc -E and cl /EP /Zc:preprocessor give "m(3, )".

substitute_function_macro (pp/expand.rs) now records the output position after each empty non-paste-operand substitution of a leading-space parameter and sets leading_space on the token that lands there. gcc and clang carry it whether the argument is empty or only expands empty; cl only for an empty raw argument (J(E) of j(x a) gives "j(x)" under cl, "j(x )" under clang and gcc), checked case by case against all three.

Fixtures: new stringize-empty-argument-space.c under clang/, gcc/ (linux x86_64) and msvc/windows/x86_64; regenerated va-args-comma-elision.c in all three (plain_omitted, plain_empty now "m(3, )"). Wiki: preprocessor.md Expansion.

Validation: parser 8599/8599, slate 22/22.
