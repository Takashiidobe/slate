# Slate frontend GNU void and function pointer arithmetic

_2026-09-30 22:12:51_

slate-p58o.4.1: GNU C counts void* and function-pointer arithmetic in bytes.

- ptr_offset with element=void now casts the pointer to *const u8, offsets, and casts back to the lowered result type. Before this, void lowered to () and .offset() on a ZST pointer silently did not move (e.g. `base + index` on a const void* lowered but was wrong).
- ptr_offset with element=fn transmutes the Option<fn> to *const u8, uses wrapping_offset (fn pointers are not in a Rust allocation), and transmutes back.
- ptr_diff with element=void uses offset_from on *const u8 casts.
- ptr_diff with element=fn subtracts addresses with isize wrapping_sub after transmuting to *const u8.
- New helpers byte_pointer/byte_pointer_type in slate_parser_frontend/lowerer.rs.

Fixtures: new gnu_void_pointer_arithmetic.c; promoted gnu_function_pointer_arithmetic.c and gcc-torture pr48571-1.c.
Validation: cargo nextest r --release --profile slate (green after the torture promotion).
