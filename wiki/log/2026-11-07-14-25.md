# Close slate-5vj0 and triage i686/aarch64 target failures

_2026-11-07 14:25:10_

Closed slate-5vj0 (FileCheck on slate-parser frontend) with slate-2mrh, .3, .5, .8. Triaged fixtures.unsupported/{i686,aarch64}-unknown-linux-gnu.txt by running each listed fixture alone (list emptied, restored after).
Oracle-limited, stay listed: clang rejects __int128 on i686 (int128_arith, int128_struct, builtin_generic_bit_counts, saturating_arith, long_double) and _BitInt(>128)/__float128 on aarch64 (bitint_*, float128, long_double, saturating_arith, switch_case_wide_bitint*). Slate accepting them is not a bug (slate-5exy.9 closed won't fix).
stdlib_fp_contract (aarch64): clang fuses under FP_CONTRACT ON, slate never fuses by design (slate-3amj.3); implementation-defined.
main_process_argv (aarch64): qemu-user /proc/self/cmdline shows the host path, not the renamed argv[0].
Filed: slate-yar4.30 (i686 __slate_f80_va_arg VaList vs *mut i8), .31 (i686 wide _BitInt va_arg), .32 (i686 64-bit "+r" asm; asm_barriers also x86-only pause), .33 (aarch64 global array alignment; object_alignment crashes the i686 C oracle), slate-xw0x.7 (i686 oracle lacks libgcc TI helpers: bitint_float, bitint_rotate).
