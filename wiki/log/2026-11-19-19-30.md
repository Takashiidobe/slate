Outcome: `slate::sensitive_wildcard` ignores single-variant projections, and `wiki/concepts/linting.md` records the suppression, no-panic and custom-lint policy.

- Rule: a catch-all fires only if the arms name two or more variants at the wildcard's position (a classifier). One named variant plus `_` is a projection, equivalent to `if let`, and is exempt.
- Triage sample of 6 `CTypeKind` hits: projections (`element`, `lower_return`) were noise; `is_sugar`, `composite`, the rotate-builtin `matches!` and `arith::integer` were real classifiers.
- `CTypeKind` marked as a probe: 91 hits before, 41 after. Top files: ctype/mod.rs 7, convert.rs 5, types.rs 4, operand.rs 4, compat.rs 4, lowerer/types.rs 4.
- Policy order for a hit: list the variants (shared sets become one exhaustive named predicate), else return an error, else an arm-level expect whose reason says why the default is right for any future variant.
