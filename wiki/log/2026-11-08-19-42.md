# Model and map i686 __vectorcall

_2026-11-08 19:42:12_

slate-lc0y. Diffed clang -emit-llvm (i686-pc-windows-msvc, i686-unknown-linux-gnu) against rustc extern "vectorcall" IR and asm. LLVM puts float/double/vector args in XMM0-5 in order whether or not clang marks them inreg, so those agree.
Parser (sema/abi.rs AbiClassifier::x86_vectorcall_parts): clang's two passes over 6 XMM registers. Non-atomic float/16-32-64 byte vector args take one first regardless of position, then HVAs (vectorcall_hva, shared with x64) take one per member or go byref. A vector left without a register is byref (rustc: on the stack). HVA results are coerce<...>. On Windows a record with required alignment > 4 is byref (clang passes the pointer in ecx, rustc on the stack). Everything else uses the cdecl shapes. Renamed VECTORCALL_X64_VECTOR_REGISTERS to VECTORCALL_VECTOR_REGISTERS. Fixtures: clang/{linux,windows}/i686/abi_vectorcall.c.
Slate: convention_abi maps X86Vectorcall on TargetFamily::X86 to vectorcall-unwind. Integer inreg gaps (an int after a long long that found a register free; Linux records with clang's inreg padding) are the fastcall ones, so Tables::uses_fastcall_registers applies fastcall_matches_rustc to i686 vectorcall definitions, externs and pointer types; rustc_spells_shapes barriers the explicit HVA/byref shapes.
Fixtures: tests/fixtures/i686/vectorcall_scalars.c, tests/fixtures/msvc/i686/flag_msvc_vectorcall_default.c (cl.exe /Gv), tests/fixtures.unsupported/i686/vectorcall_divergent.c. Docs: calls-abi i686 vectorcall section.
