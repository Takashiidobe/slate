# Label nested macro expansions and fold them bottom up

_2026-11-01 09:01:28_

slate-f2zu.9.5.11: a macro called from another macro's body now gets its own region and macro_rules, even with no direct call site. Parser: pp records a NestedExpansion (definition, parent) for a call spelled whole in a user macro body, and Span.macro_nesting labels each token with the expansions holding it and whether it is body or which argument. Location could not do this because nested tokens share the outer site's expansion. Span::cover keeps an expansion only if all covered tokens are in it, and reports body when they disagree on the argument. --show-macros prints [nested=NAME#id body|argN] and macro_nested_expansion lines. Fixture: clang/linux/x86_64/macro_nesting.c. Lowering: macro_regions is a stack of MacroRegionId (Source or Nested), and Expr::MacroArg and MacroRegion carry a level, so x passed through two macros lowers to MacroArg{1,..,MacroArg{0,..,x}}. Rewrites stop at the inner boundary as they do at top level. Fold: macro_fold runs in rounds over innermost regions, chooses templates per name once, folds matches, and flattens failed nested regions, stripping only their level.
