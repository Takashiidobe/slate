# Lower \_BitInt arithmetic, comparison and bitwise operators (slate-2o6o.16)

_2026-10-17 17:12:56_

Wide _BitInt (N>64, and 128 off aarch64) Arith add/sub/mul/div/rem/and/or/xor now lower to the bitint crate's const fns (wrapping_\*, bitand_bits/bitor_bits/bitxor_bits); comparisons lower to a new const fn compare() -> i32 compared against 0, so wide ops are legal in static initializers.

Small bit-bearing _BitInt (N<64, non-native width) Arith and Unary compute in the native container with wrapping_\* and re-normalize through narrow_integer (shl/shr sign-extend or mask). Before this, ~a, -a and overflowing add/mul left stray high bits in the container.

Shifts are untouched (slate-6uvv.1).

Fixtures: new bitint_ops (14 widths x all operators + static initializers), promoted; torture\_\_bitint-2 now passes (closes slate-6uvv.7). bitint_ops is listed for aarch64 where clang rejects \_BitInt > 128. Regenerated FileCheck for bitint_storage and switch_case_range_fold_wide (+ became .wrapping_add; constant negation of a small \_BitInt is no longer folded).

bitint_arith stays unsupported: it stops at wide switch case values (slate-6uvv.2).
