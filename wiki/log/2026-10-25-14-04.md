# Emulate cl.exe arm64 /arch:armv8.x|armv9.x and /feature:lse|rcpc|rcpc2

_2026-10-25 14:04:21_

slate-3amj.5.23: /arch on aarch64-pc-windows-msvc was rejected. /arch: and /feature: now collect raw strings and resolve per target family (compiler_args.rs): x86/x64 via resolve_msvc_arch (msvc_x86_arch_level) and resolve_msvc_apx, arm64 via resolve_msvc_arm64_arch into MicrosoftFlags::arm64_arch (MsvcArm64Arch {major, minor, lse, rcpc}). Model, from probing the arm64 cl.exe /PD: armv8.0-8.9 and armv9.0-9.4 with optional +lse/+rcpc/+rcpc2; the last /arch wins along with its +features; /feature: values accumulate in any order, across /arch. msvc_arm64_predefines (pp/mod.rs): __ARM_ARCH=major*100+minor for anything but 8.0; _M_ARM64_EXTENSION=80+x for v8.x, removed for v9.x; __ARM_FEATURE_ATOMICS from 8.1 or lse; __ARM_FEATURE_RCPC 1 from 8.3 or rcpc, 2 from 8.4 or rcpc2 (v9.x counts as 8.5+). The version and features feed the AArch64 TargetIsa march via ArmMarch::with_features. Unknown versions and features are rejected (cl errors D8021/D8043/D8044); so are x86 values and other spellings (cl ignores them, D9002). Fuzzed 200 random sequences against cl with 0 diffs; the x86 fuzz is unchanged. Fixtures: msvc/windows/aarch64/{arch,feature}_option_*.c (9) and error/msvc/windows/aarch64/ (4).
