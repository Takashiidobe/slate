# Remove emit-fixtures/emit-lowered-fixtures

_2026-09-06 13:00:18_

Deleted the emit-fixtures and emit-lowered-fixtures CLI subcommands and their
supporting functions (emit_fixtures, emit_lowered_fixtures,
clear_generated_dir, emit_c_fixture_tree, emit_project_fixture_tree,
collect_c_files) from src/main.rs, plus the orphaned private wrapper
functions they were the sole caller of (translate, translate_project,
translate_project_lib_crate -- the real CLI dispatch goes through
translate_with_clang_args/translate_project_command/
translate_project_lib_command instead).

Why: FileCheck fixture directives (SLATE-FILECHECK-BEGIN/END) now assert on
generated-code shape directly, making the gitignored tests/\*.generated
mirror-tree mechanism redundant. Part of slate-i3nv (CLI/mode consolidation
epic), child slate-i3nv.1.

Also: removed the now-stray .gitignore rule for tests/\*.generated/, deleted
the stale generated directories left on disk, and updated
wiki/concepts/slate-overview.md and tests/fixtures.cfg/README.md references.
No fixture or pipeline behavior changed -- pure removal of an inspection-only
side path.
