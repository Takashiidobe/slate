# Parse clang nullability qualifiers

_2026-10-30 20:42:38_

slate-parser-2ka9: _Nonnull, _Nullable, _Null_unspecified and _Nullable_result failed to parse, which stopped Android, Darwin and FreeBSD libc headers. Added four Keyword variants gated by StandardFeatures::nullability_keywords (clang flavor only; they stay identifiers under gcc and MSVC). take_qualifier accepts them and set_qualifier drops them, so they never reach the AST or IR. Added to the const_expr type-start list for casts and sizeof. Too permissive by policy: a non-pointer type or conflicting specifiers are accepted where clang rejects. Fixtures: clang/linux/x86_64/nullability_qualifiers.c, gcc and msvc nullability_identifiers.c, clang/{android,darwin,freebsd}/{x86_64,aarch64}/nullability_headers.c. Darwin <stdlib.h> now stops on block pointer declarators, filed as slate-e36k.3. Android <stdio.h> still needs slate-e36k.2.
