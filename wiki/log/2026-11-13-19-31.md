Implementation:
- sema/builtins.rs: new Expansion variants ShuffleLanes, AlignBytes, PermuteHalves, MaskedFmaddSub(Passthrough), MaskedStore, ShuffleWords, PermutePairs, MaskLogic(MaskOp); CompareMask now carries `integer: Option<unsigned>`. vpermilps* reuse ShuffleDwords. Direct Expansion::Intrinsic for round{ps,pd,ss,sd}[256] (llvm.x86.sse41/avx.round.*), vcvtph2ps512_mask, reduce_fm{ax,in}_p{s,d}512 (llvm.vector.reduce.fmax/fmin) and the 16 512-bit gather/scatter builtins.
- sema/x86_vector_builtins.rs: index formulas follow CGBuiltin (shufps imm * 0x01010101 per 128-bit lane; palignr shift >16 shifts a against zero, >=32 zero; vperm2f128 bit 3 zeroes a half). Integer compares use imm & 7 (eq lt le false ne ge gt true) on lanes bit cast to signed/unsigned. Operands read twice are bound once (bind_all). k-ops are integer ops on the mask type; constant kshift >= width gives zero.
- lowerer/vectors.rs: slate.masked_store -> Simd::store_select_ptr(v, p as *mut Lane, Mask::from_bitmask(k)) (shares lane_enable with masked_load). slate.vector_sign_bitmask now names Simd::<iW, L>::default(): an all-ones `!splat(0)` operand left N uninferable (E0284).
