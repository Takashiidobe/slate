# Token-precise PP diagnostics and #undef/#error

_2026-09-12 14:16:39_

slate-parser-11p.5 and .6. Directive now records name_loc, with arguments_loc()/end_loc() helpers. Diagnostics point at the offending token: invalid macro names at the bad argument (or the directive name when missing), unclosed #define parameters at the '(', bad header names at the first argument, unsupported directives at their name, include not-found/cycle/read failures at the header name. const_expr::Parser::evaluate_with_defined now returns LocatedConstExprError{error, token: Option<usize>}: the parser's stop position for parse errors (stepping back one for UnexpectedToken raised after take() consumed the token), None for evaluation errors, which map to the whole expression. Extra tokens after #ifdef/#ifndef/#undef are ignored rather than errors, matching clang (which only warns); previously they errored. Macro table is now Conditional<MacroEntry{definition: Option<MacroDef>, provenance, order}>; #undef records a None entry under the active condition. Shared Preprocessor::visible_entry (latest entry whose condition equals active or is statically true) drives expansion, which stops at an undef entry, and defined() in #if, which falls back to 'defined under any condition' when nothing is visible (the old contains_key behavior). concretize_guard only treats real definitions as the guard. #error fails the parse only when the active condition is statically true; under symbolic conditions it is dropped (follow-up filed). New fixtures: pp-undef.c, error-directive.c, if-expression-trailing-tokens.c, ifdef-invalid-macro-name.c, define-unclosed-parameters.c; missing-include.c and include-cycle.c now underline the header name.
