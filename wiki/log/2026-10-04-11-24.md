# Target-selecting flags: -m16/-m32/-m64/-mx32, GNU short triples, -mregparm

_2026-10-04 11:24:01_

slate-parser-6x05.38.1.

- -m16/-m32/-m64/-mx32 (gcc, clang; last wins) rewrite the --target triple before
  lookup, regardless of argv order (target_registry::arch_variant): x86_64 -> i686,
  i386..i686 -> x86_64, -m64 no-op on x86_64/aarch64, -mx32 -> x86_64-...-gnux32
  (no TargetSpec yet: slate-parser-6x05.38.7). -m16 is -m32 for C: clang's
  i386-unknown-linux-code16 predefines equal -m32's. -march=i386 now resolves
  against i686 instead of failing 'does not support 64-bit'.
- target_registry::lookup resolves <arch>-linux-<env> to <arch>-unknown-linux-<env>
  (x86_64-linux-gnu); also fixes slate --targets for gnu short triples (slate-4j2r
  stays open for arm-linux-gnueabihf and musl). The filecheck placement check
  compares normalized triples.
- -mregparm=N: TargetAbi::default_regparm on 32-bit x86 only; AbiSignature.regparm
  prints 'x86_cdecl regparm=3(...)' on non-variadic cdecl/stdcall signatures.
  gcc and clang both pass variadic fixed args on the stack (clang's IR says inreg,
  the backend ignores it), so variadics get none. gcc rejects N > 3. The regparm(N)
  attribute is still unapplied (slate-parser-r3gp).
- Global register variables accept esp/ebp as clang's X86TargetInfo does
  (arch/x86/boot/main.c).
- Sweep keeps --target= and -mregparm=.
- Kernel sweep: ok 54 -> 80; the remaining 2979 are -mskip-rax-setup and
  -mno-red-zone (.38.4). Found: -march=i386/i486 keep the i686 CAS/LLONG
  lock-free macros (slate-parser-6x05.38.8).
