# Lower armv7 record and _Float16 va_arg

_2026-11-07 16:46:02_

slate-yar4.4: on armv7 (AAPCS32) va_arg of records and _Float16 hit 'cannot lower VaArg'. sema/abi.rs va_passing now classifies AAPCS32/AAPCS32HardFloat records as VaPassing::Memory (like i386 cdecl; records go by value on the void* cursor).
