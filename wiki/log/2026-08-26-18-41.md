# cir.std.strlen requires whole-pipeline change, deferred (slate-3i0x.11)

_2026-08-26 18:41:36_

Confirmed cir.std.strlen is reachable: running cir-opt --cir-idiom-recognizer on the raw CIR for a plain strlen(s) call raises cir.call @strlen into cir.std.strlen(%ptr, @strlen). Unlike --cir-flatten-cfg (applied selectively per-function in cir_input.rs::emit_module, only when a function actually needs it), the idiom recognizer is a whole-module pass with no per-call-site opt-out: enabling it would retroactively turn every strlen call in the entire corpus (gcc-torture, chibicc, libc-test-functional, plus any of our own fixtures) from cir.call into cir.std.strlen simultaneously. That is a pipeline-wide behavior change, not a single-op addition, and verifying it doesn't regress the corpus is out of scope for this audit ticket. Retaining the todo fallback for Op::StdStrlen; wiring the recognizer in for real is a separate, larger initiative that needs its own corpus-wide validation pass.
