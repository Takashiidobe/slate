# Report pointer-sign and discards-qualifiers warnings from IR lowering

_2026-09-19 14:59:10_

slate-parser-x5r. IR lowering can now emit warnings. `Lowerer` gains `diagnostics: Vec<SemaError>`, `diagnostic_options`, and `standard`. `Lowerer::warn` goes through `Warning::diagnose`, now pub(super). `resolve_module` returns `(Module, Vec<SemaError>)`. main passes them through the new `sema::with_sources` (extracted from the tail of `analyze`), prints the warnings, and fails when one is promoted to an error. `resolve_type_module` has no lowering diagnostics.

New warnings, both clang ExtWarns (on by default, errors under -pedantic-errors, verified with clang 22):
- `pointer-sign`: integer pointees differing only in signedness.
- `incompatible-pointer-types-discards-qualifiers`: a dropped pointee const/volatile, or pointees that are pointers differing only in nested qualifiers. The nested case (`const int ** -> int **`) used to be a hard conversion error and is now accepted.
Clang's priority is kept: sign wins over discards. Only Assign/Arg/Return conversions warn; Explicit and UsualArith (pointer comparisons) do not.

`convert`/`convert_expr` now take &mut self. Four pointer-comparison sites had to hoist their conversions out of the `self.value(...)` call.

Known gaps:
- Plain char and signed char lower to the same i8, so clang's "unique plain char" pointer-sign warning (char* <-> signed char* on x86) is not raised.
- Warnings point at the converted value's node, which is the operand's span. So `take((unsigned *)p)` points at `p`, and initializers point at the initializer rather than the declarator.
- Dropping `_Atomic` is still accepted silently, although clang rejects it.

Fixture sema/ir_pointer_conversion_warnings.c matches clang's 7 warnings. diagnostic-severity and ir-spec updated.
