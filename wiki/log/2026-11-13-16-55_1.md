Outcome: `python3 tools/corpus/e2e.py zstd --mode clang` translates libzstd and the `zstd` CLI, builds both, and the translated `zstd` matches native on 175 differential compressions and 366 round trips.

Probe 2026-10-05 had 33 barriers (`__builtin_assume`, rotates, struct-return temporaries, cpuid asm); now 0 barriers across 30 TUs, translate 1.3s vs native compile 2.1s, `cargo check` clean. The only blocker was the Box-param `mut` bug, see slate-dghn.51.1.

Recipe (tools/corpus/e2e.py): `CMakeLibrary(library="lib/libzstd.so", variants=["lib/libzstd.a"], tools=["programs/zstd"], translate_tests=True, suite=zstd_suite)`. The corpus build has `ZSTD_BUILD_TESTS=OFF`, so there is no ctest. Differential compression at `-T2 -5`, `--rsyncable`, `--ultra -22` over sources and docs. `zstd_suite` runs 6 modes (`-1 -3 -9 -19 --fast=5 -3 --long=24`) over sources, an empty file, all sources, and 256 KiB of noise: translated compress must equal native bytes, and translated `-d` of both frames and native `-d` of the translated frame must reproduce the input.

Time: test stage 9.3s translated vs 6.8s native (1.38x). Not investigated; fullbench/datagen benchmark from the bead is not added.
