# Make C11 keywords identifiers in msvc's default C mode

_2026-11-09 10:08:02_

slate-3amj.5.24: cl.exe without /std treats _Static_assert, _Alignas, _Noreturn, _Generic and _Thread_local as plain identifiers (int _Noreturn; compiles; file-scope _Static_assert is C2143), while _Alignof, _Bool and lowercase static_assert stay keywords in every C mode (int static_assert; is C2059). New fixtures: msvc/windows/x86_64/c11_keyword_spellings_are_identifiers.c, error/msvc/windows/x86_64/static-assert-without-std-c11.c, both checked against cl.exe 19.51. parser, slate and msvc profiles green.
