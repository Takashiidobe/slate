# msvc reserves predefined macros (cxg.7.27)

_2026-10-10 18:24:03_

slate-parser-cxg.7.27: msvc ignores #define/#undef (C4117) of MSVC*RESERVED_PREDEFINES (**STDC_VERSION**, **STDC_HOSTED**, **STDC_NO_ATOMICS**, **STDC_NO_COMPLEX**, **STDC_NO_VLA**, \_INTEGRAL_MAX_BITS, **FUNCTION**) while they are defined, via is_reserved_macro in pp/mod.rs. The list comes from probing cl.exe /E with every candidate name in default, /std:c11, c17 and clatest. \_MSC_VER, \_WIN32 and the other \_M* macros are not reserved.

Fixture msvc/windows/x86*64/pp_reserved_predefines.c. Parser profile 8095 passed. Follow-up filed for predefine gaps: slate lacks \_INTEGRAL_MAX_BITS, **FUNCTION** and the \_\_STDC_NO*\* trio, and defines **STDC_VERSION** in the default mode where cl does not.
