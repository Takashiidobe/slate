# msvc reserves predefined macros (cxg.7.27)

_2026-10-10 18:24:03_

slate-parser-cxg.7.27: msvc ignores #define/#undef (C4117) of MSVC_RESERVED_PREDEFINES (__STDC_VERSION__, __STDC_HOSTED__, __STDC_NO_ATOMICS__, __STDC_NO_COMPLEX__, __STDC_NO_VLA__, _INTEGRAL_MAX_BITS, __FUNCTION__) while they are defined, via is_reserved_macro in pp/mod.rs. The list comes from probing cl.exe /E with every candidate name in default, /std:c11, c17 and clatest. _MSC_VER, _WIN32 and the other _M_ macros are not reserved.

Fixture msvc/windows/x86_64/pp_reserved_predefines.c. Parser profile 8095 passed. Follow-up filed for predefine gaps: slate lacks _INTEGRAL_MAX_BITS, __FUNCTION__ and the __STDC_NO_* trio, and defines __STDC_VERSION__ in the default mode where cl does not.
