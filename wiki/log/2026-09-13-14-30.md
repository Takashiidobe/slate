# Parse GNU asm labels

_2026-09-13 14:30:44_

Ported GNU asm labels (slate-parser-25m.3, closes oxx.1.4).

- asm / **asm / **asm\_\_ ("...") after a declarator, before attributes and
  initializer, is parsed in src/parser/asm.rs into Declaration.asm_label
  and Decl::Typedef.asm_label as Span<AsmLabel>.
- AsmLabel::Register(decode_register) when storage is register, else
  AsmLabel::Symbol. Adjacent narrow strings concatenate; wide/unicode
  strings, missing strings, and volatile/inline/goto qualifiers error.
- Struct fields reject asm labels with clang's 'expected ;' message.
- Preprocessor fix: a one-parameter function-like macro invoked as F()
  now receives one empty argument instead of failing arity and staying
  unexpanded. GCC's ASMNAME(STRING(**USER_LABEL_PREFIX**)) relied on it.
- tools/update_filecheck.py now also rewrites the absolute temp path in
  error diagnostics; tempdirs under /tmp leaked random paths into CHECKs.
- Pre-existing: 20031002-1.c and 200031109-1.c have no CHECK lines
  (filed slate-parser-oxx.1.11).
