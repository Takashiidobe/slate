# Lower AArch64 x19/x29 register-variable asm operands

_2026-10-28 14:35:42_

slate-3f8g.4.29: register long r asm("x19") (or x29) as an asm operand barriered with 'reserved register operand'. clang and gcc accept both in leaf functions (clang spills x29 around the asm). Lowerer (lowerer/asm.rs lower_asm): the operand becomes a non-late reg operand s and gets a non-late out(reg) _ scratch t, sharing the saved_reserved path with x19/x29 clobbers (slate-3f8g.4.27): mov t, R and (input) mov R, s before the template; (output) mov s, R and mov R, t after. Template references print the register itself through Slot::Register (x19, w19 under %w). A second operand on the same reserved register stays a barrier.
