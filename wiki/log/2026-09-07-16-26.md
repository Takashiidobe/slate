# fold asm lateout temp + copy into direct named output operand

_2026-09-07 16:26:20_

Closes slate-dx0m.7. New NodeRule AsmOutputTempFold (inline_temps.rs), keyed
on Assign nodes: matches `named = tmp;` immediately following an InlineAsm
stmt (unwrapping the enclosing `unsafe { }` single-stmt block) where tmp is
an uninitialized `let __vN: T;` bound as a lateout/out(InOut) operand, used
nowhere else, and named is a non-temp plain var not itself read by the asm.
Rewrites the operand's bound identifier to named, drops the Let and Assign.

Gotcha: the asm stmt sits inside an `unsafe { }` wrapper in the arena, so a
plain "is prev sibling InlineAsm" check always failed; had to reuse
unwrap_single_stmt to see through it.

Also had to restructure apply() to do all arena mutations only after every
validation passes - an early `return false` after a prior get_mut/mutation
would violate the engine's apply-must-not-mutate-on-false invariant.

Verified on inline_asm_demo.c and asm_ebx_output_cpuid.c: eax/ecx fold into
`lateout("eax") eax`/`lateout("ecx") ecx` directly; the edi ebx-clobber temp
(no real named destination) is left untouched, as designed. Regenerated both
fixtures with update_filecheck.py --profile both. Full rewrites profile:
85/85 pass. fmt/clippy clean.
