# Generate gcc aarch64/arm target builtins from a gcc plugin

_2026-11-01 20:56:41_

slate-parser-cxg.43 (arm half): the aarch64 and arm tables cannot be derived from .def files (modes, qualifiers and the arm_neon.h pragma are expanded in C++), so tools/gcc_plugin/dump_builtins.cc runs inside aarch64-linux-gnu-gcc and arm-none-eabi-gcc after compiling the NEON/ACLE/FP16/BF16 (and MVE/CDE) headers and dumps every BUILT_IN_MD decl, the builtin types the headers name, and the NEON tuple structs. dump_gcc_builtins.py rebuilds the plugin and rewrites tools/gcc_plugin/dumps/*.tsv (committed, so regeneration needs no gcc); generate_gcc_arm_builtins.py turns them into src/sema/gcc_arm_builtins.rs (GCC_AARCH64_BUILTINS, GCC_ARM_BUILTINS, *_TYPES, GCC_AARCH64_TUPLES). gcc_target_builtin now resolves both families. Builtin type names (__Int8x8_t, __builtin_aarch64_simd_hf, __simd64_int8_t, ...) are accepted by is_target_builtin_name and resolved via gcc_target_type in the gcc flavor only. aarch64 NEON tuple structs (int8x8x2_t, ...) are declared by the predeclared_typedefs prelude and registered in builtin_typedefs so prototypes can name them. arm_neon.h now parses and resolves for aarch64 and armv7 under --flavor=gcc. SVE/SME rows are skipped (sizeless types); lowering of the builtin calls is a backend matter. Fixtures: gcc/linux/aarch64/neon_builtin_types.c, gcc/linux/armv7-unknown-linux-gnueabihf/neon_builtin_types.c.
