# AArch64 +@cc and flag operand references

_2026-11-01 21:44:55_

slate-3f8g.4.38: on aarch64 clang the flags operand is NZCV itself: +@cc<cond> runs 'msr NZCV, x' from the place before the template and %w0 prints nzcv (no modifier is rejected); gcc rejects +@cc there. Lowerer adds a hidden in(reg) u64 and prepends 'msr nzcv, {N:x}', and prints nzcv for %w<N> on a flags operand. Also dropped preserves_flags for any asm with a flags operand (aarch64 defaulted it on, wrong since the asm sets NZCV). Fixture: aarch64/clang_asm_aarch64_flag_readwrite.c; asm_aarch64_flag_outputs.c regenerated. Doc: wiki/concepts/ir/asm.md.
