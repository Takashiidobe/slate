# Migrate comments off nodes removed by rewrites

_2026-10-07 14:56:44_

slate-6cgg.3.

Arena gains one comment-migration helper: release_comments(id, replacement), retire(id, replacement) (release, unlink from the parent list by id, take) and retire_subtree(id, replacement) (also moves comments inside the subtree). A node's leading run (Leading comment siblings directly before it) and trailing run (Trailing siblings directly after it) go before the replacement node; with no replacement, trailing becomes leading so it binds to the next surviving sibling, and when no non-comment sibling follows they become detached (the block's inner-trailing slot). Comments interior to a discarded subtree move before the replacement as leading.

Every rule that removed nodes now uses it: inline_temps (consumer is the replacement; asm_output_fold uses the asm), zero_init (the let), return_cleanup (the return), for_range and getenv_var (the new loop/if), bool_ternary, loop_to_while, structure_dispatch (the rebuilt switch statement; old body comments land before it), singleton_scopes and label_elide (no replacement). The index-based list edits and their now-dead fields (parent, list_index, *_pos, let_parent, remove_from_list, walk::remove_from_parent) are gone; retire locates the node by id so earlier comment moves cannot shift positions.

Dispatcher lowering: a local hoisted to a __slate_slot carries its leading and trailing comments onto the synthesized slot let (before the storage let) instead of leaving them on the next statement. Diverted trailing comment nodes still emit their block's terminal jump.
