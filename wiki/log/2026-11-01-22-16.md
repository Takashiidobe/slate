# SME keyword attributes parsed and refused

_2026-11-01 22:16:49_

slate-3f8g.4.33: **arm_streaming, **arm_streaming_compatible,
**arm_locally_streaming, **arm_new, **arm_in/out/inout/preserves parse on
AArch64 as Attribute::Sme(SmeAttribute{kind,states}) (parser/attributes.rs).
clang uses real keywords; gcc predefines them as [[**extension** arm::x]]
macros, so the C23 group now takes a leading **extension** and maps arm:: names.
States must be za/zt0, else Invalid. Recorded as FunctionSemantics.sme and
printed [sme=kind(states)]; on function-pointer/typedef declarators they parse
and are dropped. slate refuses any function with one (Tables::reject_sme_state,
definitions and extern decls): 'SME function state'. Fixtures: clang+gcc
aarch64/sme_keyword_attributes.c,
error/clang/linux/aarch64/sme_keyword_attribute_bad_state.c. Docs:
attributes.md, ir/declarations.md, ir-grammar.md, ast-grammar.md, ir/asm.md.
clang rejects trailing **arm_new/\_\_arm_locally_streaming; we accept them.
Parser and slate profiles green.
