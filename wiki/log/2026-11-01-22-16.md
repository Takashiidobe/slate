# SME keyword attributes parsed and refused

_2026-11-01 22:16:49_

slate-3f8g.4.33: __arm_streaming, __arm_streaming_compatible, __arm_locally_streaming, __arm_new, __arm_in/out/inout/preserves parse on AArch64 as Attribute::Sme(SmeAttribute{kind,states}) (parser/attributes.rs). clang uses real keywords; gcc predefines them as [[__extension__ arm::x]] macros, so the C23 group now takes a leading __extension__ and maps arm:: names. States must be za/zt0, else Invalid. Recorded as FunctionSemantics.sme and printed [sme=kind(states)]; on function-pointer/typedef declarators they parse and are dropped. slate refuses any function with one (Tables::reject_sme_state, definitions and extern decls): 'SME function state'. Fixtures: clang+gcc aarch64/sme_keyword_attributes.c, error/clang/linux/aarch64/sme_keyword_attribute_bad_state.c. Docs: attributes.md, ir/declarations.md, ir-grammar.md, ast-grammar.md, ir/asm.md. clang rejects trailing __arm_new/__arm_locally_streaming; we accept them. Parser and slate profiles green.
