# Linux kernel added to the c-corpus

_2026-10-04 10:16:36_

Added Linux (torvalds/linux shallow clone, 7.3-rc5 6addb4f38, x86_64 defconfig) to ~/c-corpus under epic slate-parser-6x05.38.
- c_corpus_setup.py: 'linux' Configure recipe (make O=build-<flavor> CC= HOSTCC= defconfig, then the full build under bear); Configure's make args are now format-expanded. Clang build: 4 min, 3059 C TUs.
- pp_diff.kept_args: keeps -m16/-m32/-m64/-mx32 and language-semantic -f flags (SEMANTIC_FLAGS); drops -mllvm/-Xclang with their value (-mllvm was swallowing the next -D). Without these, clang rejected 2666 kernel TUs (struct filename size needs -fms-anonymous-structs; counted_by needs late-parse attributes; 16/32-bit TUs need -m16/-m32).
- sweep: linux gets -idirafter /usr/include for host tools (gelf.h, openssl).
- Baseline: oracle accepts every TU; slate ok=54 rejected=3005, all on unhandled flags. 55 of the kernel's 160 distinct flags are unknown to compiler_args; filed by policy as 6x05.38.1 (target selection), .2 (language semantics), .3 (codegen flags with predefine effects, from a clang -dM diff), .4 (pure codegen/driver flags to ignore).
- Policy (user): never skip TUs or drop flags in the sweep to make a project pass; every flag needs a compiler_args policy. Sweep filtering is a stopgap.
- Side effect: musl (-nostdinc -ffreestanding) and mimalloc (-fno-builtin-malloc) now hit unhandled flags and leave the trophy case until .2 lands; README not changed yet.
