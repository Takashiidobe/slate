# Globals, linkage, thread storage, symbol attributes

_2026-09-18 19:01:09_

slate-parser-lh7.2.9. names.rs: NameResolution.declarations maps every declaration node to its binding, so Lowerer::declaration_id no longer falls back to the first binding with that name. A Resolver.linked table gives every declaration with linkage (file-scope objects/functions, block-scope extern objects, block-scope function prototypes) one binding per name, even when the block-scope extern comes first or a local shadows it (C11 6.2.2p4). sema/module.rs: declare_global and declare_function merge by BindingId, replacing the merge-by-name loop and Lowerer.local_statics. Functions used to be emitted once per redeclaration, and 'static f(); f(){}' came out external; now there is one entry and internal linkage sticks. is_thread_local was never read before, so \_Thread_local silently lowered as static; now it, **thread, and **declspec(thread) give storage=thread, and are invalid on automatic locals. New ir::SymbolAttributes on Global and Function (asm_name, visibility, weak, alias, section, used, retain, tls_model, dllimport/dllexport), merged first-wins across redeclarations; alias makes a declaration a definition. Other declaration attributes stay Unsupported; follow-up bead for aligned/common/weakref/selectany. Fixture sema/ir_globals_linkage.c; ir_call_signatures.c now shows one merged @old.
