# __builtin_types_compatible_p

_2026-09-18 20:08:06_

slate-parser-lh7.2.12 (slice). TypeResolver::types_compatible folds the builtin in both IR lowering (const<i32>(0|1) [types_compatible="A, B"]) and constant_value, so static_assert conditions using it now fold (the TypesCompatible half of eb8). IR type equality alone is wrong: long/long long and char/signed char share i64/i8, and typedefs resolve away. So each side is resolved with its top-level qualifiers cleared at the declarator (new Declarator::pointer_qualifiers_mut via a shared outermost_layer_mut walk; else the specifier qualifiers, which also clears array element qualifiers like clang's getUnqualifiedType; _Atomic kept), then both the IR type and the canonical C spelling must match. Leading qualifier words left by a qualified typedef are stripped from non-pointer spellings. An enum is compatible with an integer equal to its underlying IR type. Verified all 11 fixture cases against clang and gcc (11110001111). Known gap: a typedef of a top-level-qualified pointer (typedef int *const P) keeps its qualifier. Fixture sema/ir_types_compatible.c.
