# Reject unknown register names on register-bound variables

_2026-10-28 17:27:53_

Both gcc and clang reject these. Moved gcc/linux/x86_64/asm-label-unknown-register.c to error/{gcc,clang}/linux/{x86_64,aarch64}/; dropped the notareg lines from gcc asm-sema.c and error/clang asm-sema.c since the parse error hid their other sema errors.
