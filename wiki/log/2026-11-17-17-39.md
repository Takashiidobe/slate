Outcome: the AArch64 lane-indexed dot/complex-multiply builtins, scalar vqrdmlah/vqrdmlsh, vsha1c/m/p/h, vxarq_u64, the SM3 family and the f16 across-vector reductions now translate, run under qemu and match clang.

Gap and why it mattered: slate-yar4.53 lowered only the vector-only forms. The calls below still stopped at "builtin without a lowering" under `-march=armv8.5-a+crypto+dotprod+fp16`.

Before: each of these calls was a "builtin without a lowering" barrier, as listed under Limits in the slate-yar4.53 log.

After: `vdotq_lane_s32` is a lane splat shuffle feeding `llvm.aarch64.neon.sdot.v4i32.v16i8`; `vsha1cq_u32` calls `llvm.aarch64.crypto.sha1c` with the scalar hash_e; `vxarq_u64(a, b, 17)` calls `llvm.aarch64.crypto.xar` with an i64 immediate; `vmaxvq_f16` calls `llvm.aarch64.neon.fmaxv.f16.v8f16`.

Implementation (crates/slate-parser/src/sema/neon_builtins.rs):
- `NeonOp::Splat { quad }` lowers `__builtin_neon_splat{q}_lane{q}_v`, which arm_neon.h uses for every `_lane`/`_laneq` dot and vcmla form. It is a `shuffle` that repeats lane `index % lanes`, sized to 64 or 128 bits by `quad`.
- `NeonOp::Scalar(name)`: scalar-operand intrinsics passed through as is (vqrdmlahs/vqrdmlshs_s32 -> sqrdmlah/sqrdmlsh.i32, vsha1c/m/pq_u32, vsha1h_u32).
- `NeonOp::ScalarVector(name)`: vqrdmlahh/vqrdmlshh_s16 have no i16 scalar intrinsic. Each operand is splatted to `<4 x i16>`, the v4i16 intrinsic is called and lane 0 is read back, as clang does.
- `NeonOp::Immediate(name)`: the trailing constant becomes an i64 operand (vxarq_u64, vsm3tt1a/1b/2a/2b).
- `NeonOp::HalfAcross(name)`: vmaxv/vminv/vmaxnmv/vminnmv{q}_f16. The operand is reinterpreted as an f16 vector, then fmaxv/fminv/fmaxnmv/fminnmv is called.
- vsm3partw1q/vsm3partw2q/vsm3ss1q go through `Direct`.

Fixture: crates/slate/tests/fixtures/aarch64/neon_lane_scalar_builtins.c (saturating vqrdmlahh/vqrdmlshs edges, ±65504 and -0.0 f16 lanes).

Limits: the bead also said `__builtin_arm_crc32*` maps to llvm.aarch64 names on Arm32. That cannot happen: armv7 accepts only +fp/+simd, and without +crc `__builtin_arm_crc32b` is "use of unknown builtin" in sema, so `NeonOp::Crc` is never reached there. No change.
