# lexer: pp-numbers include underscores, fixing name##2_cb pastes (ryfo.9)

_2026-09-30 08:56:07_

slate-parser-ryfo.9. Lexer::numeric_end now accepts _ inside a number, so a pp-number (C11 6.4.8: digits, letters, _, ., and a sign after e/E/p/P) such as 2_cb or 1_2_y lexes as one token. Before, 2_cb split into IntLit 2 and Ident _cb, so libuv's name##2_cb pasted only idle+2 and left _cb as a separate token (idle2 _cb).

On its own, 2_cb now takes the same path as 2cb already did (a FloatLit spelling that sema rejects as an invalid suffix), matching clang's tokenization. Pasting onto it re-lexes to one identifier.

The only non-same rows left are clang-rejects and curl's 105 slate-errors (__has_declspec_attribute, slate-parser-dyd.67).
