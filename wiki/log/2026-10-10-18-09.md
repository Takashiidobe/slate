# Lex :: as one token per flavor and standard (cxg.7.22)

_2026-10-10 18:09:19_

slate-parser-cxg.7.22: added Token::ColonColon, lexed when StandardFeatures::scope_token is set: clang and msvc always, gcc in gnu modes and C23 (strict gcc C89..C17 keeps two Colons, as gcc does). ': ## :' now pastes to '::' there.

Consumers: [[ns::attr]] (parser/attributes.rs), #embed vendor parameters and __has_c_attribute scoped names (pp/embed.rs, pp/mod.rs) accept either form through TokenSpanExt::scope_len_at. GNU asm (parser/asm.rs) treats '::' as two section separators via consume_asm_colon, so '::', ':::' and '::::' still split sections. Other Colon sites (labels, ?:, bit-fields, designators) need no change: '1 ? 2 :: 3' now fails like gcc.

Fixtures: gcc/linux/x86_64/scope_token_{paste,asm}.c, clang/linux/x86_64/scope_token_paste.c. Parser profile: 8081 passed before the fixtures, new ones pass. Wiki: preprocessor.md.

Follow-up filed: slate never diagnoses invalid pastes (gcc errors on strict ': ## :' and '+ ## ;').
