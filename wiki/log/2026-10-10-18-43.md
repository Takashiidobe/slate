# gcc decimal float inf/nan/nans builtins (cxg.8.1)

_2026-10-10 18:43:54_

slate-parser-cxg.8.1: added __builtin_{inf,nan,nans}d{32,64,128} for the gcc flavor. clang has none, so they live in a small GCC_DECIMAL_BUILTINS table in sema/builtins.rs (built by the gcc_decimal_builtins! macro) consulted by clang_builtin before the library-prefix fallback. They are retained calls returning d32/d64/d128, memory=none for inf and read for nan/nans. has_checks::has_builtin now takes the flavor so __has_builtin reports them for gcc only.

gcc's float.h defines DEC_INFINITY, DEC_NAN and DEC*_SNAN with these builtins, which is what gcc.dg/dfp/c23-float-dfp-4..8 needed. Fixture gcc/linux/x86_64/builtin_decimal_inf_nan.c plus the five migrated gcc-dg files. Parser profile 8122 passed.
