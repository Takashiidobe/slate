# Drop -Wno-implicit-function-declaration from shim builds

_2026-09-01 22:47:38_

Removed the -Wno-implicit-function-declaration flag from all three
build.rs templates in tests/support/mod.rs. That flag was what let a
missing libc header in a generated shim degrade into a silent
implicit-int declaration (the strtold garbage-return bug) instead of a
compile error. With headers now derived correctly for both the typed and
name-based shim paths, the flag only masks future regressions of the same
class. All three nextest profiles (lowering, rewrites, libc) build and
pass without it.
