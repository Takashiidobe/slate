# Atomic read-modify-write and compare-exchange lowering

_2026-10-02 19:20:07_

slate-dghn.4. Atomic update<T, result=old|new, atomic=o> and compare_exchange were barriers (redis: ~45 functions in 9 TUs once packed records landed).

frontend/lowerer/atomics.rs: lower_atomic_rmw dispatches both. AtomicTarget picks the Rust atomic and representation: values, *mut pointers (AtomicPtr), or f32/f64 bits (AtomicU32/U64 with to_bits/from_bits); atomic_wrapper is shared with lower_atomic_access, which now also handles f32/f64 load/store.
- Native: native_rmw matches add|sub|and|or|xor(old, x) (commuted for add/and/or/xor), not(and(old, x)), and old-free computations -> fetch_add/sub/and/or/xor/nand/swap, with x bound once in a let; result=new recombines old and x (wrapping_add/sub, &, |, ^, !(&)).
- Loop: other computations become Atomic*::fetch_update(order, load order, |cur| Some(f(cur))).unwrap_or_else(|v| v), with ValueKind::OldValue lowered from the new FunctionLowerer::old_value. Only for pure f (update_children whitelist), since the loop re-evaluates it.
- CAS: compare_exchange[_weak]; write_back writes the observed value through the expected pointer only in the Err arm; success -> is_ok(); old -> either value.
Barriers kept: dynamic order/weakness, non-system scope, volatile updates, fn-pointer/i128 updates, impure loop computations.

Fixture atomic_rmw_redis.c mirrors redis (redisAtomic fields, atomicIncr/atomicGetIncr, __atomic_*_fetch, nand, *= <<= /= on _Atomic int, atomic float/double, _Atomic pointer ++, weak CAS claim loop, __sync_*, 4 pthreads). Promoted atomics, atomic_widths, atomic_explicit_orderings, atomic_fallbacks, atomic_flag_ops, gnu_builtin_atomic_flag, gnu_builtins_atomic, mt-atomics, gnu_legacy_sync_builtins, 13 gcc-dg atomic__* (compare-exchange-1..4, exchange-1..4, flag, op-1..4), gcc-torture pr122000.
Wiki: ir-to-rust-lowering.md Atomics.
Redis: no atomic barriers; 14 TUs / 38 functions blocked (was 20 / 80).
Tests: slate profile had only the ratchet promotions failing (applied, no rerun).
