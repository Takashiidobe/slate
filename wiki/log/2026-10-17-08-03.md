# Lower aggregate va_arg on SysV x86-64 and VaList lifetimes (slate-o8j2)

_2026-10-17 08:03:55_

slate-parser: ValueKind::VaArg carries a VaPassing (ir/abi.rs): regs=[int|sse|sse_wide per eightbyte] or memory for record va_arg on the SysV x86-64 convention, computed in AbiClassifier::va_passing from the existing sysv_leaves/sysv_classify; scalars print nothing. slate: va_arg with non-scalar passing lowers to **slate_sysv_va::arg (new support/sysv_va.rs), which reads the **va_list_tag directly: register save area while enough GP/SSE registers remain, overflow area (8/16-aligned) otherwise. Record va_arg on other targets is now an unsupported barrier instead of a rustc E0277. VaList is printed as VaList<'static> everywhere so struct fields and statics compile; va_start transmutes the \_\_va_args clone to 'static, and clones/next_arg on static places go through \*addr_of_mut! to avoid static_mut_refs. structs holding a VaList no longer derive Copy. va_arg of f32/i8/u8 reads the promoted type and casts (va-arg-trap-1). Promoted 19 gcc-torture fixtures; added x86_64/va_arg_aggregates.c and parser va_arg_passing.c (clang, gcc). Follow-up: i686 and aarch64 aggregate va_arg (slate-kih1.2).
