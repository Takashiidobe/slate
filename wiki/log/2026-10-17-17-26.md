# Lower switch on \_BitInt wider than 128 bits (slate-6uvv.2)

_2026-10-17 17:26:38_

A switch whose selector is a \_BitInt wider than 128 bits cannot match on an integer, and its case values do not fit i128. lower_indexed_switch evaluates the selector once into a temp, builds an if/else chain of const compare() checks (== for a case, >= && <= for a range, || across labels) that yields the arm index (or default / -1), and reuses the fallthrough loop (now lower_case_loop) to dispatch on that index.

Promoted bitint_arith, switch_case_wide_bitint and switch_case_wide_bitint_range; all three are listed for aarch64 where clang rejects \_BitInt > 128. Removed the 'wider than 128 bits' switch barrier, now unreachable.
