# Lower AArch64 zero-register asm operands

_2026-10-28 14:16:19_

slate-3f8g.4.25: a zero immediate under %x/%w barriered with 'immediate modifier Some('x')'. gcc and clang both print xzr/wzr for any zero immediate under %x/%w (gcc prints 0 unmodified; clang moves rZ zero into a register).
