# slate-e36k.20 lower_call: floating elementwise min and max variants have no lowering

_2026-11-24 11:57:28_

- bead: slate-e36k.20
- kind: bug
- area: crates/slate/tests/fixtures.unsupported

Outcome: The unsupported ratchet covers the four floating elementwise minimum and maximum variants.

With a quiet NaN and a number, minimum and maximum propagate NaN
while their Number variants return the number. With signed zeros,
the minimum picks negative zero and the maximum picks positive
zero. Lean checks both distinctions in the binary32 model.

Clang exits 0 on all four Linux targets. Slate recognizes the
builtin names in typed IR, but lower_call reports that none of
the four has a lowering. Triage reports the first barrier.
