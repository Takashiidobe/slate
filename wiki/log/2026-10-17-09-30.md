# Fold neg of f80 constants into long double literals

_2026-10-17 09:30:05_

slate-kih1.6. A static initializer like `volatile long double nldinf = -__builtin_infl();` (IR neg<f80>(const<f80>(+Inf))) was rejected as non-constant because f80 unary ops go through the __slate_f80_neg shim. The lowerer now lowers neg<f80>(const<f80>(bits)) directly as long_double_literal(bits ^ 1<<79), flipping the x87 sign bit, and Tables::is_constant_initializer accepts exactly that shape for long double (other f80 unary ops stay non-constant). This is the slate side: sema still never folds ordinary arithmetic per ir/pipeline.

Function bodies get the same literal instead of a shim call; regenerated FileChecks for intrinsic_float_overloads, long_double_function_pointer, long_double_va_arg, x86_64/long_double_classify, x86_64/long_double_shim_basics (-0.0L becomes [.., 128]). Promoted gcc-dg torture__float128-extend-inf. slate and slate-i686 profiles green.
