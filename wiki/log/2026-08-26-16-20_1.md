# Lower CIR rotate and zero-sized copies

_2026-08-26 16:20:12_

clang-ir-types already represented cir.rotate as a typed Rotate op, but Slate lacked dispatch and emitted invalid fallback identifiers. Added direct rotate_left and rotate_right lowering. Clang models empty-struct parameter initialization as a copy from a poison global; zero-sized copies are now omitted. integer_bit_builtin_ops and gnu_empty_struct pass raw release lowering.
