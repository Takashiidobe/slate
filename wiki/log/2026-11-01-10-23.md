# Report why each macro expansion stays inline

_2026-11-01 10:23:04_

slate-f2zu.9.5.12.1: translate-project --merge stdout (the merge report) now ends its unit list with a macros section: totals per reason, then lifted and inline counts per project function-like macro. Reasons are one enum, MacroInlineReason (rust_ast.rs). The lowerer records rejections once per expansion (macro_regions.rs), macro_fold records lifted and its own rejections, and invocations never reached get no_statement_region, empty_expansion or inside_argument. Counts are per distinct compile, so reused assignments do not count. System-header and object-like macros are filtered out.
