# gcc: #pragma STDC FLOAT_CONST_DECIMAL64 makes unsuffixed float constants _Decimal64

_2026-10-24 09:44:42_

slate-parser-cxg.8.3. gcc.dg/dfp/pragma-float-const-decimal64-{1,2,3} failed with 'invalid operands f64 * d64': the pragma parsed as Opaque and unsuffixed constants stayed double.

gcc rules (probed): under ON an unsuffixed decimal floating constant is _Decimal64; hex constants, imaginary constants (2.0i stays _Complex double) and the d suffix stay binary; DEFAULT is OFF. Block-scoped, restored at the end of the compound statement. Valid only at file scope or before the first item of a compound statement, otherwise ignored with -Wpragmas (not emitted). clang ignores the pragma. gcc segfaults on '.5' under the pragma, so leading-dot constants are not covered by a fixture.

- ast.rs: StdcPragmaOption::FloatConstDecimal64 (parser/decl.rs). FloatingPragmas::apply ignores it for every flavor.
- pragmas::collect (gcc flavor) runs a Decimal64Literals visitor with the checker's placement rule (compound_start, statement expressions count as compounds) and stores the affected literal NodeIds in Pragmas.
- TypeResolver::literal and literal_type (sema/operand.rs) give those literals the DecimalF64 suffix, so typing and value resolution agree. The AST keeps the spelled suffix. New FloatLiteral::is_unsuffixed_decimal (const_expr.rs).
- Fixtures: gcc/linux/x86_64/pragma_float_const_decimal64.c (self-checking _Static_assert/_Generic, verified with gcc), clang/linux/x86_64/pragma_float_const_decimal64_ignored.c; migrated dfp__pragma-float-const-decimal64-{1,2,3}.c. Regenerated dfp__pragma-float-const-decimal64-{5,6}.c and cpp__pragma-float-const-decimal64-1.c: literals after ON are now d64 converted to f64 (checked against gcc).
- Not fixed: gcc rejects 0x1.0p1dd and 1.0idd (pragma-float-const-decimal64-7.c), slate accepts; predates this change.
- Parser gate 8359/8362 before regenerating those three fixtures. Docs: ir/operations (Floating pragmas), ast-grammar.
