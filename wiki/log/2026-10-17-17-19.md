# Lower \_BitInt shifts through const shl_bits/shr_bits (slate-6uvv.1)

_2026-10-17 17:19:53_

Wide \_BitInt << and >> now lower to the bitint crate's const fns shl_bits/shr_bits instead of the Shl/Shr traits, so they are legal in static initializers. The shift amount is cast to usize; a \_BitInt amount goes through to_u128/to_i128 first.

Small non-native \_BitInt shifts (N<64) use wrapping_shl/wrapping_shr in the native container; left shifts re-normalize through narrow_integer. Before this, a << n left stray high bits in the container.

New fixture bitint_shift_widths (14 widths, int and \_BitInt amounts, compound assigns, static initializers), listed for aarch64 where clang rejects \_BitInt > 128. Regenerated FileCheck for bitint_shift (compound shifts now expand to a = a.shl_bits(..)).
