# Header pruning keeps declarations referenced from bit-field widths

_2026-10-05 11:39:19_

slate-parser-6x05.38.25. reachability.rs marked a field's type and declarator but not its bit_width, so a header function referenced only from a width was pruned. Kernel: u8 x : order_base_2(...) expands to ilog2(), whose non-constant arm calls __ilog2_u32 from linux/log2.h. Name resolution then failed for the width, the record definition failed without a diagnostic, and every later member access said 'member of incomplete or non-record' (intel_dp_link_caps.c, reported first at a static_assert over ((struct X *)NULL)->m). mark_tag now marks bit-field widths. Follow-up worth a bead: a failed record definition should report its own error rather than surface later as an incomplete type. Fixture: clang/linux/x86_64/bitfield_width_header_function.{c,h}.
