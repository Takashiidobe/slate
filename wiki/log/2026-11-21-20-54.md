Outcome: structure-goto moves a label out of a plain block or an if/else arm when a goto outside that container targets it, and forwards gotos aimed at a label whose statement is just another goto; structure_goto::reducible drops from 57 / 47 files to 49 / 41.

Gap: the IR transform only structured gotos whose label was a sibling of the goto's ancestor. Gotos into `{ ... L: ... }`, into an if arm, or to `L: goto M;` went to lower_dispatch and the Rust reducible rule.

Implementation (crates/slate-analysis/src/transform/structure_goto.rs):
- `StructureGoto::lift` runs in `list` before `labels`, bottom-up, so a label moves out one level at a time until it becomes a sibling of its gotos. `escaping` finds the first top-level `Label` whose goto count exceeds the jumps inside that list.
- Block: `{A; L: B}` becomes `{A}` then `L: B` at the parent level. Declarations in A that B uses are hoisted with the .10.6 `movable`/`extract` (cleanup and VLA in A bail). `scoped` wraps B in a block when it declares cleanup or variably modified locals, so their scope does not grow.
- If arm: `if (c) {A; L: B} else {E}` becomes `if (c) {A} else {E; goto join}` then `L: B; join:`. No join is added when the other arm ends in a jump. It bails when a goto to L comes after the if, because the backward loop would swallow `join`. Gotos counted per arm, so a goto in the sibling arm (gcc-torture 20030909-1) lifts.
- `Forwards`/`forward`: a label whose first statement (through stacked labels) is `goto M` has its gotos retargeted to M, with cycles left alone (gcc-dg c23-labels-1, c-testsuite case_00010).
