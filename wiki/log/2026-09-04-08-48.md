# Audit C23 extended binary math for slate-5exy.3.8

_2026-09-04 08:48:46_

Audited the C23 TS 18661-3 library surface for `_Float16` (strfromf16/strtof16 and the `f16`-suffixed math.h functions) since strfromf/strfromd/strfroml were already done.

Confirmed unavailable on the configured toolchain, not just gated behind strict-mode flags: neither libc-shim nor the host's real glibc headers declare any f16-suffixed symbol, even with __STDC_WANT_IEC_60559_TYPES_EXT__/_GNU_SOURCE defined. Root cause is glibc's own bits/floatn-common.h hardcoding `__HAVE_FLOAT16 0`; `nm -D` on libc.so.6/libm.so.6 confirms no such symbols are exported at all, so no reference binary could exist to diff against even if a declaration were forced.

No code change: nothing is exposed to fixture, so there's nothing to add to the shim. Documented in wiki/concepts/float16-and-fenv.md under a new "TS 18661-3 library surface for _Float16" section, closing slate-5exy.3.8 with this as the capability audit the acceptance criteria asked for.
