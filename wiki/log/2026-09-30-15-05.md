# Float/double types and FloatBits constants in slate frontend

_2026-09-30 15:05:22_

slate-p58o.3.6. lower_type maps FloatType F32/F64 to f32/f64; other formats (F16, BF16, F80, F128, decimal) stay barriers for Phase 4.

Number::FloatBits constants are emitted as suffixed literals from the type's own Debug formatting ({value:?}f32 / {value:?}f64). Debug is shortest round-trip for that type and switches to exponent notation at extremes, so literals are bit-exact and readable (0.1f32, 5e-324f64, 1e300f64). The suffix makes rustc parse directly at the target width, avoiding the double rounding of '0.1 as f32'. Negative literals become Unary(Neg) over the magnitude so HexFloat stays an atom; inf/NaN use {f32,f64}::from_bits(0x...) to keep the payload. Unary neg on f32/f64 lowers to '-x' (IEEE sign flip in both languages).

Not covered: INFINITY/NAN/HUGE_VAL expand to __builtin_inff()-style calls, which lower as plain extern calls outside unsafe (direct-call handling, 3.10).

Added tests/fixtures/float_constants.c (subnormals, FLT/DBL max/min, -0.0, literal overflow to inf, %a output).
