Outcome: Lowering emits source binding mutability from IR def-use facts.
The Rust worklist demotion rule and parameter sweep are gone.

Before:
    let mut shared: [i32; 2] = [2, 4];
    sum(shared.as_slice());

After:
    let shared: [i32; 2] = [2, 4];
    sum(shared.as_slice());

DefUse distinguishes initialization, projected reads, writes, and direct
address or fixed-array arguments. Lowering can emit an immutable binding
when a direct argument becomes a shared borrow. Mutable borrows of owned
places, opaque escapes, and new Rust writes retain mutability. OpenMP
firstprivate marks the binding that receives its generated assignment.

decl-init turns an uninitialized scalar followed by two branch writes
into a conditional initializer. The branch prefixes stay scoped with the
selected arm. Lowering emits LetIf from that form. Rust rewrites that
replace declarations decide the mutability of their own new bindings.
