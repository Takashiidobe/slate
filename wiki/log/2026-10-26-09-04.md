# Emulate complex arithmetic range flags: -fcomplex-arithmetic, -fcx-fortran-rules, range=improved

_2026-10-26 09:04:17_

slate-3amj.7: complex mul/div range now follows -fcx-limited-range, -fcx-fortran-rules (gcc, clang), clang -fcomplex-arithmetic=basic|improved|full|promoted, -ffast-math/-Ofast and -ffp-model, in argv order with each compiler's quirks (gcc: -ffast-math beats an earlier -fno-cx-limited-range, -fno-fast-math resets only without an earlier explicit cx flag, -Ofast acts first). MathOptions.cx_limited_range became complex_range + explicit_complex_range (compiler_options.rs).
IR: ComplexRange::Improved, printed range=improved. Clang's promoted never reaches the IR: sema (numeric.rs promoted_division) widens both operands with excess-precision conversions, divides with range=basic and narrows; promoted_complex_format picks f16->f32 (no excess precision), f32->f64, f64->f80/f128 long double. With no wider type (double on windows-msvc, long double) it falls back to improved and warns higher-precision-for-complex-division once per TU (assertion.rs), with clang's 'additional uses' suffix.
Static/thread initializers always fold with range=full, as clang and gcc do (module.rs). Pragma CX_LIMITED_RANGE DEFAULT restores the command-line range. gcc's __GCC_IEC_559_COMPLEX/__STDC_IEC_559_COMPLEX__ follow the range (pp/math.rs); -fcx-limited-range -fno-cx-fortran-rules used to give 0 wrongly.
Backend: lowerer/complex.rs takes the range: mul is plain unless full; div is c_div(basic) / c_div_improved (Smith, clang's operation order, |c| ugt |d|) / c_div(full). c_div_improved in vendor num-complex for f32/f64/f16/f128; __slate_cf80_div_improved in shims/long_double.c. float_fold does not fold improved division.
Fixtures: slate-parser clang/linux/x86_64/complex_arithmetic.c, clang/windows/x86_64/complex_arithmetic_promoted.c, gcc/linux/x86_64/cx_fortran_rules.c, error fixtures for unknown values and gcc/msvc rejection; slate flag_complex_arithmetic_{improved,promoted}.c, gcc/flag_cx_fortran_rules_gcc.c, promoted flag_complex_arithmetic_basic.c. Six f80 fixtures regenerated for the new shim extern.
