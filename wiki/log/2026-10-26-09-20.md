# Keep fenv shims opaque under cross-language LTO

_2026-10-26 09:20:50_

slate-3amj.47 follow-up: a user can build the generated crate with -Clinker-plugin-lto and CC=clang CFLAGS=-flto, which puts shims/fenv.c into rustc's LTO module. noinline alone is not enough there: for a plain shim clang infers memory(none) (checked with clang -O2 -emit-llvm), so LTO may move the call across fesetround or fold it via IPSCCP. fenv.c now marks every shim SLATE_FENV (**attribute**((noinline)), **declspec(noinline) for cl.exe), and under **clang\_\_ wraps the block in #pragma STDC FENV_ACCESS ON ... DEFAULT. clang then emits constrained ops (round.dynamic, fpexcept.strict) and marks the shims strictfp with memory(inaccessiblemem: readwrite). The DEFAULT reset keeps the pragma off the trampolines rendered after the block. gcc and cl.exe objects cannot join rustc's LTO, so they need only noinline; the pragma is guarded because gcc warns on it under -Wall. Test profiles were not rerun (C-only attribute and pragma change).
