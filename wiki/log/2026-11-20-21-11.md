# slate-8ge7.14 IR pointers: SLATE_IR_POINTERS on by default; FileChecks regenerated to slate-analysis lifts (28 gain, 24 lose, inventoried on slate-8ge7.12)

- kind: feature

Outcome: slate-analysis pointer facts drive lowering by default; the Rust-side pointer_lattice/length lifts run only with SLATE_IR_POINTERS=0, and every FileCheck block now shows the IR-side output.

- `lowerer::ir_pointers()` is on unless `SLATE_IR_POINTERS=0`. The `// SLATE-ENV: SLATE_IR_POINTERS=1` lines in the pointer_lift_* fixtures and fixtures.release/cross_unit_slice_lift were removed.
- 156 fixtures regenerated (fixtures/, fixtures.release/, fixtures.merge/). By parameter signature: 28 gain lifts, 24 lose at least one, 104 change only in bodies (`&local` instead of `unsafe { &(*addr_of_mut!(local)) }`, no `as *const` casts on lifted params, fewer `mut` locals).
- Losses are inventoried on slate-8ge7.12: inline asm operands (the largest group, 9 x86_64 asm fixtures), calls inside macro expansions (merge macro_boundaries/macro_fold/macro_places), nested-function captures, void pointees, returned params, ptr_lattice_escape_then_free, array_one_past_end_bound, complex_lvalue, the conservative join/alias oracles. `&str` (local_libc_strcmp, ptr_len_str_worklist) is slate-8ge7.13: the Rust-side lift read the NUL past the slice.
- calls.rs: argument hoisting next to a `&mut` borrow skips C-string literals (`c" a".as_ptr()`, through casts) as well as variables the borrowed arguments do not read, so `graph_at(c" a".as_ptr(), &mut position)` stays one call.
- slate-analysis bug found by the msvc profile: `addr_of(fn)` (C `&WinMain`) was not recorded as taking the function's address (only `FunctionDecay` was), so an address-taken function's params lifted and `Some(WinMain)` no longer coerced to the C fn-pointer type. Fixture: crates/slate/tests/fixtures/pointer_lift_address_of_function.c.

Validation: slate, merge, clang, gcc and msvc profiles pass.
