# Replace trigraphs in ISO modes before C23, with -trigraphs/-ftrigraphs overrides

_2026-10-10 15:28:46_

slate-parser-cxg.7.13.

Lexer::with_offset now runs translation phase 1 before phase 2: when StandardFeatures::trigraphs is set, each ??x trigraph becomes its character (keeping the three-byte source span via a new byte_ends vector), and only then are backslash-newline splices removed, so ??/ before a newline splices, including at the end of a // comment or a #define line.

Defaults, probed against gcc 16 and clang: on in ISO modes before C23 (c89/c94/c99/c11/c17, not c23/c2y), off in every gnu mode, off for msvc. -trigraphs/--trigraphs (gcc, clang) forces them on; clang also has -ftrigraphs/-fno-trigraphs, last wins. gcc rejects the -f spellings, so they are a clang-only Opt. The override is threaded as CompilerOptions::trigraphs into StandardFeatures::for_compiler.

Fixtures: {gcc,clang}/linux/x86_64/pp_trigraphs.c (string contents, sizeof of an escape, and a ??/ spliced #define across std and flag configurations; checked against gcc/clang object symbols), error/gcc -ftrigraphs and -fno-trigraphs. Migrated gcc.dg cpp/trigraphs.c as cpp__trigraphs.c (-std=c89). c11-trigraphs-1.c includes trigraphs.c so it is not migrated; Wtrigraphs-2.c still needs cxg.7.14 (whitespace after ??/).

slate-parser rejects -ansi (gcc_dg_sweep maps it to -std=c89, hiding the gap): filed as cxg.7.23. Wiki: preprocessor.md pipeline, compiler-flags.md.
