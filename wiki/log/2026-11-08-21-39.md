# gcc flavor: C23 va_start with arbitrary extra arguments

_2026-11-08 21:39:10_

slate-parser-cxg.18.4: gcc's C23 stdarg.h defines va_start(...) as __builtin_c23_va_start(__VA_ARGS__) and ignores everything after the va_list, which may be token soup such as va_start (ap, random ! ignored) or unbalanced va_start (ap, ]]]]]]{{{{{{). In the gcc flavor parse_input (parser/decl.rs) now runs ParserInput::drop_c23_va_start_extra_arguments (parser/input.rs), which removes the tokens from the first paren-depth-0 comma to the closing paren before parsing (remapping annotations with replace_tokens), so statement and brace scanners never see them. clang rejects such arguments and keeps parsing them. Migrated gcc.dg c23-stdarg-4/6/10; c23-stdarg-7/8 include other files and torture/ is a skipped dir, both now accepted. ir/builtins updated.
