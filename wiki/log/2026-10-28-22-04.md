# Support -mgeneral-regs-only in slate-parser

_2026-10-28 22:04:56_

slate-3f8g.2.10.1: -mgeneral-regs-only was rejected as unknown. Added Opt::GeneralRegsOnly (gcc, clang; msvc rejects). x86/x86_64 treat it as -mno-sse -mno-mmx -mno-80387 at its command-line position (X86IsaRequest::general_regs_only, drop_vector_registers in target/x86_isa.rs); gcc keeps POPCNT/CRC32 implied by an earlier explicit sse4.2/avx, a later -msse/-mavx re-enables. AArch64 drops fp, simd, crypto whatever the order (AArch64Isa::resolve). Arm32: gcc drops only the NEON macros, clang rejects (isa.rs). gcc soft float (_SOFT_FLOAT without SSE math) now also drops the IEC_559 macros (pp/math.rs). Tests/support forwards the flag from dg-options. Verified against clang 23.1.1 and gcc 16.2.1 with flag_probe.py and x86_isa_diff.py --gcc (351 cases, 0 mismatches). Fixtures: general_regs_only.c for clang/gcc x86_64+i686, clang/gcc aarch64, gcc armv7, error/clang armv7, error/msvc. Known gap, not from this change: clang turns '-mno-sse -msse' into the defaults, slate leaves SSE2 off. Interrupt lowering (slate-3f8g.2.10) not started.
