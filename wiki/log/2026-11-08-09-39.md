# Map MSVC compiler intrinsics to core::arch and slate_support

_2026-11-08 09:39:32_

slate-j3by.18: PuTTY plink/pscp/psftp for x86_64-pc-windows-msvc failed to link: __cpuid, __cpuidex, _addcarry_u64, _umul128 and 34 _mm* intrinsics were emitted as extern decls (MSVC headers only declare them; cl expands them).
- libc_catalog: MSVC _mm* map to core::arch::{x86,x86_64}. Table src/msvc_simd_table.rs is generated by crates/slate/tools/gen_msvc_simd_table.py from the toolchain's stdarch (SSE..AVX2, AES, PCLMUL, SHA, FMA, F16C; skips #[unstable] and &/&mut params): 851 shared + 19 x64-only names.
- shape(): MSVC __m128/__m128i/__m128d/__m256* typedefs (record unions) shape as core::arch types; support_argument and support-call returns transmute between the header union and the core::arch type. __size_t shapes as usize.
- slate_support: __cpuid, __cpuidex, _addcarry_u32/u64, _subborrow_u32/u64, _umul128 helpers (Rust's signatures differ: CpuidResult, &mut).
- Parser: msvc clang_builtin also consults the x86 target table, limited to ALL_MS_LANGUAGES entries. cl.exe accepts undeclared __cpuid (PuTTY aes-ni.c calls it without intrin.h, no C4013 at /W4); the call now gets the builtin prototype instead of implicit int(). Side effect: __stosb's count param is typed __size_t (clang-test inline-asm-intel fixtures regenerated).
- Slate binds intrin.h builtin declarations without system-header provenance through the catalog and skips the 'builtin without a lowering' barrier for them.
Fixtures: msvc/x86_simd_intrinsics.c, msvc/implicit_intrinsics.c (both failed first with unresolved symbols). msvc 476/476, slate 36/36, parser 9034/9034. plink, pscp, psftp now link and print the same -V output as native under wine.
