# Parse __m128-family names as ordinary typedefs

_2026-10-01 14:20:33_

slate-parser-v74.1.13: the parser turned __m128, __m128d, __m128i, __m256*, __m512* into TypeSpecifier::TargetBuiltin, which sema rejected as 'target builtin type'. Any header or user typedef with those names failed, including every SSE/AVX intrinsic from clang's and gcc's *intrin.h (zstd lib/compress, redis).

- No compiler predeclares them: clang, gcc and clang-cl all reject bare __m128. is_target_builtin_name now matches only __builtin_va_list.
- Combined with the x86 builtin registry (6x05.9), '#include <emmintrin.h>' + _mm_movemask_epi8 lowers end to end under --flavor=clang.
- Fixtures: removed clang/linux/x86_64/target-builtin-types.c (assumed predeclared names; _Imaginary already covered by ir_imaginary*.c); added error/clang/linux/x86_64/undeclared_x86_vector_types.c and clang/linux/x86_64/ir_x86_intrinsic_header.c.
- gcc-torture pr92618.c passes in both flavors (IR-ERROR dropped).
- Migrated gcc.dg Wstrict-aliasing-bogus-ref-all-2, pr32912-1/2/3, pr63914, pr94166 (gcc and clang) with gcc_dg_sweep.py over a scratch --root; the bead's other gcc.dg repros are in MIGRATE_SKIP_DIRS.
- Migrated clang-test CodeGen/X86/x86_32-arguments-win32.c (clang/windows/i686) via llvm_lit_sweep.py --filter.
