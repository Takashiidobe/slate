# NodeKindTag::ALL and ALL_EXPRS

_2026-10-18 18:19:28_

Prep for slate-o3gw.4. NodeKindTag is declared through node_kind_tags!, which also
emits NodeKindTag::ALL, so a new tag (c2y work) lands in ALL automatically.
NodeKindTag::carries_exprs is an exhaustive match with no wildcard, so a new tag
fails to compile until it is classified; ALL_EXPRS is filtered out of ALL at
compile time and COUNT is ALL.len().

peel_casts, constant_index_casts and cstr_literal now use ALL_EXPRS instead of a
hand-written KINDS list. cstr_literal's list was missing InlineAsm, so it now
also rewrites asm operands. literal_casts, bool_ternary and libc_call keep their
explicit subsets on purpose (literal_casts refuses LetIf/InlineAsm in apply).
No ALL_STMTS yet because no rule needs it. strum was considered and rejected:
VariantArray would only replace the macro, not the exhaustive classification.
