# List msvc conditional_global_initializers as unsupported on armv7

_2026-11-08 07:45:12_

slate-xw0x.8: msvc/conditional_global_initializers.c includes <stddef.h>, <stdlib.h>, <string.h>; armv7 has no msvc triple, so msvc fixtures translate for the linux triple with no msvc headers and fail with 'header not found'. Listed it in fixtures.unsupported/armv7-unknown-linux-gnueabihf.txt alongside flag_msvc_runtime_macros and the other header-dependent msvc fixtures. slate-arm green.
