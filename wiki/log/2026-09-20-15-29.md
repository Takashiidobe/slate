# Lower file-scope asm; drop pragmas in IR

_2026-09-20 15:29:11_

`resolve_module` aborted with `Unsupported("module declaration")` on every
`DeclKind::Asm` and `DeclKind::Pragma`, and `statements` did the same with
`Unsupported("module statement")` on `StmtKind::Pragma`. 20 sweep
configurations died on the first pragma in the file, whatever else they were
testing.

- `Module` gains `asm: Vec<Span<InlineAsm>>`, printed after the target block
  and before the types, reusing the statement asm printer. It is a separate
  list because file-scope asm declares no entity and belongs in no symbol
  table; keeping it out of `globals`/`functions` means nothing downstream
  filters it.
- Pragmas are dropped at both scopes. Nothing in sema consumes them today, so
  this matches existing behaviour rather than changing it — but it makes the
  gap visible: `#pragma pack` now lowers to an unpacked layout instead of
  erroring. Filed as slate-parser-dyd.27 (P1).
- `sema/names.rs` now binds file-scope asm operand expressions. GCC accepts
  operands there (constant constraints only), and the parser follows it under
  the gcc flavor, so lowering needed the bindings. Labels stay impossible.
- The `DeclKind` catch-all in `resolve_module` is gone; the match is
  exhaustive, so a new variant has to choose lower-or-drop.

Sweep: 2455/2678 -> 2485/2679 accepted, 29 configurations fixed, 0 regressed.
