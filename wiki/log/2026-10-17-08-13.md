# Lower \_Complex float/double and GNU integer complex types

_2026-10-17 08:13:00_

Complex values lower to the vendored num_complex::Complex<T> (repr(C), SysV-ABI compatible) instead of a slate support module.

- New lowerer/complex.rs: aggregate -> struct literal, RealToComplex/ComplexToReal/ComplexConvert, add/sub/mul/div (mixed scalar operands keep the scalar), neg, GNU ~ as conj, ==/!=, \_\_builtin_creal/cimag/conj.
- lower_type maps ir::Type::Complex of integer/f32/f64 components; ComplexPart places lower to .re/.im field access.
- Vendored num-complex gains c_ops.rs: c_mul/c_div(other, basic) with C Annex G NaN/inf recovery (**muldc3) and scaled division (**divdc3) for floats, wrapping integer versions; basic=true (CX_LIMITED_RANGE) uses plain formulas. Registered in main.rs write_num_complex_support.
- complex<f80> (long double) stays unsupported (slate-p58o.4.4).
- Promoted complex, complex_lvalue, stdlib_complex_cx_limited_range, 10 gcc-torture and 2 gcc-dg fixtures.
