# Survive long binary-operator chains

_2026-10-24 11:02:32_

slate-parser-cxg.27: gcc.dg/inline-24.c (~2160-term + chain) overflowed the debug stack, and release overflowed around 20k terms. Causes: Lowerer::lower_expr recursed per left operand (~200KB of debug frame per level); Hoister::value cloned the whole value tree just to read its span (now Span::erased); sequencing::walk and Hoister::value recurse over the left-deep IR tree. Fixes: binary_chain in sema/expression.rs lowers a left-associative Binary spine iteratively; sema::deep (stacker::maybe_grow, new dependency) guards Hoister::value and sequencing::walk. Probes: 20000-term chain passes in debug and release. Fixture: suites/gcc-dg inline-24.c. Other IR walkers (printer, crates/slate) still recurse and are unguarded.
