# Reject __ptr64 on 32-bit targets

_2026-09-27 10:19:53_

Follow-up to slate-parser-v74.1.3. __ptr64 was dropped unconditionally on the
assumption that every MS target is 64-bit. But --flavor=msvc enables MS
extensions on any target, including i686-unknown-linux-gnu and armv7. There,
clang (i686-pc-windows-msvc, or i686-linux -fms-extensions) makes
'int * __ptr64' a distinct 8-byte pointer (address space 272): sizeof 8,
_Alignof 8, and _Generic does not select int *. Slate gave 4/4 and int *, a
silent layout error.

parse_pointer_qualifiers now rejects __ptr64 when the target's pointer_width
is not 64 ('__ptr64 on a 32-bit target is not supported'). 64-bit targets
still drop it. In sizeof/cast type names the speculative type-name parse
swallows the message and the expression fallback fails with a generic parse
error. It is still rejected, but the wording was left alone.

slate-parser-v74.1.6 is widened to model __ptr64 on 32-bit targets alongside
__ptr32/__sptr/__uptr, replacing this error.
