# Fix _BitInt bit-field placement and aarch64 _BitInt layout (slate-6uvv.5)

_2026-10-17 10:13:46_

Two parser layout bugs found while checking bitint-17.c against clang and gcc.

1. Bit-field straddle rule (sema/types.rs, record layout). A bit-field moved to
   the next alignment boundary when it crossed a multiple of its type's size.
   clang and gcc use: move only when offset % natural_align + width > size.
   The two agree when size == alignment, so only these were wrong:
   - _BitInt(N>64) bit-fields on every target (bitint-17 S4: 56 -> 48 on x86_64).
   - long long bit-fields on i686 ({int; char; long long b:40} was 16, now 12).
2. _BitInt(N>64) scalar layout. ScalarLayouts now carries
   bit_precise_max_alignment (clang's BitIntMaxAlign): 8 by default, 4 on i686,
   16 on AArch64. It replaces the i64-alignment lookup. AArch64 _BitInt(65..)
   was 8-aligned and sized to 8 instead of 16; both clang and gcc use 16.

Verified with a scratch script comparing sizeof/alignof from slate-parser,
clang and gcc on x86_64, i686 and aarch64 (gcc only on aarch64 above 128 bits,
because clang caps aarch64 _BitInt at 128).

Fixtures:
- bitint_bitfield_layout.c: static_asserts generated from the oracles, under
  clang/linux/{x86_64,i686,aarch64} and gcc/linux/aarch64.
- gcc.dg bitint-17.c migrated into suites/gcc-dg/{gcc,clang}/linux/x86_64.

Wiki ir/types.md: added the bit-field placement rule and the per-target
_BitInt layout. A note on slate-j844 records the layout the lowering must match.
